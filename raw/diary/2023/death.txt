Title: death

when will my mind realize it was over before it even started. 

I keep repeating the loop, I keep falling for its tricks, im in a circus and I’ve been on the stage for far too long, but unaware how to leave.

Insanity, thats the perfect description. I’m Delusional, i make up scenarios and my mind starts to think they’re real. For most of my life, I’ve been living on imaginations, my thoughts are about thoughts, and everything around me is a new setting to think or day dream about.

A new person I meet, another I’m interested in, despite not sharing a single word with each other, not ever, my brain makes up scenarios, as if we actually met.

I fell for it again. Im going through another trick. It’s so hard to stop, however. Teenage hormones, i despise you so much. 

She probably doesnt even know I exist. Im just a mere … insignificant vessel to her, just an obstacle at most. When will these feelings go astray, when will they leave, I don’t know.

The sacrifices I make, all of that for nothing. Why even try, why even bother. Even if I do try, there’s obstacles blocking my way, predestined, my foundations, what makes me.. me. Appearance, things I can’t change or control. Hinderance, they are.

Besides, I dont deserve her.. not anyone for that matter. Im a leech. A mere sight of her only a second or a fraction of that is enough to make my day, even if everything else is anguish.

Once I get out of the K-12 bubble, what will be left of me? I don’t know. Im confused, no path or road. Im lost, I always am. If that’s the case, then the end is necessary. In any means to access it can suffice. Where there is nothing, no more pain and no more happiness, the distant void that is inevitable for everyone, I shall access that sanctuary, what the masses call.

Death.
