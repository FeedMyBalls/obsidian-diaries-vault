Title: everythings a waste

so.. were back.. in the gutter.

weve done so much but achieved so little, almost nothing. its almost miserable, but i guess this is how it’s going to be. it might look like im doing a lot but in the end none of it matters. ive enjoyed the journey so much but without securing any proof of my hardwork.

Call it bad luck, but it’s like ive done all this for nothing. all for nothing, just to get stepped on. used until im all empty. its impossible isnt it. happiness. I shouldnt even have the glimmer of thought or delusion that it might be achievable, because its not. Its not meant for me. I can never be happy. I’ll always be miserable, and its comforting this way. Maybe I’ll just learn to accept that this life I have lived is a story of tragedy, no happy journey nor a happy ending.

Everyone else will have their happy ending, but alas I’m here to suffer for as long as i live.
