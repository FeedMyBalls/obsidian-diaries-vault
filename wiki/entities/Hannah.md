# Hannah

The unspoken-to crush at Neo's May 2024 [[Sunnyslope-High-School]]
graduation. Username "infinite08.." (Neo declines to share the full
handle). Distinct from [[L]] and from [[Hat-In-Time-Crush]] per
Neo's confirmation. Single-entry appearance.

## What Neo writes about her

- **Instagram opener.** The night before graduation, Neo sent her
  an Instagram follow request captioned with a Filthy Frank
  "shoot me" clip overlaid with text *"1 day after graduation"* —
  a dark-humor implication that he'd be dead post-ceremony.
- **Adjacent in the line-up.** By coincidence she landed next to
  Neo during the graduation procession. They never spoke.
- **The glance pattern.** *"could feel that she was looking at me
  then looked away then again several times, although mixed with a
  feeling of unease probably because of my Instagram story and my
  attempt of trying to be her friend."*

## Architecture position

The Hannah case is the most behaviorally explicit deployment of the
[[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]] in the corpus — most other
cases have no overt opener at all. The Filthy Frank suicide-joke
Instagram request is the architecture's most aggressive opener
documented; it succeeded in producing an effect (her glances) but
not in producing contact.
