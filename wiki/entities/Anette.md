# Anette

Neo's college girlfriend. **January 6 – April 12, 2025.** The only
documented reciprocated romantic relationship in the corpus
post-[[G]]. Foundational entity — load-bearing for the
[[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]]'s "exception that confirms" case
and for the 2025-vs-2026 voice contradiction this page deliberately
holds.

## How they met

Late 2024. The Wizz / [[Relational-Combat]] arc. Neo had been
cycling through dating apps post-[[Vice-Principal-Incident]]. He met
someone "late this year" ([[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]]) —
Anette. [[Canyon]] (her ex) interfered; Neo blocked Anette
initially, she pursued reconnection, Neo broke from his
[[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]] pattern and engaged in the war passage:
*"I delivered the final blow. You lost."* The relationship started
five days later (Jan 6).

Anette was single when they got together — Canyon was prior — but
he was contesting territory in late 2024.

## The relationship

[[2025-05-21-College-Romantic-Pursuits]] documents the operational
shape:

> [!quote]
> "Had a relationship Jan 6th, she confessed, we made out a lot,
> kissed a lot, but eventually we broke up April 12th. Whatever.
> Got into drama before that with Joe. idk. i dont care."
> — [[2025-05-21-College-Romantic-Pursuits]]

[[Joe]] (34-year-old club president) was a third party romantically
interested in Anette during the relationship. Drama "before" the
April 12 breakup; the role and intensity of his involvement
unspecified in the entries reviewed.

The first kiss: parking lot, Neo's framing. Per
[[2026-03-16-She-Loves-Me-She-Loves-Me-Not]] — *"in a stupid
parking lot, just because that detail from his ex made me jealous."*
Motivated against [[Canyon]] (Anette's ex / first kiss) rather than
toward her.

The breakup: she said *"I think we should break up."* Neo answered
too quickly, missing the *think* hedge. The misread is the tragedy
Neo identifies in retrospect.

## What Neo writes about her as a person

- **Confessed first.** She made the first explicit move; Neo's
  response was to engage rather than block, breaking the
  [[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]] precedent.
- **Liked Neo's touch.** "She was the only one to make him like
  the feeling of touch" — [[2026-03-16-She-Loves-Me-She-Loves-Me-Not]].
- **Sensed his withdrawal first.** Neo retroactively reads her loss
  of interest as preceding his recognition of it; he amplified
  through "insanity," she initiated the formal end.
- **Skipped classes to be with him.** Mutual time investment
  documented.

The corpus does not name her interests, profession, or family. The
relationship's documentation is from inside Neo's experience, not
her portrait.

## Position in the architecture

[[Anette]] is the **exception that confirms** the
[[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]]. Reciprocity disabled the
engine: distance is what the architecture runs on, and Anette
provided proximity. The 2026 retrospective does not save the
relationship from architecture-failure; it complicates the failure
by naming what was real underneath the architecture-disengagement.

The post-breakup [[D]] reactivation in
[[2025-05-21-College-Romantic-Pursuits]] is the architecture
restarting on a familiar target — Anette's role had been the
falsifier; once gone, the architecture had no obstacle.
