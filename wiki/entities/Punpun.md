# Punpun

*Oyasumi Punpun* (おやすみプンプン) — manga by Inio Asano. Read by
Neo across April-May 2024 (4-5 days reading, per
[[2024-05-08-Desperation]]). One of the corpus's most-direct
identification cases — Neo registers Punpun's experience as
*relatable*, with reservations.

## What Neo writes about it

> [!quote]
> "I read おやすみプンプン for 4-5 days, and I'm unchanged. It is
> depressing, but it's just another mob of stuff to append in my
> cerebrum. I do like Punpun though. His journey, his experience,
> his feelings, it's something I relate with."
> — [[2024-05-08-Desperation]]

The reservation: Neo distinguishes himself from Punpun on the
"finally achieving his terrible relationship with Aiko" axis —
*"that is something I myself will never acquire, no matter how hard
I try."* The identification has a ceiling — Neo accepts the
suffering parallel, rejects the relationship parallel.

The final note in the entry — *"in the sex panels, I'd say we
share a similar.. structure.. or look.. but I won't expand on it"* —
the [[Body-Dysmorphia]] register intersecting with the
identification.

## Recurring reference

[[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]] frames Punpun as the start of the
[[Wizz]] arc — Neo "started reading Oyasumi Punpun, and halfway
through it, I started to get absorbed by Wizz." The reading ran
concurrent with the May 2024 [[Sen]] episode; the manga didn't
displace the dating-app desperation.

## Position in the corpus

Punpun is the corpus's clearest external-text identification. Other
cultural references (Schopenhauer, Kafka, Gatsby) operate as
register-borrowings; Punpun operates as mirror. The reservation Neo
maintains — that he won't have what Punpun has — is the part that
matters: the identification is with the suffering, not with the
trajectory toward connection.
