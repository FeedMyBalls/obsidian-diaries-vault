# Sen

[[Wizz]] contact, May 2024. The first online-only romantic contact
documented in the corpus. Single-episode case; canonical
[[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]] data point.

## What Neo writes about her

- Met via [[Wizz]] in May 2024; recommended by [[Edd]].
- Exchanged contact information off-platform; conversations
  continued past the app.
- Flirted "like a lot."
- Sent self-photos that disappointed Neo relative to her bio
  pictures.
- Texted Neo every day; Neo lost interest specifically because of
  the consistency. *"The moment a woman grows overly attached and
  talks to me everyday, I immediately lose interest"*
  ([[2024-05-08-Desperation]]).

The breakdown of [[Asexual-Atonement]] is documented through Sen —
Neo masturbated to her photos, explicitly noting he had not done
this for any prior crush. The contact was the first crack in the
2023 doctrine.
