# Dzani

The singular case in the corpus. **Bunni Dzani** is her username/handle
(Neo's reference); canonical entity name is Dzani per Neo's call.
The only person in the documented corpus whose interior Neo knew
*before* her exterior — accessed her trauma posts, account contents,
and personal information via the [[Hacking-Stalking-Pattern]] in
2022, then formed a real friendship with her summer 2025 without
disclosing the prior knowledge.

## The two-stage relationship

**Stage 1 — 2022 (one-sided knowledge).** Per
[[2026-04-XX-Rebellious-Teenager-Memories]]: Neo ran the
[[Hacking-Stalking-Pattern]] playbook against her. Found her
Instagram, linktree, Discord; read her trauma disclosures including
SA from when she was 13. She had no awareness any of this had
happened. *"i knew everything about her without her knowledge."*

**Stage 2 — Summer 2025 (real friendship).** They started talking;
*"it went so well we got so close."* The trauma disclosures she
shared with Neo in 2025 were things he had already read in 2022.
Neo registered the gap silently.

**Collapse — 2025-2026.** [[2026-04-XX-Rebellious-Teenager-Memories]]
documents the [[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]] mechanism extending to the
friendship: Neo was mean about her behind her back, was snitched on
by a third party, she blocked him.

## Additional 2026 context

[[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]] adds: *"Dzani noticing that I don't
share anything about myself, treated me well almost like her
boyfriend but that's a havoc I don't wanna get into, since in
reality, she actually did have one that she hid from everyone,
showing me more to her friends, ugh."*

The hidden boyfriend (folded into this page per Neo's call) is
referenced once. Dzani held the relationship in a register Neo
read as quasi-romantic; the reality was that she had a partner
whose existence she concealed. Neo's interpretation: she was
performing closeness rather than experiencing it. The combination
— Neo's covert prior knowledge + her covert partner — made the
2025 friendship structurally untenable in retrospect.

## Why Dzani is foundational

The Dzani case is empirically what the [[Hacking-Stalking-Pattern]]
is *for* — knowing-about without being-with. It is also the only
documented test of what happens when the pattern's target becomes
reachable in person. The result: the [[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]]
mechanism extended cleanly from romantic to friendship application;
the prior knowledge did not produce intimacy; the relationship
collapsed through the mechanism Neo runs everywhere else.

The case is filed as foundational rather than minimal because it's
the corpus's most-direct evidence about whether the
[[Hacking-Stalking-Pattern]] generates intimacy. The answer is no.
