# Schopenhauer

Arthur Schopenhauer (1788-1860). German philosopher. The only
philosopher Neo cites by name across multiple entries; the corpus's
register-affinity reference for pessimist-romantic-philosophical
prose.

## Documented invocations

- **[[2026-01-19-Deserve]]** — direct citation of the
  effort-equals-happiness formulation: *"in Schopenhauer's
  philosophy towards Happiness, he says that it lies in the effort
  one puts towards something, the difficulty."* Neo inverts it:
  the effort is evidence of his deformity rather than the path to
  happiness.
- **[[2025-12-08-7-Minutes]]** — register affinity, not direct
  citation. The cardiac-arrest meditation's idealist-time argument
  is Schopenhauer-shaped without naming him.
- **[[2025-05-12-Inaction-And-Regret]]** — oblique reference via
  the effort/value framing the entry argues with.

## Position in the corpus

Neo's relationship to Schopenhauer is closer to register-borrowing
than to philosophical engagement. Where another reader might
debate the will-as-suffering thesis, Neo treats the philosophical
position as a stylistic mode he can inhabit when his own writing
moves into the lyric-pessimist register.

The Schopenhauer-affinity is one of several philosophical
borrowings ([[Self-Mythologizing]] entries reference Kafka, Gatsby,
nihilism more broadly without committing to any). Schopenhauer
recurs more often than the others.
