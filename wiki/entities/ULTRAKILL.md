# ULTRAKILL

First-person shooter (Hakita / New Blood, early access 2020). The
recurring substitute / coping object across 2023-2026. One of the
corpus's longest-running cultural attachments — multi-year, multi-
register.

## What Neo writes about it

- **Doom Eternal-adjacent recommendation.** First flagged by a
  friend ("J") in [[2023-10-11-Completed-Pantheon-V-And-My-Youtube-Career]]
  as similar to Doom Eternal.
- **P-rank grinding.** Neo P-ranked P-2 (Pleasure Levels chapter
  challenge) "again as a yearly thing"
  ([[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]]) — a ritual, not a one-time
  achievement.
- **Cyber Grind modding.** [[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]]
  documents Neo modding ULTRAKILL to play custom music in Cyber
  Grind, importing his Spotify playlist via OnTheSpot. *"It felt
  like snorting cocaine digitally."*
- **The substitute.** [[2024-02-27-Recent]] frames ULTRAKILL as the
  Valentine's Day substitute — "while everyone in my Instagram
  stories were making out, I was just playing my favorite game
  which was way better :)." The smiley does the [[Aesthetic-of-Melancholy]]
  work.
- **The Iron-Side PC giveaway.** [[2024-02-27-Recent]] documents
  the ULTRAKILL "Iron Side" gaming-PC giveaway — 400k+ entries,
  Neo had 97 — as "the only thing that gives my life worth" during
  February 2024. The lottery long-tail framed as the only available
  upside vector.

## Why it's a substantive entity rather than minimal

ULTRAKILL recurs across every documented year. The relationship
Neo holds with the game is closer to ritual than to entertainment:
the P-rank-cycle, the modding, the *"my favorite game"* designation,
the Cyber Grind sessions documented in detail. The game functions
as a stable affective anchor across mood-floor periods that other
content/activities don't survive.
