# Lia

Neo's named "bestfriend" in 2026. The only person who reaches out
to Neo during the [[Intentional-Isolation]] period. Mid-substantial
in the corpus — present across the year's reflective entries
without becoming a portrait.

## What Neo writes about her

- **Names her bestfriend.** [[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]]: *"Lia
  thought otherwise, calling me a bestfriend."* The reciprocal
  framing — she calls him bestfriend — is what Neo registers. Neo
  doesn't claim the term himself; he reports her use of it.
- **Reaches out during isolation.** [[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]]:
  *"No one has really reached out to me, other than Lia, which is
  nice."* During the Feb 1 social-media deletion / [[Intentional-Isolation]]
  period, she remains the one peer connection that persists.
- **Neo may have ghosted her.** Same entry: *"Although I wished for
  space in general, so I might've ghosted her. I'll talk to her or
  whoever when I feel like it."* The friendship is held at
  distance — Lia maintains it; Neo lets her.

## Position in the corpus

Lia is the corpus's most positive named friendship in 2026. She
sits in interesting tension with [[Dzani]] — both substantial 2025-2026
friend-figures, but in opposite registers: Dzani is the case Neo's
[[Hacking-Stalking-Pattern]] preceded and [[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]]
ended; Lia is the case where neither pattern is documented as
having activated. The friendship persists in part because Neo
hasn't deployed his usual mechanisms against it.
