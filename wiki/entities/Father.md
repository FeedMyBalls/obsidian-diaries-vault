# Father

Neo's father. Documented in the corpus primarily through
[[Father-Wound]]'s standing position: Neo refuses paternity
cognitively (*"He's not my dad"*) while acknowledging biological
fact. This page records what Neo writes about him as a person, not
who he is independent of Neo's writing.

## In the corpus

Specific characterizations:

- **Refusal-of-paternity register.** [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]]:
  *"my piece of shit retarded 'dad' i hate calling him my dad by
  the way. I don't call him that. I never do. I mentally dont. He's
  not my dad. Fuck biology."* The doctrinal disowning is named
  consciously. Neo distinguishes mental disowning from biological
  fact.
- **Active hostility (Neo's read).** [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]]
  documents his pattern of scolding Neo — blaming gaming for missed
  schedules even when Neo had not gamed for weeks. Neo registers
  this as ongoing antagonism, not occasional friction.
- **Genetic inheritance.** [[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]]:
  *"especially my biological father, he's already such a short
  fuck, yet he still has such a short fuse."* The combination — bequeathed
  defects + active hostility — is named explicitly.
- **Origin grievance.** [[2023-12-13-December-31st-Suicide-Day]]'s
  framing of his parents as "an accident" extends to him. The
  generational comparison Neo draws in [[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]]:
  *"in my age, they had the support of their parents and grandparents
  in college, getting drunk and having sex. me, no. im working."*

## The independence project

Where the [[Mother-Wound]] expresses through rage, the
[[Father-Wound]] expresses through independence-as-spite. Neo
paying his own bills (and theirs), executing the [[Long-Term-Plan]]
without parental scaffolding, and refusing to call him "dad" all
share the same refusal-shape. The disowning is operational, not just
verbal — the [[Long-Term-Plan]]'s autonomy from family financial
support is partly motivated by this refusal.

## Position in Neo's family system

Lower-affect than [[Mother]] in the writing — the father's
disposition toward Neo had already collapsed by the time the corpus
opens, so the entries lack the dated-collapse-moment that the
mother's relationship has. He is named as antagonist when present
and ignored otherwise.
