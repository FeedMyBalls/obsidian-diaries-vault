# Vannevar Bush

American engineer and science administrator (1890–1974). Best known in
this wiki for the 1945 *Atlantic* essay **"As We May Think,"** which
introduced the [[Memex]] concept — a personal, curated, associative-trail
knowledge store. *(inference: dates from general knowledge, not the
source.)*

In the framing of [[LLM-Wiki-Pattern]], Bush identified the right shape
of personal knowledge management — private, curated, link-driven — but
couldn't solve the maintenance problem. Eight decades later, LLMs supply
the missing labor.
