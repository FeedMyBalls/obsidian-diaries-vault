# Canyon

[[Anette]]'s ex / first kiss. The third party in the late-2024
[[Relational-Combat]] passage. Distinct from [[Joe]], who entered
during Neo's relationship with Anette (Canyon precedes it).

## What Neo writes about him

- **Anette's first kiss.** This detail is the parking-lot-kiss
  catalyst — Neo learned it during the relationship and rushed his
  own first kiss with Anette in a parking lot specifically as a
  jealous response. Per [[2026-03-16-She-Loves-Me-She-Loves-Me-Not]]:
  *"in a stupid parking lot, just because that detail from his ex
  made me jealous, i regret every second of it."*
- **Was in Neo's class a semester before Anette dated Neo.** Per the
  same entry — Neo discovered this when looking up Anette's prior
  partner.
- **Late-2024 antagonist.** [[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]]
  documents the war passage where the unnamed "he" ("he spreads
  the message") is Canyon (per Neo's confirmation). Anette was
  formally single at the time but Canyon was contesting territory.

## Position in the corpus

Canyon's role is doubly catalytic:

1. **Pre-relationship antagonist.** The Relational-Combat-pattern's
   canonical case (cf. [[Relational-Combat]] entry).
2. **Within-relationship catalyst.** The parking-lot kiss is
   pre-loaded with jealousy of him before it happens. The
   [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]]'s typical absence-of-real-rival
   doesn't apply here — Canyon is the rival the architecture
   organizes against.

He is named only twice in the corpus: in the late-2024 war passage
and in the 2026 retrospective on the parking-lot kiss. No portrait
beyond role.
