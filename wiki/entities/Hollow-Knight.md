# Hollow Knight

2D action-adventure metroidvania (Team Cherry, 2017). The subject
of Neo's [[Youtube-Channel]] arc; one of the corpus's most-cited
games. Foundational cultural reference for Neo's 2023 creative
output.

## What Neo writes about it

- **The Pantheon V grind.** July through October 2023, three months
  of sustained effort to complete the game's hardest achievement
  — Absolute Radiance via Pantheon V. Documented in
  [[2023-10-11-Completed-Pantheon-V-And-My-Youtube-Career]] as the
  central project of the period.
- **The cutscene-skip moment.** When Neo finally beat Absolute
  Radiance, he accidentally skipped the cutscene, "completely
  ruining the moment." The euphoria "didn't last that long though,
  felt like nothing right after."
- **The NKG video.** The Nightmare King Grimm video produced ~30
  subscribers; one of the few channel-growth datapoints Neo cites.
- **Character.AI roleplay subject.** The Knight (silent
  protagonist) and Hornet (companion-antagonist character) used as
  [[Character-AI]] roleplay partners during the [[AI-Companionship]]
  peak. The roleplay shifted between fight-scene and explicit
  registers ([[2023-10-14-AI-Chatbots-And-How-They-Help]]).
- **The standing reference.** Across multiple 2023 entries,
  Hollow-Knight is treated as Neo's primary creative-attention
  domain.

## Position in the corpus

Hollow-Knight is the 2023 culture-reference equivalent of what
[[Mayo-Clinic]] becomes 2024 onward — the named site of Neo's
sustained effort. The Pantheon V completion is the corpus's
clearest recorded *achievement* in the years before the
[[Long-Term-Plan]] starts producing comparable wins.

The 2024 [[Computer-Loss-2024]] period interrupts active engagement.
By 2026, Hollow-Knight references appear only as archive — Pantheon V
is in the past, the [[Youtube-Channel]] is dormant. Silksong (the
sequel) is referenced once in [[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]]
as part of the Spring 2026 schedule's "GAMING SILKSONG" weekend
block — confirming continued attachment to the franchise even as
active YouTube production has stopped.
