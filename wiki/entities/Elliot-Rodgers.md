# Elliot Rodgers

Perpetrator of the 2014 Isla Vista killings. A figure in incel-
adjacent online communities; cited as a touchstone in
[[Blackpill-Vocabulary]] discourse.

In the corpus, Elliot Rodgers appears once, indirectly — via
[[Alec]]'s reference to him during a hangout. Per [[2024-04-25-Copium]]:

> [!quote]
> "he did talk about Elliot Rodgers, and honestly it made me think
> that he probably had the perception of himself as an incel, even
> though that's far from the truth."
> — [[2024-04-25-Copium]]

Neo's relationship to the reference is diagnostic-of-Alec, not
self-identifying. Neo did not invoke Rodgers in his own register
when documenting his own [[Blackpill]] commitments — he names
[[Halo-Effect]] and looks-determinism more often than perpetrator-
case-studies. The Rodgers reference here is filed because [[Alec]]
named him; Neo's interest is in what the naming reveals about Alec.
