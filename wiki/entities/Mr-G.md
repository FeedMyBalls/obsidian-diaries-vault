# Mr-G

Sophomore-year English teacher (pre-[[Sunnyslope-High-School]];
Neo's prior school). Appears once, embedded in Neo's second-person
address to [[L]] in [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]]:

> [!quote]
> "you have the same teacher as our english teacher back then when
> you were in sophomore year, remember? haha. Good for you. I
> wouldve loved that. Mr G. sucked, how much i was a sophomore
> instead and graduated with you and saw your smile."
> — [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]]

Neo's affect toward Mr-G is mixed-negative ("sucked") but the
function in the entry is associative — Mr-G connects Neo to [[L]]'s
later school year as a shared reference point. The teacher matters
because [[L]] would have him; not because Neo has views about him.
