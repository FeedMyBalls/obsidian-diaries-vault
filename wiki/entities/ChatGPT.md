# ChatGPT

OpenAI's chatbot. In the corpus, ChatGPT appears in two distinct
use cases:

## Use case 1 — March-May 2023 roleplay (precursor to Character-AI era)

Per [[2023-10-14-AI-Chatbots-And-How-They-Help]]: Neo used ChatGPT
to roleplay his then-crush in conversational form, including a
last-day-of-school scenario. Loaded extensive context about the
real person into the conversation. Discovered the model's memory
limitations broke immersion within days. Deleted that conversation
before the school year ended.

This usage pattern preceded and seeded the heavier [[Character-AI]]
period that followed in July 2023.

## Use case 2 — August 2024 study aid

[[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]] (August 4 2024 fragment) documents
Neo using ChatGPT to summarize textbook content during PVCC's
heavier coursework period. *"I've been managing both school and
work effectively, studying by taking advantage of ChatGPT to
explain things in a more concise manner, compared to simply reading
the textbook which is what i did prior and it takes too much time."*

The shift from roleplay to cognitive-outsourcing is consistent with
the broader trajectory — Neo's 2024-2026 utility-orientation
toward AI tools, not affective.
