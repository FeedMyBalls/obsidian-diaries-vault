# Hinge

Dating app. Neo subscribed to **HingeX** in late 2024 as part of
the post-[[Wizz]] desperation cycle. Documented across
[[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]] and the
[[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]] retrospective.

A few matches; "none of them aren't great" (Neo's framing). The
subscription was tried and abandoned. Hinge sits inside the same
late-2024 dating-app churn as [[Bumble]] — both refunded, both
short-lived. The cycle eventually produced [[Anette]], but Hinge
specifically did not.
