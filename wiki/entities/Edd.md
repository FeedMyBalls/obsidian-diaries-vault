# Edd

[[Sunnyslope-High-School]] AP Computer Science classmate, senior
year. Recurring across multiple 2023-2024 entries. Sometimes spelled
"Eddy" by Neo; per Neo's call these are the same person, canonical
name **Edd**.

## What Neo writes about him

- **AP CS classmate.** [[2023-10-17-Art-Club-And-New-Classes]]
  documents his Art Club presence and selfie-on-Neo's-phone moment.
- **The "speak to skye" recurring suggestion.** [[2023-11-18-Meaningless]]:
  *"every time. when i talk anything remotely related to relationships
  to him, he just says 'speak to skye'. no. no i wont edd."* Persistent
  attempt to set Neo up with [[Skye]].
- **Wizz recommender.** [[2024-05-08-Desperation]] credits Edd with
  recommending [[Wizz]]. The introduction that opens the
  [[Wizz]]-era / [[Sen]] / Asexual-Atonement-breakdown chain.
- **Cuts class with Alec** in [[2024-04-25-Copium]]. Friend-group
  overlap with [[Alec]].

## Position in the corpus

Edd is the most operationally-consequential friend in the corpus —
not because the friendship is intense, but because Edd's recommendation
of Wizz is the on-ramp to a chain of events ([[Sen]], [[Asexual-Atonement]]
breakdown, the late-2024 [[Anette]] arc). Neo would not have
arrived at [[Anette]] via that route without Edd.
