# Wizz

Mobile dating-adjacent app marketed as a "find friends" platform but
operating de facto as a teenage dating app. Recommended to Neo by
[[Edd]]; introduced via [[2024-05-08-Desperation]]. The on-ramp to
the entire late-2024 / early-2025 chain — [[Sen]], the
[[Pre-Calc-Wizz-Girl]] confession-and-block episode, [[Bumble]]
and [[Hinge]] failures, eventually [[Anette]].

## What Neo writes about it

- **Marketed as friends, used as dating.** *"main priority is to
  just get teenagers to date with each other"*
  ([[2024-05-08-Desperation]]).
- **Neo set his preference age range to 16-17** in May 2024 to
  match his pre-18 maturity self-assessment.
- **Addiction shape.** *"You see thousands to millions of faces
  every hour, and every ticking hour of your life, you're wasting
  it bit by bit."*
- **Wizz Gold subscription tried** ([[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]] —
  $3 paid; Neo registered the price as trivial relative to trading
  losses).
- **Multiple deletion-and-reinstall cycles.** May 2024 (Sen
  episode), August 2024 (relapse documented in
  [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]]), late 2024 deletion before [[Anette]]
  arrived.
- **The "everywhere is the same" recognition.** Neo registered that
  the people he was talking to were doing what he was doing —
  scrolling for more contacts while engaged in conversation. The
  insight precipitated the Sen-block move.

## Position in the corpus

Wizz is structurally consequential without ever feeling consequential
to Neo at the time. The relationships and breakdowns it produced are
rated as failures in real-time and then become the chain that ends
in [[Anette]]. The [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]] does not
operate well on Wizz because the platform produces too-rapid
reciprocity — confessions inside days. Neo's [[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]]
mechanism gets repeatedly activated and the architecture cycles
through targets faster than it can stabilize.

By 2026 Wizz is gone — the [[Intentional-Isolation]] strategy
includes deletion of all such platforms.
