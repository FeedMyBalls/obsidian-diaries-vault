# PVCC

Paradise Valley Community College (Phoenix area). Neo's first
post-high-school institution. **Attended 2024-2025**, completing
AS Biology + pre-medical prerequisites per the [[Long-Term-Plan]].

## What Neo writes about it

- **Confirmed in plan from senior year.** [[2023-10-17-Art-Club-And-New-Classes]]
  notes Neo's intention to attend community college rather than
  four-year. The class-drop decision (Honors Physics) was justified
  in part by the PVCC route.
- **First month documented.** [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]] is one
  month into PVCC; the "club meeting in a few minutes. game dev club"
  closing line is a PVCC moment.
- **Graduated/transferred 2025-2026.** Per
  [[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]] Neo is now at [[ASU-West]];
  PVCC is past tense. The corpus does not document the PVCC
  graduation event explicitly.
- **The autistic kid from graduation went there too.**
  [[2026-03-17-High-School-Graduation]] notes the unnamed peer who
  approached Neo at graduation also attended PVCC subsequently.

## Position in the corpus

PVCC is structurally important but documentationally thin — Neo
rarely writes about specific PVCC classes, peers, or events. Its
function is as a stage in the [[Long-Term-Plan]] timeline, not as
a felt institutional context. The contrast with [[Sunnyslope-High-School]]
(rich documentation) and [[Mayo-Clinic]] (rich documentation) is
notable; PVCC was the academic stage of life that ran in parallel
with the Mayo Clinic work and didn't leave its own register.
