# Mayo Clinic

Phoenix-area campus of the Mayo Clinic medical system. Neo's
employer from **July 22, 2024 onward**. The institutional vehicle
for the [[Long-Term-Plan]]'s execution. Substantive entity — the
single most-cited workplace in the corpus and the operational site
of [[Workaholism-as-Identity]]'s mature form.

## What Neo writes about it

### Roles, in sequence

- **2024-2025: EVS / Campus Facilities.** Environmental Services /
  terminal-cleaning role. Origin documented in
  [[2024-07-22-First-Day-Of-Work]]: orientation, group of older
  women coworkers, "calm before the storm" framing.
- **2025-Present: Anesthesia Tech.** Step up from EVS. Per
  [[Long-Term-Plan]] timeline, this is the current position
  (April 2026). $21/hour + night differential ≈ $3.2k/month.
  Documented in [[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]].
- **Mid/Late BSN: ICU PCA + Anesthesia Tech (planned).**
- **Post-BSN: ICU Nurse (planned).**

### The night shift

[[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]] documents the schedule:
8:00pm – 4:30am Monday/Wednesday; 9:00pm – 5:30am Tuesday/Thursday/
Friday. Five nights / week. Stocking the Anesthesia Cart, OR rooms,
equipment room. [[Dave]] as the partner-in-shift.

### The [[System-Gaming]] site

[[2026-04-XX-Rebellious-Teenager-Memories]] documents the
supervisor-procedure workaround: Neo persuaded Dave to test the
prescribed stocking order's failure case; Dave reported the failure;
the procedure was relaxed; the supervisor was fired one week before
the entry. Neo registers this as luck.

## Position in the corpus

Mayo Clinic is the operational anchor of the post-2024 self. Where
the 2023-mid-2024 corpus runs on [[Sunnyslope-High-School]], school
classes, and crisis registers, the post-July-2024 corpus runs on
Mayo Clinic, work shifts, and a slowly-emerging
[[Workaholism-as-Identity]]. The institution's role in stabilizing
Neo's life is structural — without the schedule and the income,
the [[Long-Term-Plan]] loses its execution path.
