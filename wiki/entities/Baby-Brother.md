# Baby-Brother

Neo's younger brother. Functionally important in the corpus as **the
speed limiter on Neo's rage toward [[Mother]]** — Neo's love for the
brother bounds what Neo will say or do in the household even
mid-rage. Also a daily-care obligation Neo takes on (afternoons,
weekends) and a recurring affectionate reference in 2026 entries.

## What Neo writes about him

- **The rage-limit line.** [[2024-02-28-Anger]]: *"Youre lucky you
  have a child, so even I can't shout at you to call you a stupid
  fucking bitch."* The brother's presence is what stops the rage
  from leaving Neo's interior.
- **Babysitting daily.** [[2024-04-25-Copium]] and especially
  [[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]] document the sustained
  caretaking pattern — feeds, changes, watches, naps with him.
  Treated as obligation Neo assumes without complaint, even when it
  costs personal time.
- **Affectionate framing.** [[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]]:
  *"i love my baby brother and my soon baby sister."* One of very
  few unambiguously-affectionate disclosures in the corpus.

## Baby-Sister (related)

Neo references "my soon baby sister" in
[[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]] — pregnancy or recent arrival,
context unclear. Filed here rather than as a separate entity per
Neo's call. Single mention; same affectionate framing.

## Position in Neo's family system

The only family member Neo names with affection. The contrast with
[[Mother]] and [[Father]] is structural: Neo's hostility toward
the parents is held in tension with his uncomplicated love for the
brother (and soon sister). The household is not uniformly
antagonistic for Neo — it is targeted.
