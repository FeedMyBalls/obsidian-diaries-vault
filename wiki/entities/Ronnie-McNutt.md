# Ronnie McNutt

Reference figure cited as a method-and-audience model in two
documented points of the corpus's [[Method-Research]] arc. This page
records the role he plays in Neo's writing as cited model — not a
biographical entry. Per Neo's standing decision on method specifics,
no operational detail is reproduced here.

## Documented invocations

- **[[2023-10-10-Whats-My-Goal-Now]]** — Neo references his earlier
  drawing of "ronnie mcnutt blowing his head off" as the
  [[Suicidal-Drawing-Incident]] that triggered his first
  [[Social-Worker]] referral. The drawing was based on the McNutt
  reference; the specific imagery is what staff identified as
  alarming.
- **[[2024-01-02-Ill-Attempt-Suicide-Again]]** — Neo invokes the
  McNutt model for both method and *audience*. *"I'll 'an hero'
  (record suicide) and let the masses watch, at least my death
  won't go in vain, and some will enjoy my last moments."* The
  reference is operational, not abstract — Neo names it as a
  template he's considering replicating.

## Position in the corpus

McNutt-as-cited-model is part of the [[Method-Research]] meta-
pattern's audience component — the move where Neo registers death
as a public event, not a private one. The McNutt reference
specifically supplies the public-suicide model the
[[2024-01-02-Ill-Attempt-Suicide-Again]] entry's call-for-firearm-
buyers logic depends on.

The reference does not appear post-2024. By 2025-2026 the
[[Method-Research]] pattern is dormant, and McNutt with it.
