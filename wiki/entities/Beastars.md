# Beastars

Manga (Paru Itagaki, 2016-2020) and anime adaptation. Functions in
the corpus as a connector — the show that bridges multiple
crush-figures.

## What Neo writes about it

- **[[G]]'s 2019 recommendation.** [[2023-07-04-Comfort-And-Past-Diaries]]
  references G recommending Beastars in 2019. Neo watched the OP
  on Netflix at the time, registered the visual quality, didn't
  pursue further then.
- **[[D]]'s connection.** [[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]]
  documents D approaching Neo about Beastars in art class —
  D had read the manga in Japanese (untranslated). The Beastars
  recognition was the first conversation D initiated with him.
- **2023 active engagement.** Neo watched Season 2 during fall 2023
  via RealDebrid.

The show operates in the corpus as a thread that connects [[G]]
and [[D]] via Neo. Both crush-figures' Beastars-adjacency suggests
Neo finds the show signaling something he's drawn to in romantic
partners — possibly the furry-adjacent / animal-fable register, or
simply that Beastars-readership correlated with the "interesting"
girls of his school years.
