# D

The high-school-era crush whose architecture went furthest toward
contact without crossing into reciprocity. The single-letter
anonymization is Neo's. Distinct from [[L]] and the
[[Hat-In-Time-Crush]].

## What Neo writes about her

- **Native (likely Navajo per surname).** [[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]].
- **High-pitched voice.** Others in class disliked it; Neo
  registered it as one of her distinguishing features and "almost
  liked" it.
- **Furry-adjacent interests.** Read [[Beastars]] manga in original
  Japanese; uses Ibis Paint X. Approached Neo about Beastars in
  art class — *the first time a girl had approached him*
  ([[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]]).
- **[[RTC]] history.** Disappeared from school for months during
  junior year for a residential treatment center stay. Returned
  the following school year.
- **Shares the [[Viking-Center]].** Senior-year mornings and
  lunches Neo and D occupied the same space, glanced at each other,
  never spoke. The geometry is documented in
  [[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]] in detail.
- **Drew a dog painting** in the art gallery; Neo notes seeing it
  positively.
- **Year younger; recent graduate.** Per
  [[2025-05-21-College-Romantic-Pursuits]], graduated 2025 alongside
  [[L]].

## Architecture position

D's case was the closest a [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]] run
came to reciprocity in the high-school years. She approached him.
She was the unnamed staring-girl in
[[2023-10-17-Art-Club-And-New-Classes]]'s closing line. Neo
self-imposed the no-approach rule to "give her peace" after his
prior failed attempt with [[Hat-In-Time-Crush]] —
[[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]] formalizes the rule
and applies it to D's case.

The closing of the 2023 D entry contains the hostage clause: *"If
she still feels weirded out in any way. Then.. I'll just kill
myself."* The architecture's stakes externalized to her experience
of him.

## The 2025 reactivation

[[2025-05-21-College-Romantic-Pursuits]] documents the post-Anette
pivot. D had reactivated her Instagram around the time of her
graduation. Neo followed; she followed back. The architecture
re-engaged within the same paragraph that dismissed
[[Anette]]'s reciprocated relationship as having "no spark."
Whether the re-engagement produced any actual contact is not
documented in entries up through April 2026.
