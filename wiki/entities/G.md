# G

Neo's sophomore-year girlfriend. The corpus's only **pre-architecture
relationship** — predating the [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]]'s
mature form. Single-letter anonymization is Neo's. Distinct from all
other crushes; the only one Neo dated in the high-school years.

## What Neo writes about her

- **Sophomore-year prom dance, then dating.** The recovery arc
  from [[GC-Scandal-2021]] partly threads through this relationship —
  by prom, "things were starting to heal," and Neo had begun dating
  her ([[2023-06-10-Im-A-Creep-No-Seriously]]).
- **Recommended Beastars in 2019.** Watched the OP back then,
  registered the show as visually striking.
- **The Expo first date.** [[2023-07-04-Comfort-And-Past-Diaries]]
  recovers a 2023 dream Neo had about her — a Kirby-guessing date,
  her head on his shoulder, location reminiscent of Expo (their
  actual first date).
- **Texted goodbye / breakup.** The relationship ended; Neo's
  characterization is *"a total failure but I'll accept it."* No
  detailed post-mortem in the corpus.
- **The April 2023 letter fragments.** In
  [[2023-07-04-Comfort-And-Past-Diaries]] Neo recovers two unsent
  letters from April 2023 addressed to her — confessional, hopeful,
  pleading. Annotated by July-2023 Neo with sardonic asides ("yeah
  too fucking bad neo.. she never said a single word").

## Position in the corpus

G matters structurally because she is the proof that
[[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]] is not a permanent disposition
but the form Neo's relating took *after* the GC era. With G, before
the architecture had hardened, Neo dated. He has not dated again
in the same way since — [[Anette]] was online-met via
[[Wizz]]/post-[[Relational-Combat]]; [[Sen]] was Wizz; the rest are
unspoken.

The G case is also where Neo's [[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]] mechanism
was already evident in proto-form — the relationship "ended" with
characteristic ambiguity in the corpus, and Neo's recovered April
2023 letters show the architecture's seeds (writing letters not
sent; performing affection in absence rather than presence).
