# Frieren

*Frieren: Beyond Journey's End* (anime, 2023-). Watched by Neo
during the documented Friday-night-alone period in
[[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]]. *"The anime I was
watching was nice, stopped at episode 4 and about to watch ep 5
later or never. Makes me feel nice about being short, since I feel
like a cute short loli girl lol."*

The identification — short-stature-via-anime-protagonist — is one
of the corpus's softer self-references about [[Body-Dysmorphia]]
during a period otherwise dominated by harsher self-assessment.
