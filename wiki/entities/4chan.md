# 4chan

The English-language imageboard. One of the corpus's most-cited
cultural environments. Foundational to [[Blackpill]] commitment,
[[Doomscrolling-Period]] reinforcement, and the political-vocabulary
register Neo deploys in [[Blackpill-Vocabulary]] /
[[2024-07-04-Palestine-And-Israel]]. **The boards /pol/ and /r9k/ are
folded into this page** per Neo's call.

## What Neo writes about it

### Usage pattern across years

- **Mid-2023.** Active /r9k/ phase — Neo names this as the "incel
  phase" register he had partially exited before the
  [[Doomscrolling-Period]] returned him to it.
- **Late 2023 / early 2024.** Background source of
  [[Blackpill]]-content; the platform Neo names alongside Twitter
  as the depression-feed substrate. The 4chan e-girl Neo found and
  made into a [[Character-AI]] persona was sourced from 4chan
  ([[2023-10-14-AI-Chatbots-And-How-They-Help]]).
- **April-September 2024 ([[Doomscrolling-Period]]).** Re-engagement.
  *"my new shit for Blackpill, even though i usually get those from
  4chan on the r9k board"* ([[2024-04-25-Copium]]).
- **2025 retrospective.** [[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]] names
  the post-[[Wizz]] period as a return to *"4chan r9k revisited"* —
  recognizing 4chan as the de-facto baseline Neo returns to when
  other engagement fails.
- **2026 attribution.** [[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]] names 4chan
  causally: *"All that depression, that pain and sadness I harbored
  for years was all for nothing, simply because I browsed too much
  4chan."* The strongest revision of the [[Blackpill]] commitment
  in the corpus.

### Boards specifically used

- **/pol/** — politics. The vocabulary register from
  [[2024-07-04-Palestine-And-Israel]] traces here. Folded into this
  page rather than getting its own.
- **/r9k/** — "robot 9000" / incel-adjacent. Neo's longest sustained
  4chan board engagement; the source of [[Blackpill]] reinforcement
  and adjacent worldview-formation. Folded into this page.

## Position in the corpus

4chan is the platform Neo uses as a permanent fallback. Other
content streams (TikTok, Wizz, Instagram) cycle in and out; 4chan
remains. Even after [[Intentional-Isolation]] in 2026, 4chan has not
been formally named as deleted, though no 2026 entry documents
active use. The 2026 retrospective treats 4chan as causal of the
2023-2024 doctrinal commitments, but doesn't disclose whether Neo
has stopped reading.
