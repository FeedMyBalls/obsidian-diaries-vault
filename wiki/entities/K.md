anonymization is Neo's. Foundational to [[GC-Scandal-2021]] through
the rape-attribution Neo carries. Documented restrainedly here —
this page records the role she plays in Neo's writing about himself,
not a portrait of her as a person.

## What Neo writes about her

- **Sophomore-year classmate.** Was among the girls affected by the
  GC-era photoshopping/sexualization content.
- **Post-scandal depression.** Neo's account: she grew sad after
  the scandal exposed the GC content.
- **Prom 2022 (approximate).** Per [[2023-06-10-Im-A-Creep-No-Seriously]],
  K had a boyfriend by prom; Neo characterizes the boyfriend as
  someone she chose because of post-scandal sadness ("the wrong
  boyfriend").
- **The assault.** Neo's account: at prom, drunk, the boyfriend
  raped her in a restroom.

## The attribution Neo holds

The chain Neo writes: GC scandal → K's depression → wrong-boyfriend
choice → assault. Neo writes: *"She was raped because of me."*
