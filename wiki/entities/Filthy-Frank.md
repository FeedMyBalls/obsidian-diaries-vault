# Filthy Frank

Internet persona / character (George Miller, active 2011-2017). The
specific clip Neo deployed — the "shoot me" exchange with h3h3 — is
a dark-humor staple of late-2010s YouTube culture.

In the corpus, Filthy Frank appears once: as the meme Neo overlaid
on a pre-graduation Instagram follow request to [[Hannah]] with
the caption *"1 day after graduation"* — implying his death
post-ceremony.

The opener is the most behaviorally-explicit deployment of
[[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]]'s standard pattern documented
in the corpus. Neo's framing: *"classic dark humor."*
