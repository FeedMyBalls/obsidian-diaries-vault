# Viking Center

[[Sunnyslope-High-School]]'s student center / common space. Neo's
habitual on-campus location across senior year. The site of the
[[D]] glance-pattern that runs through fall 2023.

## What Neo writes about it

- **Morning sleep spot.** Neo arrived at school at 6am during
  depressive periods of senior year and slept in the Viking Center
  for an hour or two before classes. *"I'd go to the Viking Center
  and just sleep there for an hour or two or just listen to music.
  Did that for months."* ([[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]])
- **Lunch space.** Neo registered the Viking Center as his lunch
  spot during senior year after the prior lunch arrangement *"sucked."*
- **6th-period work space.** [[2023-10-17-Art-Club-And-New-Classes]]
  notes the personal-development online class run from there.
- **The [[D]] geometry.** Neo and D both used the space for morning
  arrivals and lunches. The repeated glance-then-look-away
  exchanges happened here. The space is canonical for the [[D]] /
  [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]] case.

The Viking Center is named with affectionate possessiveness —
*"I've always belonged in the Viking Center, it was my place."*
The space's role exceeded utility; it functioned as Neo's chosen
on-campus solitude / stand-by-D zone.
