# Sunnyslope High School

Phoenix-area high school. Neo's school for **junior and senior years
(2022-2024)** after transferring from his prior school in the
aftermath of [[GC-Scandal-2021]]. Graduated May 2024. Substantive
because a large portion of the documented corpus's events and
relationships happen here.

## What Neo writes about it

- **The transfer school.** [[2023-06-10-Im-A-Creep-No-Seriously]]
  documents the junior-year transfer as identity reset following the
  GC scandal at his prior school. *"I changed schools and reset my
  reputation as a whole. New Neo, New Me."*
- **The [[Viking-Center]].** Neo's habitual school-day space —
  mornings, lunches, hallway transitions. The site of the [[D]]
  glance-pattern sequence in 2023.
- **The art-class community.** Arts 3-4 class with [[Ms-L]],
  [[Gavin]], the Art Club, and (briefly) [[D]]. The class-switch
  Neo executed mid-senior year happened here.
- **The AP track.** AP Government, AP Calculus AB, AP Computer
  Science Principles, Honors Physics (dropped). The
  [[AP-Exams-2024]] context is here.
- **The [[Vice-Principal-Incident]].** April 2024 — happened at
  Sunnyslope.
- **The faculty Neo names.** [[Cunningham]], [[Cummings]],
  [[Ms-L]], [[Dr-Lovell]], the unnamed [[Vice-Principal]], the
  [[Social-Worker]]. Mostly recalled positively at graduation in
  [[2026-03-17-High-School-Graduation]].
- **Graduation, May 2024.** Documented in
  [[2026-03-17-High-School-Graduation]] as two-year retrospective.
  The event closes the Sunnyslope chapter.

## Position in the corpus

Sunnyslope is the most-named place across the corpus's first half
(2023-mid 2024). It's the institutional context Neo's identity reset
happened inside, the social environment most of the early
crush-figures populated, and the operational site of every
[[2024]] AP exam, social-worker session, friendship attempt, and
(via the [[Vice-Principal-Incident]]) the 2024 cascade.

The school is one of the corpus's positive-affect institutional
anchors — Neo writes about graduation memory with affection toward
faculty (*"It serves as a fuel for my future endeavors"*). Compare
the [[Mother-Wound]] register: Sunnyslope is where Neo had
relationships with adults who weren't his parents and could be held
in affection without ambivalence.
