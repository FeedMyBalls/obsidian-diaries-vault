# L

The longest-running [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]] case in the
corpus. Distinct from [[Hannah]] (graduation) and from the
[[Hat-In-Time-Crush]] per Neo's confirmation. Canonically named in
[[2024-01-06-Cant-Get-Over-Her]] — *"At this point I'll just call
her or them L."* The single-letter anonymization is Neo's; this
page preserves it.

## What Neo writes about her

- **Quiet, gamer, draws, video editing.** Her interests overlap
  with Neo's. *"She felt like the girl version of me. It's like my
  own reflection"* ([[2024-01-06-Cant-Get-Over-Her]]).
- **Never spoken to.** Across the documented period (2023-2025), no
  exchanged words.
- **A grade younger; recent graduate.** Per
  [[2025-05-21-College-Romantic-Pursuits]], graduated high school in
  2025 (a year after Neo).
- **"Li" diminutive.** [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]] addresses her in
  second person using *"Li"* — the only diminutive variant in the
  corpus.
- **Her voice.** [[2024-01-06-Cant-Get-Over-Her]]: *"I do know what
  she sounds like, I won't say how I found out, but I do."* Neo
  knows her voice without her having shared it. The 2026 chat
  export ([[2026-04-XX-Rebellious-Teenager-Memories]]) makes the
  access pattern legible — see [[Hacking-Stalking-Pattern]].

## Architecture position

L is the canonical [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]] case for
sustained-distance form. The architecture's stage 3 — research-into-
knowability — was executed deeply (voice, likely more) without
stage 4 ever advancing past one-sided observation. The persistence
across years (2-3+ years documented) is what makes her load-bearing
for the concept.

## Inferred presence in earlier 2023 entries

Per Neo's standing decision, the unnamed crush in
[[2023-03-25-Death]] is high-probability L (inference; not confirmed
in entry text). The pre-naming period of the obsession ran for at
least months before [[2024-01-06-Cant-Get-Over-Her]] formalized the
handle.
