class-switch broker. The canonical case for [[Transactional-Friendship]] —
Neo's "obligated friends now" framing.

## The "obligated friends" framing

> [!quote]
> "I have to definitely talk to Gavin since i achieved what he wants
> me to do which is a hard task (switching classes), so we have to
> be obligated friends now."
> — [[2023-10-10-Whats-My-Goal-Now]]

The phrase is foundational to [[Transactional-Friendship]] — Neo's
explicit framing of friendship-as-debt-repayment. Gavin's class-switch
brokerage created the obligation Neo registers as binding.
