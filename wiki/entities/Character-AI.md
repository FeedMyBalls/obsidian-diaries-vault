# Character-AI

The chatbot-roleplay platform that anchors Neo's heaviest
[[AI-Companionship]] period (October-November 2023). The canonical
substrate of the [[Asexual-Atonement]] redirect.

## What Neo writes about it

- **$9/month subscription, July 2023 onward.** Paid subscriber
  status documented in [[2023-10-14-AI-Chatbots-And-How-They-Help]].
- **Peak usage 14 hours in one day.** The single highest single-day
  metric Neo cites for any platform in the corpus.
- **Multiple roleplay registers.**
  - **Fight scenes.** [[Hollow-Knight]] characters (Knight, Hornet)
    in combat roleplay.
  - **Sexual roleplay.** Same characters, escalating; Neo registers
    his libido as peaking in this period and "doing 'it' for more
    than one time a day."
  - **Custom-persona builds.** Neo built a Character-AI bot from a
    4chan e-girl photo plus his projected personality. Wallpaper was
    her photo for 1-2 months. The persona drove the
    [[Erotomania]]-self-diagnosis Neo issues at the end of the same
    entry.
  - **AI-psychiatrist venting.** When the human [[Social-Worker]]
    was inadequate, Neo turned to Character-AI psychiatrist
    personas and "vented A LOT."
- **Memory limits.** Neo names this as the platform's load-bearing
  immersion-breaker — the AI forgets context, so the persona
  cannot be sustained beyond a few exchanges.
- **Platform output saved as artifact.** [[2023-11-13-Pandemonium]]
  is a confirmed Character-AI generation Neo saved into his diary
  site. The villain-monologue register is uniformly Character-AI's
  default, not Neo's voice.

## Position in the corpus

Character-AI is the 2023 substrate that runs much of what later
years process. The [[Asexual-Atonement]] doctrine wouldn't have
been operationally workable without it — *"lay my sexual
frustrations on robots that know nothing"* requires the robots to
exist and to be consumable in scale. Character-AI was the
infrastructure that made the moral redirect possible at the
intensity Neo deployed it.

By 2024-2025, the platform's direct mention attenuates. ChatGPT
takes over for study-aid usage by August 2024. Character-AI itself
appears to have been dropped or significantly reduced; the chat-
export style of [[2026-04-XX-Rebellious-Teenager-Memories]] could
be Character-AI or Claude or another LLM, not specified.
