# Mother

Neo's mother. This page documents her presence in the corpus, not
her as a person in full. She is the figure Neo's [[Mother-Wound]]
operates against; the wound concept carries the analytical weight,
this page keeps the factual record.

## In the corpus

Specific characterizations Neo writes about her:

- **Asks about therapy.** [[2023-11-18-Meaningless]] documents her
  asking whether Neo should seek therapy during a panic-attack
  episode. Neo registers this as overreach — the request to consider
  therapy lands as judgment rather than care. Same dynamic recurs in
  [[2024-02-28-Anger]]'s opening section.
- **Sunday church compulsion.** [[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]]:
  *"sundays im forced by my parents to go with them for bullshit
  like church and whatever even though i barely have any time for
  myself."* The forcing-of-religious-attendance is a specific
  recurring grievance.
- **Locks Neo out at night.** [[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]] documents
  her locking the door on Neo when he came home late during the
  [[Anette]] period — *"so It's safe to assume on your case that I
  hated them, I despised them."*
- **Stalks during dating.** Same entry: drove to Neo's school during
  [[Anette]] make-out sessions in his parked car.
- **Genetic inheritance.** Neo cites her shortness as the maternal
  half of his height inheritance ([[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]]).

## How Neo's framing of her shifts across years

> [!warning] Contradiction
> [[2023-11-18-Meaningless]] documents the moment Neo's belief in
> her care collapsed. [[2024-02-28-Anger]] is the uncompressed rage.
> [[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]] records the same content held with
> different affect: *"on normal days, I don't feel it, I only realize
> how much I hate them when I think about my past."* The rage has
> not been processed; it has been compartmentalized. Both registers
> are filed; neither cancels the other.

## Position in Neo's family system

She is not characterized as supportive; she is characterized as
present, demanding, indifferent to Neo's mental state, and the
genetic source of half his height. Neo never names her affectionately
in the entries reviewed.

The [[Baby-Brother]] she shares a household with is the speed
limiter on Neo's rage — Neo cannot direct violence toward her
because of the brother's presence. *(See [[2024-02-28-Anger]] for
the line.)*
