# Dave

Anesthesia PCA Tech coworker at [[Mayo-Clinic]]. **23 years old**,
4 years older than Neo. The most extensively-portrayed living person
in the 2026 corpus. The functional second-character of Neo's
2026 reflective entries.

## What Neo writes about him

[[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]] contains the fullest portrait:

- **Demographics.** 23, "dark brown" complexion, "quite fat,"
  "weird beard hair," "balding."
- **Type 9 [[Enneagram-Type-9]] per Neo's typing.** "The lazy one.
  Always on his phone, slouched appearance, moves almost
  lethargically."
- **Possibly autistic** (Neo's read; same diagnostic basis Neo
  applies to himself).
- **Senior to Neo at work** but Neo "takes the lead" — gives orders,
  counts items, runs the [[System-Gaming]] supervisor-rule
  workarounds.
- **Listens.** Neo describes Dave as the only person Neo talks to
  every night during work; the listener-not-interlocutor frame.
  *"I've shared lots of topics with him, but like how people
  probably perceive me, he himself is quite inexpressive too, I
  don't think he cares much about things I talk about."*
- **Conversation partner for [[Enneagram-Type-9]] and cognitive-
  function teaching.** Neo uses Dave as the audience for
  enneagram-subtype lectures "to make my nights a little more
  bearable" — service-as-coping.
- **Pattern-collaborator.** [[2026-04-XX-Rebellious-Teenager-Memories]]
  documents Dave testing the supervisor's stocking procedure for
  Neo, reporting the failure, getting the rule relaxed. Neo's
  [[System-Gaming]] disposition operationalized through Dave.

## Position in the corpus

Dave is the [[Living-Contradiction]] in compressed form:

- Neo describes him in catalog detail with private contempt
  ("unpleasing to stare at," "lazy") and simultaneously names him
  as the only present-tense relational anchor of 2026 work life.
- Neo *teaches* him (savior-complex-shaped) while ranking him below
  ("smarter, much more pragmatic" — Neo of himself) — both moves
  in the same paragraph.
- The asymmetric-listening pattern Neo runs everywhere else is on
  display here: Neo talks, Dave listens, Neo registers the
  listening as inadequate while continuing to talk.

[[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]]: *"if I were to leave the job, he
wouldn't be no different to a flash of memory that I'll forget
instantly, well like all people are I guess."* The cold-disposition
register Neo applies to most non-family people, with Dave as the
named instance.
