# Shane

Old friend referenced briefly in [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]]. Connection
dates from "the 2021 glory days" — pre-[[GC-Scandal-2021]] or
overlapping with it. Reconnection in August 2024 documented:

> [!quote]
> "Stopped working out and learning japanese, but i did manage to
> talk to shane again, i missed him, reminds me of the 2021 glory
> days, we talked a bit about the past, oh just how much i wanted
> to vent to him and tell him how much i miss those days haha."
> — [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]]

The reconnection is registered with one of the corpus's rare
unambiguous "I missed him" moments. Shane is one of very few
named males Neo writes about with simple affection.
