# Eva

[[Mayo-Clinic]] coworker, 24 years old. Single mention in
[[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]] — Neo notes "good convos for a bit" with
her, then declines romantic possibility on the basis of the 7-year
age gap (Eva 24, Neo 18 at the time).

> [!quote]
> "I did have good convos for a bit with Eva, a co worker of mine
> but she's 24. a 7 year gap, so fuck that. But even then, i'm
> not really interested at all that way."
> — [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]]

The 7-year age gap is the inverse mirror of the 15-year [[Joe]]
case (where the older party pursued the younger). Neo's stance
toward age-gap relationships is consistent in direction (declines
larger gaps) but the intensity differs based on who's the older
party.
