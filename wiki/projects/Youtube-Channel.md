# Youtube Channel

Neo's [[Hollow-Knight]]-focused YouTube channel. The corpus's
longest-running creative project — multi-year ambition since 2016,
with peak active investment in 2023, attenuation from
[[Computer-Loss-2024]] onward, dormancy by 2026. Unrealized as a
career; documented as the project Neo most-recurrently writes
*about* even when not actively producing.

## Status (April 2026)

**Dormant.** No documented production since the [[Vice-Principal-Incident]]
removed Neo's computer in April 2024. The Pantheon V video Neo had
prepared in October 2023 — the project's intended career-pivot
moment — was edited in late 2023 / early 2024 but no later entry
documents the upload's reception or follow-up.

The 2026 reflective entries do not mention the channel directly.
The last documented active engagement is the 2023 Pantheon V
completion arc.

## The 2023 peak

> [!quote]
> "i actually expected to get a thousand back in 2016. it's been
> more than half a decade now and i haven't gotten to that point
> yet."
> — [[2023-10-11-Completed-Pantheon-V-And-My-Youtube-Career]]

October 11, 2023 — Pantheon V (Absolute Radiance) beaten in
[[Hollow-Knight]]. Three months of grinding documented. Trailer
edited. The intended Pantheon V video would have been the channel's
career-pivot moment.

The entry articulates the ambition's stakes: if the video
"explodes and sorta 'makes' my channel," Neo would *"double down
and start focusing on the HK vids."* If it failed, the project
would be left "in the dust to pretty much rot." Plan A vs Plan B
framing parallels the same shape Neo will later apply to
[[Long-Term-Plan]].

The closing prayer-form: *"wish me luck, and i hope you do it
future me.. make them proud. make me proud.. make us proud."*

## Stylistic models (folded in per Neo's call)

Neo names three YouTubers as influences in
[[2023-10-11-Completed-Pantheon-V-And-My-Youtube-Career]]:

- **Technoblade** — for skyblock-video intro register and
  inspirational-speech style. Neo planned to model the Pantheon V
  intro on this register.
- **Videogamedunkey** — for video-essay writing. *"his La La Land
  video, where at the end of the video his message was so cool
  accompanied with the music."*
- **Pyro** — for documentary-style game-essay. The myhouse.wad
  video specifically named as the model Neo would aspire toward
  if the channel achieved scale.

These three are folded into this page rather than getting their
own entity pages per Neo's call. They function in the corpus as
reference-points rather than independent figures.

## The 2024 collapse

[[2024-04-22-My-Last-Prayer-Amen]] documents the
[[Vice-Principal-Incident]]'s consequence — computer privileges
revoked at home. Without the desktop, video editing was impossible.
The Pantheon V video either was already uploaded by then or wasn't;
the corpus does not document either way explicitly. The channel's
active production stops here.

## Position in the corpus

The Youtube-Channel is Neo's pre-[[Long-Term-Plan]] life-project —
the thing he organized creative time around before medicine became
the operational answer. The project's failure (or stasis) preceded
the [[Workaholism-as-Identity]] period and is, in effect, the
displaced ambition the work-and-medicine path replaced.

The 2016 origin date is significant. Neo has been pursuing this
ambition since age 9. Its 2023-2024 peak-and-collapse is the most
sustained creative pursuit in the corpus, and its dormancy by 2026
is registered without grief — Neo's [[Long-Term-Plan]] energy has
fully redirected.
