# Long-Term Plan

Neo's medical-career execution path. The structural project the
[[Workaholism-as-Identity]] schedule serves; the operational form
of the [[Savior-Complex]] (per the standing flag below). Documented
visually in `raw/assets/Long-Term-Plan.jpg` and verbally in
[[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]] as "the 10 year plan still in
effect" — that verbal phrasing is the alias; *Long-Term-Plan* is
the canonical name.

## The savior-complex framing

> [!quote]
> "Ambition fuels me, not for myself but for others, that's the
> purpose of medicine."
> — [[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]]

This line is the project's thesis. Medicine is not a job Neo wants;
it is the [[Savior-Complex]] **made operational at career scale** —
service-orientation industrialized through licensing, training, and
institutional setting. The schedule's sustainability — anesthesia
tech night shifts, ASU labs on no sleep, babysitting in afternoons —
runs on the meaning the [[Savior-Complex]] supplies. Without that
frame, the cost-benefit of the path doesn't cohere. With it, the
path is the only legible answer to the [[Undeservingness]] doctrine
(must earn presence by serving) Neo holds.

Every reference to the project in source pages threads back to this.
Cross-link: [[Savior-Complex]].

## Education path

Per the JPG, structured as a critical chain:

```
HIGH SCHOOL  (AP Calc AB + AP Credits)
    ↓
PVCC         (AS Biology + Pre-Med Prereqs)
    ↓
ASU WEST     (BSN Nursing)
    ↓
RN LICENSURE
    ↓
ICU NURSE
    ↓
   ┌─────────────────┴─────────────────┐
PLAN A (Physician)              PLAN B (CRNA)
    │                                  │
Post-Bacc Neuroscience          1-3 Years ICU RN
    │                                  │
Medical School                  CRNA / DNP Program
    │                                  │
Neurology Residency             CRNA
    │
Cognitive Neurology Fellowship
    │
Cognitive Neurologist
```

The fork is structurally important. **Plan A** (physician → cognitive
neurology) is the higher-aspiration, longer, more uncertain branch.
**Plan B** (CRNA via DNP) is the bounded-cost, fully-credentialed
fallback. The split reads as Neo's hedge against his own ambition —
both branches end in well-compensated specialist medicine; one
preserves the [[Neo-Mythology]] register's "save the world" scale,
one delivers the operational satisfaction without the long-tail risk.

Each step is upward in clinical responsibility while maintaining

double-duty (income, experience-for-the-next-credential, professional
reference network).
