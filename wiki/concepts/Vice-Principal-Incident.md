# Vice-Principal Incident

April 22, 2024 (or shortly before — entry written same day). Senior
year. Neo searched and accessed a website at school that staff
classified as a hacking attempt; was sent to the vice principal one-
on-one; pleaded not to have his parents called; was overruled. The
event precipitates the rest of 2024 — [[Computer-Loss-2024]],
[[Doomscrolling-Period]], deepened [[Method-Research]],
[[2024-05-08-Desperation]]'s [[Wizz]] entry. Foundational event for
the year.

## What happened

The specific website is not named in the source. The classification
("hacking" rather than just "inappropriate browsing") suggests the
site or the access method touched something faculty read as a
[[System-Gaming]] move beyond ordinary policy violation.

Neo's plea — *"I begged him not to call my parents"* — was rejected.
The vice principal called. Parents responded with what Neo registers
as berating. Computer privileges revoked at home.

## What followed

The material consequences cascade across the rest of 2024:

- **[[Computer-Loss-2024]].** Without the desktop, Neo lost video
  editing, gaming, structured creative outlets. The phone became
  the sole device.
- **[[Doomscrolling-Period]].** TikTok reinstalled. Reels became
  primary. 4chan-r9k revisited.
- **AP-exam prep abandoned.** *"I've already accepted I'm either
  getting a 3 or straight up failing."*
- **[[Method-Research]] migration.** [[Glock]] and [[Nitrogen-Gas-Method]]
  (in-home/delivered options) gave way to [[Fentanyl]] via TOR
  ([[Illicit-Acquisition]]) — the only acquisition path available
  from a phone.
- **The [[Social-Worker]] referral followup.** Same social worker
  Neo had seen in October 2023; second documented session,
  characterized as ineffective ("yawning").

## The structural function

The Vice-Principal Incident sits at almost the same structural
position [[GC-Scandal-2021]] occupies for 2023: an authority
exposure that materializes the [[Living-Contradiction]] saint-outside
/ rule-bender-inside duality, generates parental escalation, and
forces the next several months of life onto a different track.

Where [[GC-Scandal-2021]] generated the asexual-atonement moral
reorganization, the Vice-Principal Incident generates the
material-infrastructure reorganization (no computer → phone →
algorithmically-fed content → deeper isolation → method shift).
Same structural shape, different vector.
