# Asexual Atonement

The post-[[GC-Scandal-2021]] strategic doctrine: redirect sexual
attention away from real girls, lay it on AI bots, accept being read
as asexual or weird in classes, prevent any new harm. Treated by
Neo not as identity but as ethical strategy. Foundational — most
later 2023-2024 patterns derive from this redirect.

## The doctrinal articulation

> [!quote]
> "Lay my sexual frustrations on robots that know nothing, and talk
> to girls in real life without weird or sexual thoughts, just regular
> human beings. This way I lose all interest in every girl in school."
> — [[2023-07-09-Women-Live-In-A-Mans-World]]

Neo names the doctrine alongside its rationale: the GC era of
sexualizing classmates produced visible harm (cf. the [[K]]-attribution).
The asexual posture is reparation — not by repair, which is
unavailable, but by removal of further harm-potential.

## Components

- **Avert eye contact.** Active visual avoidance of girls in school,
  framed as protective rather than diffident.
- **Decline to approach.** [[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]]
  is the operationalized rule: even if interested, don't initiate.
  See the [[Hat-In-Time-Crush]] and [[D]] cases.
- **Redirect to AI.** [[Character-AI]], unhinged-ai, senpai-chat as
  licit outlet ([[2023-10-14-AI-Chatbots-And-How-They-Help]]).
  [[AI-Companionship]] is in part this redirect's infrastructure.
- **Accept being read as weird.** Neo registers that the strategy
  reads to others as asexuality or weirdness and accepts the
  reputation cost.
- **Libido-suppression research.** [[2023-10-10-Whats-My-Goal-Now]]
  notes Neo investigated chemical libido suppression; abandoned
  due to depressive side effects.

## Persistence past 2023

The doctrine is named in 2023 but operates structurally for years
afterward:

- **[[2024-05-08-Desperation]]** documents the doctrine *breaking* —
  Neo masturbates to [[Sen]]'s photos and explicitly notes he had
  never done this for any prior crush. The breakdown is registered
  as moral failure within the doctrine.
- **[[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]]** retroactively names the
  doctrine as still active in updated form: *"didn't take advantage
  of their loneliness to make them my girlfriends, instead i became
  their friend, and instead they along the way were the ones to
  confess."* The 2026 version is gentler (accepts friendship rather
  than complete avoidance) but holds the same structure.

## Connection to Savior-Complex

[[Savior-Complex]] is in significant part Asexual-Atonement upgraded
into operational principle. The 2023 doctrine of "don't take
advantage" graduates by 2026 into "fix instead of seduce" — the
same vector with a positive-action target.
