# System Gaming

Neo's loophole-finding disposition. Rules accepted nominally, then
worked around for efficiency or thrill. The pattern operates across
contexts — school, hacking, employment — with consistent shape and
consistent self-justification ("for the patient or the greater
good"). Concept name per Neo's call (replacing earlier
"Loophole-Operating").

## The shape

The pattern across instances:

1. A rule or system creates an obstacle.
2. Neo identifies a path around it that preserves the appearance of
   compliance.
3. The path is exploited, often with a collaborator (best friend in
   high school, [[Dave]] at work).
4. If exposed, Neo provides post-hoc legitimating language ("for the
   greater good," "more efficient").
5. Frequently, the system *changes* in Neo's favor afterward — rules
   relaxed, supervisors removed, etc. Neo registers this as "luck."

## Documented instances

- **High school suspensions.** Senior year detentions, almost-suspensions,
  cut classes ([[2024-04-25-Copium]] documents one cut-class day).
  The [[GC-Scandal-2021]] sat at the upper bound of the pattern.
- **The [[Hacking-Stalking-Pattern]].** The full-extreme of System
  Gaming applied to relational unavailability. Credentials, password
  managers, school accounts. *"i taught this method to my best
  friend and we built a hacking empire in the school."*
- **[[Black-Market-Selling]].** Robux for cheap on the black market;
  fake courses; piracy "for the thrill" rather than need. Selling-side
  application of the same disposition.
- **The [[Vice-Principal-Incident]].** Neo accessed a website at
  school that staff classified as hacking. The line between
  System Gaming and rule-violation moved on a single keystroke.
- **Mayo Clinic stocking workaround.** Documented in
  [[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]] and openly retold in
  [[2026-04-XX-Rebellious-Teenager-Memories]]. Supervisor's procedure
  produced inefficiency; Neo persuaded [[Dave]] to test the
  procedure failing on purpose; Dave reported the failure;
  procedure was relaxed. Supervisor fired one week before the chat
  export. *"Luck is on my side too."*

## The legitimating language

The 2026 form has acquired a register the school-era version lacked.
"For the patient or the greater good" is the language Neo deploys
when the System Gaming might reflect badly. The legitimating frame
isn't insincere — Neo does believe the workaround is better — but
it's also a learned move that wasn't available to a sophomore.

## The disposition in 2026

[[2026-04-XX-Rebellious-Teenager-Memories]] frames the disposition
as continuous from 14 to 19. The arc Neo names is from
"narcissism" to "inferiority/savior complex" — but the System Gaming
disposition itself didn't change. What changed is what gets gamed
*for*. Earlier: thrill, status, proximity to crushes. Later:
operational efficiency, patient care, work cohort cohesion.

The capability has not been disowned. *"I still do loopholes."*
