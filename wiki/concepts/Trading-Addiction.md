# Trading Addiction

Neo's options-trading habit on Robinhood, named without that label
in [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]] and operative throughout late 2024.
Distinct from his savings habit (which is conservative); this is
the gambling tier — SPY puts, calls, FAFSA book money redirected
into options, $40 lost in a day "I don't even care anymore."

## The instance the entry organizes around

> [!quote]
> "Could've been so rich right now. If hinge works, i could have a
> girlfriend right now i could share stories with and just hold
> hands after a long tiring day."
> — [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]]

The missed SPY 560 puts, expiring Sept 5 2024 — the trade Neo did
not place because he overslept market open — would have made him
"almost 100 grand" in his account. The leitmotif of the entry. The
miss is the structure for everything else in the entry — Hinge
failures, [[L]] memory, parents-as-hostile, [[Method-Research]]
psychedelic-pivot. All of it organized around what would have
worked, hadn't.

## The shape of the addiction

- **FAFSA-redirect.** $400 in book money pivoted into options. Money
  with a designated educational purpose deployed instead at risk.
- **Daily losses tolerated.** $40-loss days reported with flat
  affect. The losses don't register as defeat; they're the price of
  the engagement.
- **Treating $5.7k as nothing.** *"That shit can disappear in a
  matter of seconds."* The normal money-anxiety register replaced
  by gambling logic.
- **No stop-loss self-rule.** Neo doesn't describe a position size
  or a daily loss limit. The addiction's signature is the absence
  of the bounding moves a non-addicted version of the same habit
  would have.

## The structural function

The trading is the [[Aesthetic-of-Melancholy]] applied to money. The
near-miss-as-leitmotif structure is identical to the
[[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]]'s near-miss structure — a
counterfactual better-life imagined in detail, mourned, then
exited from rather than corrected. *"If hinge works"* and *"could've
made $100k"* are the same sentence in two registers.

By 2026, the trading register is absent from the entries. Whether
the addiction was abandoned, processed, or just no longer documented
is not establishable from the corpus.
