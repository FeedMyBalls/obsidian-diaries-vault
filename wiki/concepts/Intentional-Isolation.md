# Intentional Isolation

The strategic withdrawal Neo executes February 1, 2026. Distinct from
prior periods of involuntary loneliness or the post-[[Anette]]
processing register: this is *chosen*, *named*, and *dated*. Frames
2026 as the year Neo deliberately empties his social environment
to recover access to himself.

## The decision, dated

> [!quote]
> "I haven't used Tiktok or Instagram and even twitter since feb 1.
> I've deleted them all, socially isolated. i go to school to work
> to home rinse and repeat."
> — [[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]]

The strategy is named with operative components: deleted social
media, ghosted [[Lia]] (his named "bestfriend"), drove only between
[[ASU-West]], [[ASU-Downtown]], [[Mayo-Clinic]], and home. By the
March 14 entry the isolation has held six weeks.

## Neo's own framing

The framing across 2026 entries is consistent:

- **Recovery.** *"It's like, finding myself again. It's like I lost
  myself along the years and I'm slowly recovering myself, catching
  unto my interests again and learning them for fun."*
  ([[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]])
- **Lucidity.** *"I'm more lucid when I'm alone."*
  ([[2026-03-16-She-Loves-Me-She-Loves-Me-Not]])
- **Preference.** *"I like being alone. Its made me realize, I don't
  want romance or friends, I'm content with myself."*
  ([[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]])

## What it surfaced

[[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]] is structured as a discovery
*because* of the isolation: with no strangers traumadumping at him,
Neo notices his pre-isolation pattern. The
[[Savior-Complex]] becomes legible to him only once it's
temporarily inactive. Multiple 2026 entries credit the isolation
with making the year's reflective writing possible.

## The shadow

[[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]] complicates the story. Two paragraphs
into the same entry that praises being alone, Neo writes the
opening wish for "the comfort of another person, embracing the
touch of someone else who reciprocates your feelings." The
isolation is preference and deprivation simultaneously, per
[[Living-Contradiction]]'s standard pattern.

[[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]] also names appetite-escalation
within the strategy: *"I want to stop talking to Dave and just do my
job, I want to move out and not babysit, I just want my own time
and my own space."* The isolation works enough that Neo wants more
of it. Whether that's recovery deepening or avoidance ratcheting is
not resolved.
