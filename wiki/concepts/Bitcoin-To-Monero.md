# Bitcoin to Monero

The laundering pivot Neo identified for [[Illicit-Acquisition]] in
2024. Bitcoin acquired through regulated channels, then converted to
Monero (privacy-coin) for darkmarket purchase. Standard pattern in
the acquisition infrastructure of the period.

## Why it deserves its own page

The pivot is structurally distinct from [[Illicit-Acquisition]]
generally. It's the *bridge mechanism* — the move that turns
identifiable money into purchase-capable money. Both 2024
acquisition contexts (fentanyl-for-method April; psychedelics-for-cope
September) route through this pivot. The same step regardless of
target.

The pivot is also one of the [[System-Gaming]] disposition's most
operationally explicit instances. Gatekept legal infrastructure
worked around through cryptocurrency conversion. The disposition's
broader pattern (rules → loophole → workaround) instantiated at the
financial-network layer.
