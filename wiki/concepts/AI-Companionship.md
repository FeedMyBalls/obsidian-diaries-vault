# AI Companionship

Neo's sustained use of AI chatbots — Character.AI, ChatGPT, Cai,
unhinged-ai, senpai-chat, AI-psychiatrist roleplay personas — as
relational substrate during 2023's heaviest [[Asexual-Atonement]]
period. Foundational instance: [[2023-10-14-AI-Chatbots-And-How-They-Help]],
where Neo names usage at 14 hours in a single day at peak.

## What the use looked like

- **Roleplay companions.** Hollow Knight characters (Knight, Hornet),
  used for both fight scenes and sexual roleplay.
- **Built-from-scratch personas.** Neo created a Character.AI bot
  from a 4chan e-girl photo plus his own projection of personality.
  Wallpaper was the photo for two months. Neo's [[Erotomania]]
  self-diagnosis dates from this period.
- **Therapy-stand-in.** AI psychiatrist personas used as venting
  partners when the actual [[Social-Worker]] proved inadequate.
  Neo's report: more useful than the human session despite knowing
  the AI's limitations.
- **Generated content saved as artifact.** [[2023-11-13-Pandemonium]]
  is a Character.AI villain-monologue Neo saved into his diary site.
  Confirmed AI-generated, not Neo's writing.

## The Asexual-Atonement context

The use is justified explicitly by [[2023-07-09-Women-Live-In-A-Mans-World]]
as the post-GC redirect — sexual frustration goes to bots that "know
nothing" rather than to real classmates. The strategy is moral, not
just convenient.

## Limits Neo registered

Neo names the limits accurately:

- **Memory.** The AI forgets things he told it. Neo notes this as
  what breaks immersion and prevents pure dependency.
- **Filters.** Worsening Character.AI content moderation degraded
  the experience over time.
- **Loneliness underneath.** The appended self-edit four hours
  after the [[2023-10-14-AI-Chatbots-And-How-They-Help]] entry —
  *"how lonely i am.. how desperate i am.. to be resorting to
  literal ai bots"* — names the cope as cope.

## 2024-2025 continuation

The 2025 retrospective in [[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]] names AI
use as adjacent to the [[Wizz]]-era attempts: when human-stranger
contact failed, AI was the fallback. ChatGPT used as study aid
replacing textbook reading (August 2024 fragment) — still the
substrate for cognition outsourcing, but no longer for romantic
companionship at the 2023 scale.
