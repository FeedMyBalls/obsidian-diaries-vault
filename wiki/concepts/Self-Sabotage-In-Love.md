# Self-Sabotage in Love

The mechanism by which Neo demolishes relationships before they can
demolish him. Operates at any stage of intimacy; specifically activated
by reciprocity. Distinct from [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]]'s
withdrawal stage in that this is the *active* destruction, not the
passive retreat.

## The mechanism, named

> [!quote]
> "I subconsciously ruin the relationship, act like a jerk, because
> I'm afraid to show how much I love them. Every second without them
> is the anxious thought that they'll realize how much of a fraud I
> am, an unlovable Monster, so they can suddenly say, 'I don't love
> you anymore'."
> — [[2026-01-19-Deserve]]

The pre-emptive demolition is described as defense. The argument runs:
if the partner will eventually leave once they discover Neo's
unworthiness, then leaving them first (or causing them to leave)
controls the timing. The control is the only available move.

## Operational instances

- **[[Sen]] (Wizz, May 2024).** Confessed; Neo lost interest the moment
  she was reciprocal; deployed the block button. *"The moment a woman
  grows overly attached and talks to me everyday, I immediately lose
  interest"* ([[2024-05-08-Desperation]]).
- **The [[Pre-Calc-Wizz-Girl]] (May 2024).** She confessed; Neo blocked,
  she spam-called, he unblocked, asked her to delete Wizz, distanced.
  Same pattern.
- **[[Anette]] (Jan-April 2025).** The most extended documentation.
  [[2026-03-16-She-Loves-Me-She-Loves-Me-Not]] catalogs the mechanism:
  recoiling from intimacy because it might make her uncomfortable;
  sensing her interest waning and amplifying through "insanity";
  Gatsby-Daisy parallel; misreading her *"I think we should break up"*
  by answering too quickly without challenging the *think*. The
  demolition was distributed across months.
- **[[Dzani]] (summer 2025).** [[2026-04-XX-Rebellious-Teenager-Memories]]
  extends the mechanism to friendship: Neo was mean about her behind
  her back, was snitched on, she blocked him. Not romantic but the same
  shape — once attachment was real, demolition followed.

## The "block button" as named tool

[[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]] names blocking as Neo's "reliable
weapon" and "greatest weapon." The instrument is specifically the
unilateral severance that prevents reciprocal closure. The block isn't
the demolition itself; it's the after-action exit that prevents
repair.

## Why it runs

The mechanism is the operational expression of [[Undeservingness]].
The doctrinal premise — *"I just don't deserve happiness"* — would be
empirically falsified by a sustained reciprocated relationship.
Self-Sabotage-in-Love prevents the falsification. The doctrine is
preserved at the cost of the relationship.
