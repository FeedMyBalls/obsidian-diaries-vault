# Blackpill

The ideology Neo accepts in [[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]]:
"You already lost, there's no hope, so why not just give up." A
nihilistic-deterministic worldview originating in 4chan-r9k and
adjacent online communities, organizing claims about looks-based
social hierarchy as biologically fixed. Neo's relationship to it
across years is uneven — accepted explicitly in late 2023, intensified
through 2024, partially revised in 2026.

## The acceptance

> [!quote]
> "I accept blackpill ideology, it's a cure for any foolish ideas
> that I might think of. You already lost, there's no hope, so why
> not just give up."
> — [[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]]

The framing is striking: blackpill named not as truth-claim but as
*epistemic prophylactic*. The ideology's function for Neo is to
inoculate against optimism. Believing the worst preempts being
disappointed by it.

## What blackpill organizes for Neo

- **Genetic determinism on appearance.** [[Body-Dysmorphia]] meets
  ideological scaffolding. *"Bone structure defines how people will
  think of you."*
- **[[Halo-Effect]] and [[Looksmaxxing]] vocabulary.** Halo-effect
  cited as the mechanism for opportunity-distribution; looksmaxxing
  vocabulary (manlet, canthal tilt, mewing) used as self-assessment.
- **The dating market as solved.** *"Almost all girls (90% or higher)
  are taller than me, so I already fail that criteria."* The
  argument has the form of empirical observation; the function is
  permission to stop trying.
- **A conspiratorial-political adjacency.** [[Blackpill-Vocabulary]]
  ("glowie," "psyop") and the [[2024-07-04-Palestine-And-Israel]]
  essay show the ideology's slide from sex-and-status into
  geopolitics. Same epistemic structure: hidden hierarchies,
  predetermined outcomes, doomed effort.

## The 2024 intensification

The [[Doomscrolling-Period]] reinforces blackpill materially. Neo
turns his FYP "into a mosh pit" of nightcore, hyperborean,
blackpill content ([[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]]). The
[[2024-07-04-Palestine-And-Israel]] essay marks the
political-radicalization peak.

## The 2026 partial revision

[[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]] contains the corpus's strongest
revision attempt:

> [!quote]
> "All that depression, that pain and sadness I harbored for years
> was all for nothing, simply because I browsed too much 4chan."
> — [[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]]

The doctrinal commitment is reframed as ideological-environmental
rather than constitutional. *"I might've been sad for other reasons,
but at least it's because of things I lack anymore, since I'm
surprisingly well off now."* The blackpill's concrete claims fail
when checked against Neo's 2026 status (he is, by his own
assessment, attractive enough, capable enough, financially
independent — most of the doctrine's empirical predictions don't
hold).

But the revision doesn't fully take. Neo continues: *"but maybe
there was a reason I succumbed to it so easily."* The blackpill
explained sadness Neo was already feeling; removing the explanation
doesn't remove the sadness. [[Aesthetic-of-Melancholy]] survives
the ideology's partial dissolution.
