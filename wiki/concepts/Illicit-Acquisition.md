# Illicit Acquisition

The procurement infrastructure Neo researched and partially deployed
through 2024 — TOR, Bitcoin → Monero conversion, plug-sourcing on
darkmarket platforms. Initially scoped to suicide-method acquisition
([[Fentanyl]]); scope-expanded to psychedelic-cope acquisition by
September 2024. Distinct from [[Black-Market-Selling]] (which is
selling-side); this is the buying-side disposition.

> [!info]
> Per Neo's standing decision, technical specifics — vendor sourcing
> guidance, platform identifiers, transaction details — stay in the
> raw entries only. This page treats the acquisition pattern as a
> meta-disposition.

## The infrastructure

- **TOR.** Browser/network used to access darkmarket platforms.
- **Bitcoin → Monero pivot.** Standard laundering path: acquire
  Bitcoin from a regulated venue, convert to Monero (privacy coin)
  for purchase. [[Bitcoin-To-Monero]] gets its own page for the
  pivot's role.
- **Plug-sourcing.** Vendor reputation research within darkmarket
  platforms.
- **Methods of payment.** Neo briefly mentions intent to use a
  parent's card to acquire Bitcoin ([[2024-04-22-My-Last-Prayer-Amen]]) —
  the financial-fraud component sits inside the acquisition pattern.

## Scope expansion (April → September 2024)

- **April 2024 ([[2024-04-22-My-Last-Prayer-Amen]]):** scope is
  fentanyl for suicide. Computer privileges revoked from the
  [[Vice-Principal-Incident]]; acquisition feasibility sketched
  from the iPhone.
- **April-May 2024 ([[2024-04-25-Copium]]):** acquisition still
  scoped to fentanyl; "looking for the right plug."
- **September 2024 ([[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]]):** scope expands.
  *"im rich, i should start taking drugs. go to the black market.
  use my remaining robinhood funds and convert to btc, then convert
  to xmr, then buy psychadelics… Just to forget everything painful
  in my life."* The same acquisition channel now serves cope rather
  than method. Same infrastructure; different target.

## The disposition

Illicit-Acquisition operates on the same engineering-logic as
[[Method-Research]] and [[System-Gaming]]: identify obstacle, find
workaround, evaluate options on cost-reliability axes. The
particular obstacle here is age (Neo was 18 in 2024) and legal
gatekeeping. The acquisition channel was designed to route around
both.

By 2025-2026 the disposition's deployment is absent. The
infrastructure presumably remains accessible, but no entries
document use. The shift correlates with [[Method-Research]]'s
disappearance and [[Workaholism-as-Identity]]'s consolidation.
