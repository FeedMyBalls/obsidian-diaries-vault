# Transactional Friendship

Neo's framing of friendship as accumulated obligation rather than
chosen tie. Friends are people who attached after providing or
receiving favors; the relationship runs on ledger logic. Distinct
from [[Savior-Complex]] (which is asymmetric service to strangers) —
this is what happens when the asymmetric service produces a stable
peer relation Neo then must maintain.

## The framing, named

> [!quote]
> "I always seem to attract people to be my friends and they become
> close with me almost instantly despite having others already as
> their friends... [Gavin] helped me a hard task (switching classes),
> so we have to be obligated friends now."
> — [[2023-10-10-Whats-My-Goal-Now]]

The "obligated friends now" is the load-bearing phrase. The friendship
is real; the binding is moral debt. Neo names this as the operating
model rather than incidental condition.

## Operational instances

- **Gavin** — Neo switched into Arts 3-4 on Gavin's recommendation;
  Gavin had gone to bat with the teacher; Neo registers the resulting
  tie as obligation.
- **Cesar (sophomore year), Alec (junior-senior)** — same pattern
  ([[2023-10-10-Whats-My-Goal-Now]]: "every year too").
- **Strangers Neo helped on Wizz, Discord, etc.** — the
  [[Savior-Complex]] asymmetric form. Many of these would have
  graduated to transactional friendship had Neo not deployed the
  block-button when they grew attached.
- **The performance tax with the social worker.** [[2024-04-22-My-Last-Prayer-Amen]]
  notes: *"People force themselves to listen to me. everything is
  transactional."* The frame extended to therapeutic relationships.

## Why it runs

Several pressures converge:

- **[[Undeservingness]].** Real chosen friendship would require Neo
  believing he was someone people would *choose*. The transactional
  frame replaces choice with debt — easier to accept.
- **[[Living-Contradiction]].** Inside the GC era and after, Neo had
  reason to suspect his friendships were paid attachments. The
  transactional frame survives that period as the explanation that
  fits.
- **Charisma without belief in it.** [[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]]
  notes Neo's "magnetic aura" toward strangers — people attach to him
  rapidly. The transactional frame is the one Neo can hold without
  having to believe he deserves the attachment.
