# Black-Market Selling

The teenage-era pattern of off-platform commerce: Roblox-economy
grift, fake courses, piracy. Distinct from [[Illicit-Acquisition]]
(which is Neo as buyer of methods/cope substances); this is Neo as
seller of grey-market goods and digital contraband, motivated as
much by thrill as by money.

## The pattern as named

> [!quote]
> "i still do loopholes... selling robux for cheap on the black
> market, selling fake courses for money, pirating even tho i can
> afford it cuz its more fun and theres a thrill."
> — [[2026-04-XX-Rebellious-Teenager-Memories]]

The "even though i can afford it" qualifier is doing the structural
work. Black-Market Selling isn't survival commerce; it's
[[System-Gaming]] applied to commerce. The thrill is the point.

## Components

- **Roblox economy grift.** Selling Robux for cheap on off-platform
  black markets. Robux is the in-game currency; "cheap" implies
  acquired below platform-rate, then resold.
- **Fake courses.** Educational-product fraud. No specifics given;
  registered as one item in the broader pattern.
- **Piracy.** Software, media, content. Neo distinguishes his piracy
  from need-driven piracy by the affordability qualifier.

## The teenage continuity

The 2026 chat export retells the period (~14-18) as a unit: the GC
era ([[GC-Scandal-2021]]), the [[Hacking-Stalking-Pattern]], and
Black-Market Selling as parallel manifestations of the same
[[System-Gaming]] disposition aimed at status, thrill, and
proximity rather than survival. *"i was a huge asshole half angel."*
