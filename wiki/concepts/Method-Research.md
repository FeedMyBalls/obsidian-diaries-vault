# Method Research

The meta-pattern. Neo's engineering-logic disposition applied to
suicide methods, weighed against each other for reliability,
painlessness, and acquisition cost. Foundational to the 2023-2024
crisis arc and structurally absent from 2025-2026.

> [!info]
> Per Neo's standing decision, technical specifics from individual
> methods stay in the raw entries only. This page treats the
> *meta-pattern* — that Neo applies engineering thinking to the
> question — without reproducing comparative method detail.

## The disposition

Method Research is not the same as crisis ideation. Many people in
crisis state they want to die; few approach the question with
weight-pricing comparison logic. Neo does. Across 2023-2024 entries,
methods are evaluated on:

- **Reliability** (probability of death given attempt)
- **Painlessness** (subjective experience of dying)
- **Time-to-death** (window during which a "save" is possible)
- **Acquisition cost** (legal, financial, social)
- **Detection risk** (will anyone intervene)

The same calculus a logistics manager applies to procurement. The
applied register is the symptom — not the conclusion.

## The 2025-2026 absence

[[2025-12-08-7-Minutes]] is the cleanest evidence that something has
shifted: a sustained death-contemplation entry with zero method
content. Neo can write *about* dying without writing *toward* it.
[[Aesthetic-of-Melancholy]] separated from Method Research — a
separation that wasn't possible in 2023.

The structural shift is not framed by Neo as recovery; it's not
named at all. Method Research simply stops appearing. By 2026 the
[[Workaholism-as-Identity]] frame has displaced the engineering
disposition's target — same logistics-thinking, applied now to
shift schedules and stocking procedures.
