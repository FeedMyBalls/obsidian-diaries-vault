# Suicidal Drawing Incident

September 2023. Neo drew a Ronnie McNutt-style image of a person
shooting himself in the head. The drawing was discovered by his
honors physics teacher 27 days later. The teacher reported it; Neo
was sent to the [[Social-Worker]] for the first documented
intervention session. Filed as event because the *consequences*
(the social-worker referral, the framing of Neo as that-quiet-kid
to a wider faculty audience) outlive the discrete moment.

## What Neo said about it

> [!quote]
> "they saw my drawing of ronnie mcnutt blowing his head off like
> 27 days or so ago. they only saw it recently, and they think i
> wanna kill myself, which yes i definitely do and will do before i
> turn 20."
> — [[2023-10-10-Whats-My-Goal-Now]]

The flat affect is part of the event's signature. Neo treats the
referral as paperwork — something happening to him that he
processes with bureaucratic register, not the discovery of his
state.

## The Ronnie McNutt reference

[[Ronnie-McNutt]] is a documented suicide-livestream figure. Neo
returns to the reference in [[2024-01-02-Ill-Attempt-Suicide-Again]]'s
public-firearm-recruitment passage as model for both method and
audience. The drawing is the earliest dated McNutt invocation in
the corpus.

## What followed

- **First documented [[Social-Worker]] session** (October 2023).
  Neo's October entries make brief reference; the session is
  characterized as not particularly helpful.
- **The "quiet kid" reputation faculty-wide.** [[2023-10-10-Whats-My-Goal-Now]]
  documents the physics teacher mentioning Neo as "that quiet kid in
  class" during the session — Neo registers this as confirming what
  classmates also thought.
