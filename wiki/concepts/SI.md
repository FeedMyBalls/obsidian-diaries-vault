# SI

Neo's term for survival instinct. Used precisely once in the corpus,
in [[2024-01-01-Failed-Suicide-Attempt-Hanging]] — the entry that
documents the failed Jan 1 attempt. Files as concept rather than
just abbreviation because Neo's *naming* of the intervention as
*SI* rather than *I* (or *fear*, or *body*) is itself the data.

## The naming

> [!quote]
> "However when I was ready, SI kicked in (survival instinct) and
> forced me to just go down."
> — [[2024-01-01-Failed-Suicide-Attempt-Hanging]]

The grammar is third-person. "SI kicked in" — not "I changed my
mind." The intervention is described as something *other than Neo*,
acting on Neo. Neo's phenomenological experience is presented as
having been overruled by a sub-personal system. Whether the framing
is accurate or stylistic, the framing exists — Neo distinguishes the
deciding-self from the surviving-self.

## What the term licenses

By naming SI as separate, Neo can hold both: the wish to die remains
unrevised, and the body's contrary action is registered without
contradiction. The [[Living-Contradiction]] given technical
vocabulary. SI lets Neo say "I tried, and I survived, and both are
true" without either canceling the other.
