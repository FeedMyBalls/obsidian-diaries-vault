# Umbilical Origin Story

Neo's invocation of an at-birth origin myth: that he tried to kill
himself before being born by twisting the umbilical cord around his
neck. The story functions as a self-mythological frame — death-wish
as constitutional, predating any life events.

## The claim

> [!quote]
> "When I was first born. I already tried to kill myself, twisting
> the umbilical cord around my neck inside the womb."
> — [[2023-12-13-December-31st-Suicide-Day]]

The factual basis (a possible cord-around-neck birth complication
narrated to Neo by family) and the interpretive layer (it counts as
a suicide attempt) are not separated by Neo. The story is invoked
declaratively, as the opening data point in the December 31 2023
suicide-planning entry.

## How it functions

The story does specific work in Neo's self-narrative:

- **Constitutional framing.** If the death-wish predates any life
  events, then it isn't caused by any of them. The
  [[Vice-Principal-Incident]], the [[GC-Scandal-2021]], the
  [[Mother-Wound]] / [[Father-Wound]] — none of them produced it,
  per the story's logic. They merely revealed what was already there.
- **Symmetry argument.** Neo deploys the story as the prior to a
  parents-don't-care-about-suicidal-children argument: if even his
  birth was an escape attempt, his parents had nineteen years to
  notice and didn't.
- **[[Self-Mythologizing]].** The literary use is unmistakable.
  Origin myths are how characters get composed. Neo is composing
  himself.
