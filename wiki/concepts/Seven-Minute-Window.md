# Seven-Minute Window

The cardiac-arrest dying-experience meditation Neo develops in
[[2025-12-08-7-Minutes]]. Reportedly, the brain may retain some
function for ~7 minutes after the heart stops. Neo extrapolates: if
time-perception is cerebral, removing time-perception removes time —
the seven minutes might be subjectively eternal. Heaven and hell
collapse into a question of consciousness duration.

Filed under both *method* and *self-perception* because the
meditation is method-adjacent (death-contemplation) but operates
without method content — the sole 2025 entry in either domain that
treats death without naming a way to bring it about.

## The argument

> [!quote]
> "Maybe when we die, those 7 minutes are forever. The flashes
> before death might just be a measly moment for the outside
> world, but it might be forever to me. In an eternal dream like
> state where nothing else matters."
> — [[2025-12-08-7-Minutes]]

The structure is a Schopenhauer-inflected idealism: time is a
condition of cognition; remove cognition, time becomes plastic.
What follows is the most lyrical-philosophical writing in the
corpus.

## Why it earns its own concept

The meditation marks a structural transition. Through 2023-2024,
death-contemplation and [[Method-Research]] are continuous — Neo
imagines dying *and* researches how to bring it about, often in
the same entry. The Seven-Minute-Window is the first entry where
they are decoupled. Neo can sustain death-contemplation as
contemplation, without the method register engaging.

The decoupling is what makes 2026's [[Workaholism-as-Identity]] and
[[Intentional-Isolation]] strategies possible. Method had to
disappear before reflective-distance from death-imagery could
emerge. The Seven-Minute-Window is where that disappearance gets
documented.
