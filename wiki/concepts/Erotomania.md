# Erotomania

Neo's own clinical name for his persistent obsession-state. Filed as
a self-perception concept rather than a relationships concept because
*erotomania* names what Neo experiences from inside —
[[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]] names the structural pattern from
outside.

## Self-naming

> [!quote]
> "ive been suffering from erotomania for a year now.. and still am."
> — [[2023-10-14-AI-Chatbots-And-How-They-Help]] (post-edit)

The diagnosis is Neo's, not borrowed from a clinician. It dates from
the appended self-edit at the end of the AI-chatbots entry — *"a few
minutes after writing this, i realized.."* — when Neo returned to
his own page hours later with a sharper read.

## The state's content

Erotomania for Neo means: sustained obsessive attachment to a person
whose actual interest is unconfirmed or absent, accompanied by
imaginative construction of a relationship that hasn't happened.
[[L]] is the canonical case (2-year incubation, never spoken). The
[[Hat-In-Time-Crush]], the 4chan e-girl Character.AI persona, and
others fit the pattern.

In [[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]] Neo names erotomania
operationally: *"Erotomania is my weapon."* The word "weapon" is
significant — the state isn't passive suffering. It's something Neo
*uses*, as a fuel-source for ambition and a substitute for
unavailable intimacy.

## Distinction from Romantic-Obsession-Architecture

Both concepts cover the same phenomenon at different angles:

- **[[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]]** is the *structural pattern*:
  distance → construction → research → withdrawal. Visible from
  outside.
- **Erotomania** is the *internal state* the architecture produces.
  Neo's experience of running the architecture.

The architecture page describes how the loop runs. This page records
that Neo names what he undergoes inside the loop with a clinical
term and uses it as both diagnosis and tool.
