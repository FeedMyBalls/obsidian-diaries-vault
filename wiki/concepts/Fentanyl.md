# Fentanyl

The method Neo researched April 2024 onward, after the
[[Vice-Principal-Incident]] removed his computer-and-firearm
proximity. Acquisition planned via [[Illicit-Acquisition]] —
TOR, [[Bitcoin-To-Monero]] route, plug-sourcing. Per Neo's
standing decision, no acquisition specifics, dosage discussion,
or vendor information is reproduced in this wiki; the raw entries
retain the original text.

## The page's only function

To name that fentanyl was the preferred April-May 2024 method
target, that acquisition was attempted (research and sourcing),
and that the acquisition did not complete during the window the
entries cover. Specifics in the raw.

By [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]] the [[Illicit-Acquisition]] channel
had been repurposed toward psychedelics-as-cope rather than
fentanyl-as-method. The method-target shifts; the channel persists.
