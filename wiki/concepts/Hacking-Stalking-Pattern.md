# Hacking-Stalking Pattern

The systematic credential-access playbook Neo deployed in late high
school against girls he was attracted to. Confirmed openly in
[[2026-04-XX-Rebellious-Teenager-Memories]] (chat export). Explains
the "I won't say how I found out" voice-knowledge in
[[2024-01-06-Cant-Get-Over-Her]] and earlier inferred prior-knowledge
patterns.

> [!info]
> Per Neo's standing decision, technical specifics are summarized
> only — not reproduced — in this wiki. The raw entry retains the
> full detail.

## The playbook (summarized)

The chain of access Neo describes for a junior-year crush:

1. Find her on a public-facing study site (Quizlet) by username.
2. Pivot through cross-platform credential overlap to her school
   account. [Specific method redacted in the source.]
3. Reach her password manager.
4. Use her password manager to access her personal accounts —
   including Messenger, where Neo could read her conversations with
   her parents. [Specific method redacted in the source.]
5. Acquire incidental information: floorplan of her home, address,
   DeviantArt account containing her self-described trauma posts.

The same playbook was run on a second girl (later [[Dzani]]),
producing a more extensive interior dossier (Discord linktree,
trauma disclosures including SA from when she was 13).

## The "hacking empire"

Neo and a best friend ran a sustained operation through high school.
Neo describes the division of labor: he pursued depth on individual
targets; the friend pursued breadth across more accounts. *"He
likes to broaden his, but i like mine on more specificity, something
like quality over quantity or whatever."* The friend eventually
surpassed Neo in scope while Neo retained the deeper-knowledge
advantage on his specific targets.

## The Dzani case as singular

[[Dzani]] is the only case in the corpus where Neo formed a real
friendship with someone whose interior he had previously accessed
without her knowledge. They became close in summer 2025 — three
years after the playbook ran on her. *"she wasnt even aware i ddi
all that, i knew everything about her and i only sstatted talking
summer of 25 and it went so well we got so close."* The relationship
collapsed through [[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]] — Neo was mean about her
behind her back, was snitched on, blocked.

The Dzani case is the empirical instance of what the pattern is
*for*: not surveillance for its own sake, but a means to the
[[Erotomania]]-and-[[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]] state of
knowing-about-her without knowing-her.

## Where it sits in the larger architecture

The pattern is one stage of [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]]
(stage 3: research-into-knowability) executed at its most extreme.
It's a form of [[System-Gaming]] — credential-access as the
loophole-finding move applied to relational unavailability. It's
also one face of [[Living-Contradiction]] — saint outside (quiet
short cute fella) / hacker inside (full credential pivot).

## The 2026 self-reading

In the chat export, Neo names the arc-level shift: *"this past year
or so you have noticed ive been more leaning towards 'good' and
using my intelligence and myself as a stepping tool for others and
savior complex, so it went from full blown narcissism etc to
inferiority/savior complex."* The hacking-stalking pattern does not
appear to be active in 2026 (the [[Intentional-Isolation]] period
removes most of the targets), but the *capability* has not been
disowned. Neo's framing is "I was that"; not "I am no longer
capable of that."
