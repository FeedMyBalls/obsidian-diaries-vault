# Halo Effect

The cognitive bias by which attractive people are read as also
trustworthy, competent, kind. Neo invokes the term correctly in
[[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]] as the cited mechanism for why
appearance gates social outcomes — and therefore why
[[Body-Dysmorphia]] is not just distress but, in Neo's framing,
empirical disqualification.

## How Neo uses it

> [!quote]
> "They're gonna see good looking guys as angels due to the Halo
> effect because of their looks despite the atrocities they might
> carry out, and puke at the idea of seeing an ugly piece of shit
> like me and will literally enable a Holocaust to get rid of
> subhuman wretches like me."
> — [[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]]

The bias is invoked as evidence-not-distortion. Where the term in
psychology names a *bias* (an error in judgment), Neo uses it as
*structural fact* (a stable feature of how social outcomes
distribute). The slip is small but consequential — it's the move
that turns a research finding into a fatalist doctrine.

## Function in the ideology

Halo Effect serves as the mediating concept between [[Body-Dysmorphia]]
(individual experience) and [[Blackpill]] (systemic doctrine). It
provides the mechanism: appearance → halo effect → distributed
opportunities → Neo permanently disadvantaged. Without it, the
inferences blackpill makes don't connect.
