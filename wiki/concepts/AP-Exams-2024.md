# AP Exams 2024

The May 2024 Advanced Placement testing window. Neo's senior-year
exams — AP Government and Politics (taken May 8 2024 per
[[2024-05-08-Desperation]]), AP Calculus AB (May 13), AP Computer
Science Principles (May 15). The window is the immediate context
for [[2024-04-22-My-Last-Prayer-Amen]], [[2024-04-25-Copium]], and
[[2024-05-08-Desperation]] — the [[Vice-Principal-Incident]]
collapsed Neo's prep three weeks before the first exam.

## The structural function

The exams matter to the corpus mainly as the *dropped goal*. Across
February-April 2024 entries, Neo had been planning to "lock in" for
AP prep. The [[Vice-Principal-Incident]] removed his computer; the
[[Doomscrolling-Period]] absorbed the time he had reserved for
study; AP prep was abandoned weeks before the tests:

> [!quote]
> "I'm not even studying for the AP Exams anymore, and I've already
> accepted I'm either getting a 3 or straight up failing."
> — [[2024-04-25-Copium]]

The collapse of AP-Exams-2024 prep is structurally identical to the
collapse of [[Youtube-Channel]] momentum in the same period — both
casualties of the [[Computer-Loss-2024]] state.

## Outcomes

The corpus does not document scores. By [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]]
Neo is in [[PVCC]], so the AP outcomes — whatever they were — did
not fundamentally alter the [[Long-Term-Plan]] trajectory.
