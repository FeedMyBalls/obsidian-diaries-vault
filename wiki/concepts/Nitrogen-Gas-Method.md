# Nitrogen Gas Method

The method Neo planned for December 31, 2023. A nitrogen-gas tank
was ordered and did not arrive in time; the Jan 1 attempt substituted
hanging ([[2024-01-01-Failed-Suicide-Attempt-Hanging]]) and was
interrupted by [[SI]]. Per Neo's standing decision, no technical
specifics are reproduced in this wiki; the raw entries retain the
original text.

## The page's only function

To name that the method was researched and ordered for a specific
dated attempt, and that delivery failure (not changed intent) is
why the December 31 plan from
[[2023-12-13-December-31st-Suicide-Day]] did not run.

The Nitrogen-Gas-Method was the preferred option of the late-2023
[[Method-Research]] period — preferred over the [[Glock]] on
pain-experience grounds, preferred over hanging on time-to-death
grounds. Specifics in the raw.
