# Father Wound

Neo's relation to his father. Lower-affect than [[Mother-Wound]]
because the belief that the father cared had collapsed long before
the 2023 corpus opens — the father is referenced as already-disowned.
Where the mother-wound has a dated collapse moment, the father-wound
is structural baseline.

## The standing position

> [!quote]
> "my piece of shit retarded 'dad' i hate calling him my dad by the
> way. I don't call him that. I never do. I mentally dont. He's not
> my dad. Fuck biology. Were not related, my mind says so. We are,
> wel since no shit, but i just don't."
> — [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]]

The refusal is consciously cognitive. Neo names it as a mental move
he makes against acknowledged biological reality. *"He's not my dad"*
is doctrine, not factual claim.

## The grievance set

- **Genetic inheritance.** Same as the mother-wound's height
  inheritance, but with the additional specific charge that the
  father is "such a short fuck, yet he still has such a short fuse"
  ([[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]]). The combination of bequeathed
  defects and active hostility.
- **Origin grievance.** The accident framing — Neo as collateral
  damage of his parents' young-adult sexual decisions.
  [[2023-12-13-December-31st-Suicide-Day]] develops this most
  thoroughly.
- **Active hostility.** Scolding patterns documented in
  [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]] (blaming gaming for missed schedules,
  even when Neo hadn't gamed for weeks). The father as ongoing
  antagonist, not just absent figure.
- **The age-comparison frame.** Neo positions his self-sufficiency
  against his parents' alleged dependency at the same age. The
  contempt is generational in form ([[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]]:
  *"in my age, they had the support of their parents and grandparents
  in college, getting drunk and having sex. me, no. im working"*).

## The independence project

Where the [[Mother-Wound]] expresses through rage, the Father-Wound
expresses through independence-as-spite. [[Long-Term-Plan]] is in
significant part organized around being financially and operationally
independent of the father — paying his own bills (and theirs); 10-year
career plan executed without parental scaffolding. *"i wanted to be
better… i wanted to be the parent"* ([[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]]).
