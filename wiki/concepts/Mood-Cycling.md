# Mood Cycling

Neo's named pattern of oscillation between elevated ("manic," "like a
god") and depressive states, often within days. Originally named in
[[2024-02-27-Recent]]; later expanded in
[[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]]'s years-as-emotional-states framing.
Concept name reflects Neo's preference for plain naming over clinical
("Manic-Depressive-Oscillation" was the clinical alternative — Neo
chose the tighter form).

## The pattern as named

[[2024-02-27-Recent]] is the first explicit framing:

> [!quote]
> "times when I felt like a god thanks to these frequent manic
> episodes I've been having where I feel like I'm on top of the world…
> feelings of despair and loneliness have surged and go plateau in
> more volatile intervals."
> — [[2024-02-27-Recent]]

The cycling is described as accelerating — "plateau in more volatile
intervals" — by early 2024. It correlates loosely with
external-context shocks (the [[Vice-Principal-Incident]] in April,
loss of computer privileges, AP exams) but persists between them.

## The years-as-emotional-states formulation

[[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]] extends the pattern to a yearly
scale, retroactively:

> [!quote]
> "The Sleepless Nights of 21, Confusion from 22, Insanity of 23,
> Desperation by 24 and the incoming Agony of 25."
> — [[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]] (Dec 25 2024 fragment)

Each year condensed to one emotional register. The framing fits
[[Self-Mythologizing]] — Neo composing his own emotional biography
in advance. The 2026 retrospective in [[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]]
breaks the projection: 2026 is not "Agony" but "Alone but successful."
The sequence stops at 2025.

## The 2026 reframe

[[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]] revises the read on the prior years.
The earlier emotional states are partially attributed to ideological
intake ("simply because I browsed too much 4chan") rather than
constitutional cycling. But Neo also acknowledges the cycling as
older than the ideology: *"sadness feels familiar and good to me."*
The cycling isn't dissolved; its causal story gets complicated.

## Distinction from Aesthetic-of-Melancholy

[[Aesthetic-of-Melancholy]] is the *relationship* Neo has to sadness
as a register. Mood Cycling is the *empirical pattern* that produces
the up-and-down inputs the aesthetic operates on. They're distinct;
both kept per Neo's call.
