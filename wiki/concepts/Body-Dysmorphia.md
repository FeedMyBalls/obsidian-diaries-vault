# Body Dysmorphia

Neo's sustained negative perception of his physical appearance. Not
just dissatisfaction — a structural distortion that includes
[[Mirror-Avoidance]], filtered-image-as-real perception,
genetic-fatalist framing, and active extension to family
("parents-as-extension" of his own appearance).

## The catalog

Across multiple 2023-2024 entries, Neo enumerates specific perceived
defects:

- **Height.** *"the shortest guy since kindergarten."* The most
  persistent and load-bearing item. Identified as inherited from both
  parents. Frames almost every social-romantic disqualification.
  Per [[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]]: *"Bone structure defines how
  people will think of you."*
- **Face.** Acne, "shitty nose," weak/heading-outwards mouth, "tired
  eyes with huge eyebags." [[Looksmaxxing]] vocabulary applied —
  canthal tilt assessment, jawline rating, neutral-vs-negative
  positioning. [[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]] includes
  the most detailed self-assessment.
- **Hair.** "Looks like I'm balding"; bangs unsuccessful most of the
  time.
- **Side profile.** Specifically named as the worst angle. Selfies
  always front-facing, always filtered.
- **Phenotype.** "horrendous side profile" framed as combinatorial —
  ugly face × short stature × inherited features = compounded.

## The dynamics

The dysmorphia operates several characteristic moves:

- **[[Mirror-Avoidance]].** Months without looking at his reflection
  ([[2024-04-22-My-Last-Prayer-Amen]]). Selfies hidden if taken;
  others' photos avoided.
- **Filter-as-real.** Selfies "heavily filtered" — and Neo notes
  *"I ended up molding my perception to think I'm the filtered
  image"* ([[2024-04-22-My-Last-Prayer-Amen]]). The dissociative
  element documented openly.
- **Mewing and self-correction.** [[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]]
  notes mewing since April 2022 (19+ months at writing). Squinting
  subconsciously as a looksmaxxing-adjacent micro-correction.
- **Parents-as-extension.** [[2026-03-17-High-School-Graduation]]
  documents avoiding being seen in photos with his parents at
  graduation specifically because their height makes the heredity
  visible. The dysmorphia extends to anyone whose adjacency would
  make Neo's body visible-as-inherited.

## Partial revision in 2026

[[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]] — the birthday entry — contains
the corpus's first sustained positive self-appraisal:

> [!quote]
> "I am much more physically attractive, thin to average body. Much
> shorter sure but straight back, either a lot of hair or a well kept
> hair when cut, shaven everyday, jawline is prominent."
> — [[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]]

Used comparatively (against [[Dave]]). [[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]]
revises further: *"I don't hate my appearance as much as before
surprisingly."* The dysmorphia partially metabolized after the
[[Anette]] relationship and the post-glow-up period; the height
assessment alone remains stable.

## Connection to the wider doctrine

Body-Dysmorphia is the somatic instance of [[Undeservingness]] — the
genetic-fatalist version of "I don't deserve." It interlocks with
[[Halo-Effect]] (Neo's cited mechanism for why looks gate
opportunities), [[Looksmaxxing]] (the corrective vocabulary), and
[[Blackpill]] (the ideology that systematizes the fatalism).
