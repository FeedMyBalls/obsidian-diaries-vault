# Self-Mythologizing

The pattern of writing about oneself as if drafting a Wikipedia entry —
treating the present-moment self as a character whose profile is being
documented in real time. The third-person register is its most visible
form. Distinct from [[Neo-Mythology]] (which is about the *name* as
self-fulfilling prophecy); Self-Mythologizing is the *act* of
biographing oneself from inside.

## The third-person pivot

A recurring move in 2026: Neo writes a first-person entry, then drops
into third person ("the child," "this child," "Neo," "he") to deliver
the entry's most pointed observations. The pivot is not literary
distance for safety; it's a rhetorical authority move — the third
person carries the weight of profile-writing.

Canonical sites:

- **[[2026-03-16-She-Loves-Me-She-Loves-Me-Not]]** — sustained
  third-person register for the entire middle section ("This child
  dreams of many, completes few"). Closes on punitive imaginative
  scene addressed to "him." The most extended use.
- **[[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]]** — closing paragraph drops
  to third person ("Cold and calm not as expressive…"), ending
  *"This is Neo."* Reads like signing a self-portrait.
- **[[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]]** (September 18 fragment) — the
  spectator-of-own-life passage. Watching himself succeed from
  outside himself; "I feel like a spectator of my own life... an
  impostor." Folded here per Neo's call as the *Spectator-Self* face
  of the same pattern.

## Annotating one's own archive

A second mode: Neo reads his prior writing and annotates it in present
voice with sardonic asides. [[2023-07-04-Comfort-And-Past-Diaries]] is
the earliest documented instance — Neo recovers his April 2023
post-breakup letters to [[G]] and writes interlinear commentary
mocking his earlier self ("yeah too fucking bad neo.. too fucking bad").
The relation is self-as-witness-and-tormentor.

## Why the move runs

The Self-Mythologizing register tracks confidence rather than crisis.
2023's caps-rage and crisis-spiral entries are first-person throughout.
The third-person move appears when Neo has enough distance to compose
rather than confess. It correlates with the [[Workaholism-as-Identity]]
maturation and the post-Anette reflective period — the years where
Neo can hold himself as object without dissociating from himself.

The closing line of [[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]] —
*"This is Neo."* — captures the function. The self-mythology is
declarative: Neo is composing the public-facing version of who he
is, in the same document where he's confessing the private version.
Both are him.
