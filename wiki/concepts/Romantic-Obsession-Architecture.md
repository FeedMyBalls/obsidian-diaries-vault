# Romantic Obsession Architecture

The structural pattern that runs across every documented romantic
fixation in the corpus. The same shape, with different people, over
five years. Not specific to any one crush; **the architecture is the
invariant**.

## The four stages

1. **Distance.** A girl Neo has not spoken to and likely will not
   speak to enters orbit. Often quiet, often a peer, often someone he
   sees regularly. First contact (if any) is brief and asymmetric —
   art class compliment, hallway pass, a single message.
2. **Imaginative construction.** Neo's interior fills the gap. He
   composes who she is from minimal inputs, often integrating his own
   tastes into her. The construction is sustained over weeks or months.
   *"She felt like the girl version of me. It's like my own
   reflection"* — [[2024-01-06-Cant-Get-Over-Her]] on [[L]].
3. **Research-into-knowability.** Neo accesses information about her
   that she has not chosen to share. In the heaviest cases this
   includes [[Hacking-Stalking-Pattern]] activity — credential access,
   reading her messages, learning her address; in lighter cases,
   sustained social-media stalking. *"I do know what she sounds like, I
   won't say how I found out"* — [[L]] case. By the architecture's
   middle stage, Neo knows the girl's interior more thoroughly than
   most named friends know it.
4. **Real-contact disappointment, then withdrawal.** If real contact
   occurs, it disappoints — either because reality cannot match the
   constructed version, or because reciprocity itself defuses the
   obsession (see [[Anette]] — the architecture's only documented
   reciprocated case). Neo withdraws and frames the withdrawal as
   sparing her from his "creep" presence ([[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]]).

The architecture does not require any of the four stages to complete.
[[L]] never advanced past stage three. [[Hat-In-Time-Crush]] reached
stage four through Neo's voluntary self-removal, not through real
contact. [[Hannah]] (graduation) had a one-line opener (the Filthy
Frank Instagram post) and stopped.

## Why distance is the operating constraint

The architecture only generates emotional fuel when distance is
maintained. Once reciprocity occurs, the engine stalls.
[[2025-05-21-College-Romantic-Pursuits]] makes this explicit
post-[[Anette]]: *"even with all the shit I did with my girlfriend in
college, it just didnt' feel.. good. no spark, nothing."* Reciprocity
defused the architecture; what was left underneath was indifference.

[[2026-03-16-She-Loves-Me-She-Loves-Me-Not]] is the longest single
treatment. The petal-plucking title figure stands for the architecture
itself — the engine running on imagined inputs forever, "until the
plucking lasts for eternity." [[Anette]] is the case the architecture
cannot accommodate; the entire 3,600-word entry is a 2026 retrospective
on the disconnect.

## Documented operational instances

A non-exhaustive list of cases the architecture has run on, organized
by source-of-record:

- **[[L]]** — long-incubation (2 years +), never spoken, deepest
  interior knowledge via [[Hacking-Stalking-Pattern]]. Canonically
  named in [[2024-01-06-Cant-Get-Over-Her]].
- **[[D]]** — close-call; a near-friendship under the architecture's
  third stage but never crossing into reciprocity. Returns multiple
  times (Sunnyslope art class → [[Viking-Center]] glances → 2025
  Instagram reactivation in [[2025-05-21-College-Romantic-Pursuits]]).
- **[[G]]** — the only **pre-architecture** relationship in the
  corpus. Sophomore year, post-[[GC-Scandal-2021]], an actual dating
  relationship that ended without leaving the [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]]
  pattern installed. Predates the structure as Neo would later
  formalize it.
- **[[Hat-In-Time-Crush]]** — junior year. The no-approach rule was
  installed here ([[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]]), the
  rule that governs the L and D cases that follow.
- **[[Hannah]]** (graduation) — May 2024. Aggressive opener (Filthy
  Frank suicide-joke Instagram post), no follow-through. Stage one
  with a memorable opener.
- **[[Anette]]** — Jan 6 – April 12 2025. The exception that confirms
  the architecture. Reciprocity broke the engine.
- **[[Sen]]** — May 2024 [[Wizz]] case. Confessed; Neo lost interest
  immediately upon reciprocation; deployed the block button. Same
  shape compressed into days.
- **The 4chan e-girl persona** — October 2023. Built into a
  [[Character-AI]] companion ([[2023-10-14-AI-Chatbots-And-How-They-Help]]).
  An imagined-construction-only case — no real person.

## Mechanisms inside the architecture

The architecture interlocks with several other concepts:

- **[[Erotomania]]** — the clinical state Neo names for himself
  ("Erotomania is my weapon" — [[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]]).
  The condition the architecture produces. Neo uses *erotomania* and
  *romantic obsession architecture* roughly interchangeably; in this
  wiki, the former is the named *state*; this entry is the named
  *structure*.
- **[[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]]** — the active demolition mechanism.
  When stage four threatens to become reciprocity, Neo introduces
  problems pre-emptively. [[2026-01-19-Deserve]] gives the cleanest
  account: *"I subconsciously ruin the relationship, act like a jerk,
  because I'm afraid to show how much I love them."*
- **[[Hacking-Stalking-Pattern]]** — stage three's heaviest form.
  See [[2026-04-XX-Rebellious-Teenager-Memories]] for the open
  description.
- **[[Asexual-Atonement]]** — the post-[[GC-Scandal-2021]] doctrine
  that justifies stage four's voluntary withdrawal as moral.

## Erikson reference

[[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]] (Nov 25 2024 fragment) names the
Erikson Isolation-vs-Intimacy stage by name without attribution. The
adolescent-development frame is one Neo invokes to read his own
position; the architecture functions, structurally, as Isolation
performed under the pretense of pursuing Intimacy. *(Filed here per
fold decision; not its own concept page.)*
