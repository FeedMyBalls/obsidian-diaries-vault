# Blackpill Vocabulary

The lexical set Neo deploys in his blackpill-and-/pol/-adjacent
political register. Renamed from "/pol/-Vocabulary" per Neo's call.
Filed as concept rather than just style note because the vocabulary
is functional — it organizes reasoning, not just decoration.

## The lexicon

- **Glowie** — federal/intelligence agent, especially online; broadly
  any state-actor presumed to manipulate. Used in
  [[2023-12-29-Psyop-And-Glowies]] as accusation against the
  TikTok-recruitment specialist.
- **Psyop** — psychological operation; broadly any communication
  Neo reads as manipulative-by-design. Title of
  [[2023-12-29-Psyop-And-Glowies]].
- **Libtard / faggotry** — pejorative shorthand for progressive
  positions, deployed throughout
  [[2024-07-04-Palestine-And-Israel]]. Used as terminus of argument
  rather than provisional descriptor.
- **Chad** — high-status male; used in [[2024-04-25-Copium]] as
  ironic frame for [[Alec]]'s discussion ("how he should be a Chad
  and just spread his seed everywhere").
- **Manlet** — short male; self-applied in
  [[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]]. Looksmaxxing-adjacent.
- **Halo effect** — see [[Halo-Effect]] (proper concept page).
- **Canthal tilt** — see [[Looksmaxxing]].
- **JPAC / "the Jew"** — the antisemitic conspiracy nucleus of the
  [[2024-07-04-Palestine-And-Israel]] essay. The most ideologically
  charged element of the lexicon.

## Function

The vocabulary doesn't decorate; it does work. It compresses
arguments — *"glowie psyop"* is a four-word claim that the speaker
is government-affiliated, manipulative-by-design, and discreditable.
The compression is part of why the vocabulary persists across
contexts: blackpill, /pol/-political, and dating-market arguments
all run on the same compressed claim-shapes.

## 2026 residue

[[2026-03-16-She-Loves-Me-She-Loves-Me-Not]]'s passing reference to
"the Iran War going on" is delivered without the vocabulary engaged
("I could care less, other than the gas prices purging through my
wallet"). The political register has fully attenuated by 2026; the
lexicon's residue is occasional rather than structural.
