# Doomscrolling Period

The bounded period (April – mid-2024) when Neo's primary input
device became his iPhone after the [[Computer-Loss-2024]] following
the [[Vice-Principal-Incident]]. TikTok, Instagram Reels, Twitter,
4chan/r9k, /pol/-adjacent content. The period concentrates the
political-radicalization register that produces
[[2024-07-04-Palestine-And-Israel]], the [[Wizz]] introduction, and
the deepest [[Method-Research]] of 2024.

## The precipitator

> [!quote]
> "Sucked into Instagram Reels, and this is my new life now. Just
> doom scrolling. I was so optimistic, so ready for a new chapter
> to grind and prepare for the AP Exams, but just one minor bad
> thing can lead to such out of proportion consequences."
> — [[2024-04-25-Copium]]

[[Computer-Loss-2024]] is the material precipitator: parents
revoked computer privileges after the [[Vice-Principal-Incident]].
Without the desktop, Neo lost video-editing, gaming, and structured
creative outlets. The phone became the sole device. The phone's
content economy is doomscrolling-shaped.

## What Neo consumed

- **Reels and TikTok blackpill content.** *"Even more depressed than
  ever, and I miss that sudden spike of joy."*
- **/r9k/ 4chan revisited.** Neo names this as the "incel phase"
  return — material he had partially exited before April 2024.
- **The "save Europe" TikTok aesthetic.** Named in
  [[2025-01-01-Buried-Old-Stories]]: *"I turned my fyp into a mosh
  pit of whatever resembled anything I found in that forum.
  Nightcore, hyperborean, blackpill content."*
- **Wizz.** [[2024-05-08-Desperation]]: the dating-app tier of the
  doomscrolling — same swipe-mechanics, romantic-ish target.

## What it produced

- **[[Blackpill]] re-deepening.** The 2023 commitment to blackpill
  ideology was already named ([[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]]); the
  Doomscrolling Period reinforced it materially. Algorithms feed
  what Neo half-consciously sought.
- **[[2024-07-04-Palestine-And-Israel]].** The political-essay
  register that emerges in summer 2024 has its register-source
  here. The vocabulary — *libtards, faggotry, glowies, JPAC* — is
  the same material the FYP fed.
- **Method drift.** Fentanyl researched via TOR documented in
  [[2024-04-22-My-Last-Prayer-Amen]] and continued in
  [[2024-04-25-Copium]] runs alongside the doomscrolling. Whether
  the platform exposure intensified the method drift or merely
  coincided with it is not establishable from the corpus.

## The 2026 retrospective

[[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]] revises the period: *"All that
depression, that pain and sadness I harbored for years was all for
nothing, simply because I browsed too much 4chan."* The Doomscrolling
Period named retrospectively as the cause Neo earlier treated as
constitutional. The revision doesn't fully take ([[Aesthetic-of-Melancholy]]
is older), but the doomscrolling is acknowledged as having
intensified beyond what the underlying disposition would have
produced alone.

## Bounded duration

The period ends, structurally, when Neo gets his first job
([[2024-07-22-First-Day-Of-Work]]) and the schedule begins
displacing screen time. By 2025 [[Wizz]] has been deleted; by 2026
[[Intentional-Isolation]] has formally severed the FYP feed.
