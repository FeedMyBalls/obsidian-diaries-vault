# Mother Wound

Neo's relation to his mother. Distinct from [[Father-Wound]] in that
the mother was the last-held belief Neo had in parental support — and
the collapse of that belief in November 2023 generates the corpus's
deepest single-entry crisis. The 2024 [[2024-02-28-Anger]] entry holds
the corpus's most uncompressed expression of rage toward her.

## The collapse moment

> [!quote]
> "I've lost overall faith for my dad a long time ago, I know he doesn't
> care about me in the tiniest bit. But I still had hopes and clinged
> unto the fact that my mom still probably supported me even when I'm
> mentally struggling, but I'm wrong... at that very moment when I knew
> that I was living for no one, if none of my parents actually wanted
> to support or help me, and especially a person who I formerly thought
> still clung unto aiding me — my mom — just to realize that she also
> doesn't care, I felt at that very moment my heart dropped."
> — [[2023-11-18-Meaningless]]

The triggering event: a panic attack during which Neo's mother
"demeaned" him rather than helped. The belief collapse is named as
discrete and dated. Everything earlier 2023 entries point toward
condenses into this realization.

## The rage register

[[2024-02-28-Anger]] is the foundational rage document.
"Hours-later" continuation after a fight where Neo punched walls.
The mother had asked whether he should "seek therapy" — a question
Neo read as territorial overreach and registered with sustained
sexualized contempt and explicit violent ideation (specific images
of stabbing and shooting her, filed for the record).

The single line that captures the mechanism: *"Youre lucky you have
a child, so even I can't shout at you to call you a stupid fucking
bitch."* The [[Baby-Brother]]'s presence is the speed limiter on the
rage. The fantasy is unbounded; the action is bounded by Neo's love
for someone who shares the same household.

## The standing complaint set

Across years, the grievances Neo names:

- **Inattention to his suffering.** "She doesn't even acknowledge my
  suffering" ([[2024-02-28-Anger]]).
- **Genetic inheritance.** Specifically her shortness, which combined
  with the father's shortness produced Neo's own
  ([[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]]).
- **The "stalking" during the [[Anette]] period.** Going to school
  while Neo was parked, locking him out at night
  ([[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]]).
- **Forced compliance.** Sunday church, family obligations, the
  performance of nice-son when Neo "despises every living second"
  ([[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]]).
- **Origin grievance.** [[2023-12-13-December-31st-Suicide-Day]]'s
  parents-as-accident framing.

## The 2026 numbed register

[[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]] revises the affect without revising the
content:

> [!quote]
> "Well, I hate them when I think about it, but on normal days, I
> don't feel it, I only realize how much I hate them when I think
> about my past, and it fuels me with rage inside, but when I'm at
> home, I'm numbed out."
> — [[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]]

The 2024 rage has not been processed; it has been compartmentalized.
The mother-wound persists as cold disposition rather than active
grievance, surfacing only when retrospection requires it.
