# Social Worker

The institutional counselor / social-worker role at
[[Sunnyslope-High-School]] (or possibly Neo's prior school for the
2023 first-session). Filed as a concept rather than entity per Neo's
call — the institutional role matters more than the specific
person, and the role itself functions as a recurring failure-of-
support pattern in the corpus.

## Documented sessions

- **October 2023.** Triggered by the [[Suicidal-Drawing-Incident]]
  — Neo's drawing of a Ronnie McNutt-style scene was discovered
  by his honors physics teacher 27 days after he made it. First
  documented session. [[2023-10-10-Whats-My-Goal-Now]] reports the
  session in low-affect logistical register: the social worker
  mentions Neo as "that quiet kid in class," Neo registers the
  framing without engagement.
- **April 2024.** Following the [[Vice-Principal-Incident]]. Same
  social worker. Neo's framing in [[2024-04-22-My-Last-Prayer-Amen]]:
  *"talked to my useless social worker, no advice worth taking. The
  way she'd just yawn, it's as if she just wanted to go home. I've
  grown more suicidal since the last time i saw her back in October.
  I've been more ill since then."* The session is registered as
  failure-of-support.

## Why the role recurs

The Social Worker is the institutional answer Neo's school produces
to his documented mental-health states. Neo's framing is
consistent across both sessions: the role was offered, the role
was inadequate, the role's inadequacy is one input to Neo's
[[Method-Research]] continuing.

> [!info]
> Neo also engaged with **Character-AI psychiatrist personas** as a
> functional alternative to the Social Worker, per
> [[2023-10-14-AI-Chatbots-And-How-They-Help]] — explicitly framed
> as more useful than the institutional version despite known AI
> limitations. The substitution is filed under [[AI-Companionship]].

## Position in the corpus

The Social Worker functions structurally as the institutional
register's failure-state. The role's recurring inadequacy supports
Neo's broader [[Living-Contradiction]] frame — the world is
arranged to provide nominal support that doesn't function, which
Neo registers as evidence the [[Blackpill]] doctrine generally
holds. Whether the social worker was actually inadequate or whether
Neo's framing-as-inadequate is itself part of the
[[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]] mechanism extended to therapeutic
relationships is an open question.
