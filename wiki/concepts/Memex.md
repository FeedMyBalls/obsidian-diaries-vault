# Memex

A 1945 concept by [[Vannevar-Bush]] for a personal, curated knowledge
store with associative trails between documents. Introduced in his
Atlantic essay *"As We May Think."*

The Memex was envisioned as a private, actively curated repository where
the **connections between documents** were as valuable as the documents
themselves. Bush's vision was closer to a personal wiki than to what the
web became — private rather than public, actively curated rather than
passively crawled, with explicit associative trails rather than emergent
hyperlinks.

Per [[LLM-Wiki-Pattern]], the unsolved part of Bush's vision was always
the **maintenance question**: who maintains the trails, who keeps
connections current, who notices when one document contradicts another?
Humans don't, at scale. LLMs do. The LLM Wiki pattern is positioned as
the resolution to that missing piece.

> [!info]
> The Memex was a hypothetical desk-sized device using microfilm — never
> built. Its influence on hypertext, Engelbart, and Nelson is
> well-documented but tangential to this wiki's interest, which is the
> curation-plus-linking idea itself. *(inference)*
