# Workaholism as Identity

Sustained over-employment as the operating answer to existential
emptiness. Origin point: [[2024-07-22-First-Day-Of-Work]] (EVS at
Mayo Clinic). Mature form: [[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]]'s
"Alone but successful" thesis. The shape Neo's adult life takes once
[[Method-Research]] subsides — work as the substitute for the meaning
the [[Suicide-Methods]] register was supposed to provide.

## Origin frame

> [!quote]
> "I dont care about money. I care about being assigned something to
> help for the greater good."
> — [[2024-07-22-First-Day-Of-Work]]

The first-day position is already the [[Savior-Complex]] mapped onto
labor. Money is acknowledged as instrumental; purpose is the actual
target. The work is the operationalization of meaning that 2023 had
sought through doctrine.

## The 2024 vs. 2026 evaluation reversal

The structural inversion is one of the corpus's most striking
year-over-year contrasts:

- **[[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]]** documents the same load
  ([[PVCC]] full-time, [[Mayo-Clinic]] night shifts, [[Trading-Addiction]],
  no sleep) and reads it as collapse. The keyboard breakdown
  passage. *"I'm purposeless. Empty. A shell."*
- **[[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]]** documents an even more
  taxing schedule (anesthesia tech 8pm-4:30am five nights, ASU labs
  early morning, Wednesday off, babysitting in afternoons, "30
  minutes of sleep" before lab) and reads it as success. *"Alone but
  successful."* The closing thesis.

Same load. Opposite evaluation. The mood floor is comparable; what
changed is the framing. By 2026 Neo has a structure for the work.

## The components Neo uses

- **The schedule as identity.** Detailed schedule documentation
  becomes its own genre. [[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]] is
  most exhaustive — every shift, every shuttle, every nap window.
  The schedule's *legibility* is part of the satisfaction. Neo can
  produce the document; therefore Neo exists.
- **Perfectionism + System-Gaming.** Neo runs a strict perfectionist
  register on stocking work alongside loophole-finding patterns
  ([[System-Gaming]]). The two coexist. The stocking precision
  proves competence; the loopholes prove intelligence.
- **Subordinate-as-leader.** Neo "takes the lead" despite [[Dave]]
  being the senior worker. Status in the role, not from the role.
- **Money as proof, not goal.** Neo names $3.2k/month, savings,
  paid tuition with neither pleasure nor anxiety. The numbers are
  evidence the schedule is doing what it's supposed to.

## The cost named in 2026

[[2026-04-16-Tender-Moment]] is the necessary counterweight. The
schedule succeeds at the level the [[2026-03-14]] entry evaluates;
fails at the level the [[2026-04-16]] entry surfaces. Neo names the
cost: *"this form of masochism helps me, it helps me cope with the
feeling that I'm no different from others if not worse… the more I
give to a world that I owe to, the more I feel at ease."* The
workaholism is partly compensatory — service to a society Neo feels
he must repay. The [[Savior-Complex]] economics, internalized.
