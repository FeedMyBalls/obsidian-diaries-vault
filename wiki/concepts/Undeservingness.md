# Undeservingness

The doctrine that Neo does not deserve happiness, love, or attention.
Treated by Neo himself as an early-childhood decision adapted into a
stable operating frame. Distinct from situational self-deprecation;
this is the structural premise from which the
[[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]], [[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]], and
[[Savior-Complex]] all draw.

## The doctrinal statement

> [!quote]
> "Since I was younger, things never went the way I wanted it to, so
> I decided that I just don't deserve happiness. I accepted my
> insignificance; however, as a consequence, when things go well for
> me, I self-sabotage and get destructive."
> — [[2026-01-19-Deserve]]

The decision is framed as adaptive: accepting non-deserving is a
defense against disappointment. The cost is documented in the same
sentence — when good things arrive, the doctrine disqualifies them
and they are demolished.

## How it propagates

The doctrine spreads across three domains in
[[2026-01-19-Deserve]]'s structure:

- **Objects.** Neo as a child wouldn't pick gifts because he didn't
  feel things were meant for him. Parents had to force him to choose.
- **People (friendship).** Conversations savored, then poisoned by
  guilt watching the other enjoy talking to others more. The
  [[Living-Contradiction]] of social-skill development: each
  improvement is read as evidence of inhumanity rather than progress.
- **Love.** Every relationship undone by the inability to accept
  being chosen. *"Why me, why not someone else?"*

## The Schopenhauer inversion

[[2026-01-19-Deserve]] cites Schopenhauer's effort-equals-happiness
formulation and inverts it. Where Schopenhauer says happiness lies
in the effort, Neo argues effort itself is evidence of his deformity:
*"the very effort I exerted is just evidence of my inhumanity when
it comes naturally to others."* If Schopenhauer is right, Neo is
double-disqualified. The doctrine survives even the philosophy that
should resolve it.

## Earlier manifestations

The 2026 statement is the cleanest articulation, but the doctrine
runs across all years:

- [[2023-03-25-Death]] — *"Im a leech. A mere sight of her only a
  second or a fraction of that is enough to make my day."*
  Doctrine in pre-articulate form.
- [[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]] — the bone-structure-defines-treatment
  formulation. Genetic disqualification.
- All [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]] cases — each one filtered
  through the doctrine; reciprocity disqualified by the partner's
  presumed lapse in judgment ("Why someone so imperfect, ugly, short
  and stupid").
