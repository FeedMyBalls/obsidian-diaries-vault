# Valentine's Day 2024

A named pressure-point in the 2024 calendar. Referenced in
[[2024-02-27-Recent]] as a window Neo had to navigate — *"the days
leading up to valentines day were hell, and I just wanted to
disappear, but after that I felt very relaxed."* The structure is
holiday-dread / post-holiday-relief.

## Why it gets a page

The reference returns. [[2024-02-29-Leap]]'s mid-sentence trail-off
attempts to circle back to the topic ("i didn't really talk about
what happened to the days leading up to valentines. It was —")
and fails to continue. Two of the 12 2024 entries reference the
period; one of them is the corpus's shortest entry, breaking off
at exactly that reference. That brevity-by-association is itself
data.

## Neo's relation to the holiday

The day functions as the visible-form of every absence Neo's
[[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]] organizes around. While peers
"were making out," Neo *"was just playing my favorite game which
was way better :)."* The smiley does work — the
[[Aesthetic-of-Melancholy]] register Neo will later name explicitly
operates here in compressed form.
