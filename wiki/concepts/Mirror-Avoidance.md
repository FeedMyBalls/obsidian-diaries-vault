# Mirror Avoidance

A documented sub-pattern of [[Body-Dysmorphia]]: Neo stops looking
at his reflection. Filed as its own concept because the
filtered-image-as-real perception that follows is structurally
distinct from generalized body-dysmorphia and operates as an active
dissociative dynamic Neo names without flinching.

> [!quote]
> "No one knows this but i haven't looked at my reflection for
> months. Everytime i see my self in a mirror i look away. if someone
> takes a picture, i hide my face. My 'selfies' are heavily filtered.
> I ended up molding my perception to think I'm the filtered image."
> — [[2024-04-22-My-Last-Prayer-Amen]]

## The mechanism

The avoidance is not passive. Neo actively averts gaze in mirrors,
hides face when others photograph, and uses heavy filtering on
self-photos. Over time the filtered version supplants the unfiltered
version as Neo's interior reference point. The dissociation is
specific: he has *a* face, but it isn't the face that exists in the
world.

## Why it matters as its own concept

The page captures something distinct from [[Body-Dysmorphia]]
generally:

- **The corrective is unavailable.** With a body issue Neo can name,
  there's at least the option to test it. With mirror-avoidance, the
  reality-check itself is refused. The dysmorphia becomes
  un-falsifiable from inside.
- **The filtered image is dissociative, not aspirational.** Neo isn't
  treating the filter as goal; he's treating it as fact. The relation
  is closer to depersonalization than to body-improvement effort.
- **Documented period only.** The mirror-avoidance is dated
  ("for months") around the 2024 [[Vice-Principal-Incident]] period.
  The 2026 partial-revision in [[Body-Dysmorphia]] doesn't address
  whether mirror-avoidance also lifted. *(Open: ask Neo whether
  this still holds.)*
