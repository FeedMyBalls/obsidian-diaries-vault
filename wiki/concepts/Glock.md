# Glock

A 9mm handgun present in Neo's home in late 2023. Identified in
[[2023-11-18-Meaningless]] as the available [[Method-Research]]
option. Per Neo's standing decision, no technical specifics
(caliber discussion, accessibility detail, comparative ballistics)
are reproduced in the wiki; the raw entries retain the original
text.

## The page's only function

To name that a firearm was identified in-home as a method, and
that this is documented across three late-2023 entries. The
specifics belong with the raw.

The Glock was rejected in favor of the [[Nitrogen-Gas-Method]] in
[[2023-12-13-December-31st-Suicide-Day]] on pain-experience grounds.
The Nitrogen tank failed to arrive; the Jan 1 attempt
([[2024-01-01-Failed-Suicide-Attempt-Hanging]]) substituted a
different method again. The Glock was not deployed.
