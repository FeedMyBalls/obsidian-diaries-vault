# GC-Scandal-2021

**November 8, 2021.** Sophomore year. The day Neo was called from class
to the [[Sunnyslope-High-School]] (then his prior school) office and
shown printed copies of his group-chat messages. The event organizes
half the corpus's later self-narrative. Foundational origin point for
[[Living-Contradiction]] (saint-outside / monster-inside as exposed
rather than structural at the time), [[Asexual-Atonement]], and the
self-narrative as creep that runs through 2023.

> [!info]
> Per Neo's standing decision, this page reflects the felt truth of
> [[2023-06-10-Im-A-Creep-No-Seriously]] (the foundational document),
> not a corrected version. The K-attribution and the moral arc are
> reproduced as Neo holds them.

## The event

Sophomore year. Neo had been the leader of a group chat where the
content escalated over months from typical adolescent crudity into
photoshopping classmates and teachers in sexual scenarios, jokes about
rape, sustained sexualized contempt for specific named girls and
faculty. Outside the GC, Neo was reading as the "innocent short cute
fella'" — the praised video editor of class projects. Inside it, by
his own characterization, "an absolute monster."

On November 8, 2021, Neo was called to the office in the morning,
shortly after a praised video presentation. The interview opened with
the question *"What's your definition of Respect..."* Silence. The
faculty then produced printed copies of the chat — paper-form, his
messages and his friends' messages. The full content was visible to
faculty.

Subsequently, faculty informed the affected students and other faculty
members. The information spread school-wide. Specific girls and
teachers Neo's chat had targeted were now witnesses to material that
had been private until that morning.

## Aftermath (year-of)

- Neo grew depressed in November.
- Considered transferring schools; declined because the alternative
  was worse. Stayed at the original school through the rest of
  sophomore year.
- Recovery arc partial: by prom, "things were starting to heal,"
  Neo had begun dating [[G]] (his first relationship), faculty had
  begun reintegrating him.
- Junior year: transferred to [[Sunnyslope-High-School]]. The
  school-switch is framed in Neo's writing as identity reset.

## The K-attribution

This is where Neo's own framing must be reproduced as held, not
corrected. Per [[2023-06-10-Im-A-Creep-No-Seriously]]:

[[K]] was a girl from the affected group. Neo's account: K's post-
scandal sadness drove her toward "the wrong boyfriend"; the boyfriend
got drunk at prom and assaulted her at a restroom. Neo traces the
chain back through the GC to himself.

The chain Neo holds: GC → K's depression → bad partner choice →
assault. Neo writes: *"She was raped because of me. Imagine sitting
in her position, having to endure such a traumatic event."*

## What it set up

The event becomes the explanatory engine for almost every later
self-narrative:

- **[[Asexual-Atonement]] (July 2023).** [[2023-07-09-Women-Live-In-A-Mans-World]]
  responds with the strategic redirect: lay sexual frustration on AI
  bots; avoid eye contact with girls; accept being read as asexual or
  weird; prevent any new harm. The doctrine carries through 2024 and
  is partially named again in [[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]].
- **The "creep" self-narrative.** [[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]]
  formalizes it as operational rule: do not approach, withdraw if
  noticed, atone via distance.
- **[[Savior-Complex]] as compensation.** [[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]]
  explicitly names the arc shift from "narcissism" to "inferiority/
  savior complex" — the inversion is staged from this event.
- **[[Living-Contradiction]] as the underlying engine.** The GC
  exposed that Neo had two simultaneous selves; the saint/monster
  duality stops being a hidden architecture and starts being a known
  one Neo lives inside.

## The 2026 retrospective view

[[2026-04-XX-Rebellious-Teenager-Memories]] (chat export) reframes the
GC-era from a five-year distance. Neo names the same teenage period
not via the GC alone but as a continuum that includes the
[[Hacking-Stalking-Pattern]], [[Black-Market-Selling]], and
[[System-Gaming]]. The line: *"i was a huge asshole half angel."*

The GC isn't denied or revised in 2026, but it sits inside a wider
"intelligent rebel" frame Neo accepts. The atonement structures it
generated remain in operation; the original guilt has metabolized
without dissolving.
