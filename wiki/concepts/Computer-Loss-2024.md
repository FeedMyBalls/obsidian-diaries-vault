# Computer Loss 2024

The material consequence of the [[Vice-Principal-Incident]]: Neo's
desktop computer was confiscated by his parents. Treated as a
distinct event — separate from the Incident itself — because its
consequences are different in shape and operate over a longer
timeframe.

## What was lost

- **Video editing.** The [[Youtube-Channel]] arc paused. The
  Pantheon V video momentum from late 2023 dissipated in this
  period.
- **Gaming as outlet.** [[ULTRAKILL]], Hollow Knight, structured
  creative play.
- **Code projects and creative work.** Neo notes in the chat
  export ([[2026-04-XX-Rebellious-Teenager-Memories]]) that the
  hacking-empire era was largely past by senior year, but
  ordinary technical work was the desktop's domain.
- **Privacy.** Phone-only computing meant parents could more
  easily monitor screen time.

## What replaced it

- **iPhone SE (jailbroken).** The single available device.
- **TikTok reinstalled** (after 18-month abstinence).
- **Instagram Reels** as primary attentional environment.
- **Twitter, 4chan/r9k** in fragments throughout the day.

This is the phone-only attentional environment that constitutes the
[[Doomscrolling-Period]].

## The duration

Neo doesn't document a return-of-computer event in 2024. By
[[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]] he has a laptop ("the same thing I'm
typing on right now") and is using it for trading and for diary
entry. By 2025-2026 the computer is operational. Whether the
revocation lasted weeks or months is not establishable from the
corpus; the period was "months" by Neo's framing in the April-May
entries.

## Why it deserves its own page

Separate from the [[Vice-Principal-Incident]] because the *event*
was the office meeting; the *condition* was the device deprivation.
The condition produces effects (algorithmic content exposure,
[[Method-Research]] migration to TOR-acquirable substances,
[[Wizz]] introduction) that the event itself doesn't directly
produce. The cascade is mediated through the device-loss state.
