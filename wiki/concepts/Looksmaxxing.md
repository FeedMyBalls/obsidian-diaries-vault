# Looksmaxxing

The self-assessment-and-improvement vocabulary Neo deploys for
[[Body-Dysmorphia]] across 2023-2024. Originates in incel-adjacent
online spaces; comprises both diagnostic terms (canthal tilt,
manlet) and corrective practices (mewing, jaw exercises). Distinct
from [[Body-Dysmorphia]] in that this is the *vocabulary and
methodology*; Body-Dysmorphia is the underlying experience.

## The diagnostic vocabulary

- **Canthal tilt** — the angle of the eye's outer corner relative to
  the inner. Positive (upward) read as attractive; neutral as
  normal; negative (downward) as ugly. Neo's self-assessment in
  [[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]]: probably neutral,
  possibly negative. *"if I do, i'd list it as one of the reasons
  for killing myself, but not yet."*
- **Manlet** — short male. Self-applied across multiple entries.
- **Side profile.** Not strictly looksmaxxing terminology but treated
  with the same diagnostic weight: rated as the worst angle, hidden
  in selfies.
- **Halo effect.** See [[Halo-Effect]] for full treatment.

## The corrective practices

- **Mewing** — tongue-against-palate posture practice, claimed to
  reshape jawline. Neo started April 2022, sustained ~19 months by
  the [[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]] entry. *"idk if
  it has helped in anything though."*
- **Subconscious squinting.** Neo notes squinting may be a
  looksmaxxing-adjacent micro-correction; observes himself doing it
  but doesn't deliberately practice.

## The framing the vocabulary enforces

The vocabulary makes appearance feel measurable, hierarchical, and
fixed. The diagnostic terms imply objective evaluation; the
corrective practices imply marginal improvability without
disturbing the fundamental ranking. The combination is fatalist —
you can mew, but you can't change your bone structure. Neo names
this directly: *"Bone structure defines how people will think of
you."* ([[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]])

## 2026 residue

The vocabulary is largely absent from 2026 entries. The
[[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]] self-assessment is in plain
language — "thin to average body," "jawline is prominent," "decent
nose for an asian young adult" — not looksmaxxing register. The
vocabulary has dropped without the underlying concerns dropping
entirely (height remains stable as a complaint).
