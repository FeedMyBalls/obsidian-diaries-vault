# Inaction and Regret

> One month post-[[Anette]] breakup (April 12, 2025). 2,000-word
> philosophical entry. Anette unnamed throughout; "she" carries the
> weight. Neo's processing is structured into mini-essays —
> Inaction, Regret, Care, Apathy, Empathy-as-Defect — and each is
> Neo arguing himself toward a settled position. The settled
> position keeps changing. Per Neo's voice-balance call, this page
> reflects 2025-Neo's read straight; the [[Anette]] entity page will
> hold the 2025 vs. 2026 contradiction.

## Summary

Opening declarative: "Upon entering my 19th year, I stoop lower to
misery." Compares his current state to his 18th year saved files
and concludes "We have come full circle." The cyclical-stasis
framing (which 2026 will partially revise) is fully online here.

The body is structured into themed sections. Each one is Neo
working out his position by writing it.

**On ambition.** "Ambition is a disaster that will leave you in
ruins." Argues for staying in one's "baseline." A retreat-from-
[[Long-Term-Plan]] register, written immediately post-breakup. The
position will not last (cf. [[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]]'s
opposite stance: "I'm pretty successful for my age").

**On potential.** "The word Potential. It annoys me." Reads
"potential" as a violation of conservation-of-energy applied to
human capacity. *"You can't have more than the amount of energy
given."* Neo treats himself as already at full output and
"potential" as an externally-imposed pressure to do impossible
work.

**On care and apathy.** *"To Care / To Get Hurt, is the act of
Caring. Without it, the reciprocal occurs… Nothing does."* The
post-breakup logic: caring caused this pain; therefore stop
caring. The argument is structured as if it's epistemic — about
what's true — but the function is anesthetic. Whether Neo
believes it is a separate question from whether the writing
helps.

**On empathy as defect.** *"Empathy is a defect."* Neo argues a
trans-humanist evolutionary frame — humans are "in that border"
between animal and machine, and intellect is the direction up.
Compassion read as evolutionary baggage to be left behind. This
is the one passage of the entry that goes furthest from Neo's
stable values (which throughout the corpus are unmistakably
empathic — see [[2023-07-09-Women-Live-In-A-Mans-World]] and
[[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]]). The "Empathy is a defect"
position is the position Neo argues *against himself*; the rest
of the corpus is the negation.

The closing collapses the structure. *"I'd even argue she was my
only form of optimism, but of course, she left me just like that,
because she felt so, and I have to accept that."* Anette unnamed
but addressed. *"I could feel anger, but all I could feel is
apathy."* The anesthetic working.

> [!info]
> The entry's argumentative form is the texture worth preserving.
> Neo doesn't write dismissive prose post-breakup — he writes
> philosophical prose post-breakup. The systematicity of the
> dismissal is the symptom.
