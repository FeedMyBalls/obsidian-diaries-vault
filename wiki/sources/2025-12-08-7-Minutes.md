# 7 Minutes

> Late 2025. The most lyrical-philosophical entry in the corpus
> after the [[2025-05-12-Inaction-And-Regret]] post-breakup
> meditation. Sustained tender-elegiac register — closer to prose
> poem than diary. Pulled twice into the corpus (the duplicate
> file `facets.txt` was dropped per Neo's call). Marks the December
> 2025 mood-floor; six months after [[2025-06-21-Everythings-A-Waste]]
> and three months before the [[2026-03-14-March-14-2026-19-Years]]
> structured-recovery entry.

## Summary

The conceit: the seven minutes of perceived consciousness sometimes
reported between cardiac arrest and brain death. Neo imagines this
window from the inside — a green-valley pastoral, lying flat,
"thoughtless and without worries of a future that will come." The
imagery returns from earlier tender registers (cf.
[[2023-07-04-Comfort-And-Past-Diaries]]'s "Their dainty little
hands run through your hair") but redirected from a partner's
embrace to nature's. Death as the most reliable beloved.

The middle section pivots to time-perception philosophy. If time
is cerebral, removing the cerebrum removes time; the seven minutes
might be subjectively eternal. Heaven and hell collapse into a
question of consciousness duration. The thinking is genuine;
Neo's reading and his suicidal contemplation are continuous, as
they have been throughout the corpus.

The closing miniature — *"Logic and Feeling. Two facets of human
cognition, so limited when compared to the vastness of the
universe."* — reads as a thesis-statement for an essay Neo will
not write. The entry stops there. (The dropped duplicate had the
same closing.)

What's notable: this is the highest-craft writing in 2025 and one
of the calmest crisis-adjacent entries in the corpus. The death
contemplation is present, but the violence isn't. Method has
disappeared — neither named nor implied. The 2024 [[Method-Research]]
register is fully absent. The [[Aesthetic-of-Melancholy]] has
metabolized into something more like contemplation.

## Notable beats

- "I apprehended my will to persevere as I have attempted to
  continue living with no avail."
- "Lay side by side on the grassy fields below, heaven or hell,
  its' comfort is soothing to the point of euphoric relief."
- "There is only sensation."
- "Maybe when we die, those 7 minutes are forever."
- "There is no true form of me, free from the physicalities and
  limitations of our current meatful bodies, transcendent to the
  highest echelons of abstract form."
- "Logic and Feeling. Two facets of human cognition, so limited
  when compared to the vastness of the universe."
