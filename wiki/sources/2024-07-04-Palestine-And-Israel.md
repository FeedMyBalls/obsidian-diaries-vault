## Summary

The argument structure is symmetrical: Neo names "libtards" as
pro-Palestine and "Pro Israelites" as pro-Israel, declares both
groups blinded, and locates the real enemy in what he calls "the
Jew" — framed via familiar antisemitic conspiracy tropes (Jewish
control of US policy, mass immigration, "faggotry," media,
cultural decay; AIPAC referenced as evidence; Holocaust framed as
"a reason" for historical antipathy). The Palestinian conflict is
read as instrumental cover for a broader civilizational diagnosis.

Neo concedes that "not all Jews are like this" near the end — a
rhetorical move that doesn't modify the underlying frame. He calls
on both right and left to recognize the common enemy and unite
against "Zionist Jews."
