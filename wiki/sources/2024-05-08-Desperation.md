# Desperation.

> AP-exam season. The [[Wizz]] era begins. Neo names May 2024 as
> "Desperation" and documents the breakdown of the
> [[Asexual-Atonement]] strategy from 2023. First sustained admission
> of pursuing online romance, masturbating to a chat partner's photos,
> abandoning the redirect-to-bots-only protocol. Foundational for the
> opening of the dating-apps arc that runs through 2025.

## Summary

May 2024 is named the month of Desperation. Neo opens by stating he
let "hormones control me again for the entire school year" — chased
[[D]] by switching classes, sacrificed academic preparation. AP Gov
just taken; he doesn't think he passed. AP Calc and AP CS upcoming.
The [[Vice-Principal-Incident]] computer-loss is still in effect —
no gaming setup back, no creative outlet.

The [[Wizz]] introduction: [[Edd]] recommended it. Neo names what
the app is — "main priority is to just get teenagers to date with
each other" — and uses it anyway, picking the 16-17 age range
because his birthday is approaching and he doesn't want to commit to
adulthood yet. He cites his parents calling him "immature" as
rationalization, then breaks frame: "Fuck them, fuck you. I hope
you guys die, but this diary's not about you."

[[Sen]] enters the corpus. Neo started talking to her, exchanged
contacts, lost interest because she texted too consistently — "The
moment a woman grows overly attached and talks to me everyday, I
immediately lose interest." The [[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]] pattern
running with the structure already named.

The most documentation-dense passage is the breakdown of the
[[Asexual-Atonement]] doctrine. Neo openly names that he has been
masturbating to [[Sen]]'s photos — explicitly naming this as something
he has not done before with anyone. "A few months ago, I never felt
the urge to 'do it', because despite feeling 'hopeless', I still
wanted to save it up for 'someone'." That principle is now gone.

The [[Punpun]] reading documented: 4-5 days, identifies with the
character. Notes that Punpun's eventual "terrible relationship with
Aiko" doesn't apply to him because Neo will "never acquire" love.
The [[Aesthetic-of-Melancholy]] mapped onto canonical sad-manga
identification.

The closing is direct and specific. [[Erotomania]] doesn't get named
here but the structure is identical. "I'm desperate for any light I
could find even if it hurts me in the long run."
