# Life so far.

> A month into college. Working night shifts at [[Mayo-Clinic]],
> attending [[PVCC]], trading options on Robinhood. The first sustained
> "alone but doing the work" entry — the structure that 2026 will
> later name and approve of. Here, Neo doesn't approve. The packed
> schedule has not delivered the relief it was supposed to. Money
> arrives; nothing else does.

## Summary

The entry is structured as a present-tense unburdening typed at school
during a club meeting break. Neo notes he had written
[[2024-07-22-First-Day-Of-Work]] but forgotten to upload it; he has
just done so. The reflection that follows is dense.

**Money.** Neo has begun [[Options-Trading]] on Robinhood. The week
prior he had a setup for SPY puts at strike 560 expiring Sept 5 —
the trade would have made him "almost 100 grand." He overslept the
market open. He didn't place it. He has $5.7k in his bank account
and treats this as nothing — "That shit can disappear in a matter
of seconds." A documented $400 of FAFSA book money has been
redirected into options. He's risking work paychecks. Loses $40 in
a day, "doesn't even care anymore." [[Trading-Addiction]] named
without that label.

**Romance.** [[Wizz]] reinstalled. Bumble Premium subscribed to and
refunded — "not a single match." Hinge X subscribed to — a few
matches but "none of them aren't great." Wizz Gold tried. Coworker
[[Eva]] (24, 7-year gap — declined). The [[Erotomania]] structure
running in production with no new emotional output. The closing
fantasy: "during work, I just imagine myself holding someone else's
slender fingers."

**[[L]] and [[D]]** both return as named yearnings. Neo addresses
[[L]] directly in second person — references [[Mr-G]], remembered
hallway sequence — and addresses [[D]] obliquely. The [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]]
output continues even with active dating-app contact, because that's
the architecture's invariant.

**Parents.** The 2023 [[Father-Wound]] in present tense. "my piece of
shit retarded 'dad' i hate calling him my dad by the way. I don't
call him that. I never do. I mentally dont. He's not my dad."
The compartmentalization Neo will later name as
[[Living-Contradiction]] doesn't yet have its label here — it
operates as raw refusal.

**Method.** A late paragraph names psychedelics as a possible cope —
Bitcoin to Monero, beginner-friendly substances first then "iffy
ones." [[Illicit-Acquisition]] from earlier 2024 has
expanded scope from suicide to numbing. Per Neo's standing decision,
specifics in raw.

The closing collapse is documented in real-time: typing degrades
("MIdersbrle. I'm fucking not .. ;asldf"), then resumes coherent in
the next paragraph: "you can tell i'm not in a good place. it's
getting really difficult to stay afloat on top of this whole thing."
And then the matter-of-fact sign-off: "Well. Club meeting in a few
minutes. game dev club. another day."
