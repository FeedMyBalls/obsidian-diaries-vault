# She Loves Me, She Loves Me Not.

> The longest 2026 entry (3,606 words). The retrospective on
> [[Anette]] — eleven months after the breakup. The corpus's
> deepest single-entry treatment of a relationship, written with
> [[Self-Mythologizing]] in third person ("the child") for the
> middle and dropping back into first person for the close.
> Foundational for the [[Anette]] entity page; canonical site
> for the [[Self-Mythologizing]] third-person pivot.

## Summary

The entry opens with a metaphysics — petal-plucking ("she loves
me / she loves me not") as the structure of Neo's mind itself.
*"I pluck more imaginary ones until the plucking lasts for
eternity, and in the end, there's nothing but petals inside my
head designed to appease my anxiety."* The flower-divination
folk image becomes the figure for the [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]]
running on no input.

Then the third-person pivot. Mid-entry, without warning, "I"
becomes "the child." *"This child dreams of many, completes few,
but designs it as if these 'few' are bigger than they are to
delude him."* The pivot is sustained for the entire middle
section. The [[Anette]] retrospective is delivered in this
third-person register — Neo describes himself from outside as
a writer would a character.

The relationship retold as story:

- The pre-relationship withdrawal, then the courage from her
  pursuit, then the [[Canyon]] discovery — *"this man was in the
  same class as him a semester ago"* — the realization Canyon was
  Anette's first kiss. The wound. The shift from love to
  "someone to 'just have fun with'."
- The first kiss in a parking lot — *"in a stupid parking lot,
  just because that detail from his ex made me jealous, i regret
  every second of it."* The [[Canyon]]-jealousy origin of the
  parking-lot kiss explicitly named for the first time.
- The escalation through touch ("kissing to the curling of her
  hair, to the touch of her waist, her clavicles, her arms, her
  thighs and her breast"), the boredom, the recognition that
  what he wanted was love after all.
- The [[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]] mechanism running visibly: the
  Gatsby-Daisy parallel ("edited videos made for her, like Gatsby
  that threw parties to attract his Daisy"), the spiraling
  paranoia, the certainty she was losing interest before she
  expressed it.
- The breakup line and Neo's misread: *"'I think we should Break
  up'…I could've denied it, I could've said, we can fix it. But
  no, I answered what she wanted, not aware that she included 'I
  think'."* The grammar of the *think* as the missed branch.

The closing third of the entry returns to first person and a
softer register. Self-isolation framed as recovery — going outside
without glasses to "skip most details in life," social-media
deletion, dreams of Anette "molding in the real world, seeping
its way to his peripherals."

The most important admission lives near the end, in first person:
*"I admit, I did actually love her."* The 2025 dismissals
([[2025-05-21-College-Romantic-Pursuits]]'s "didnt' feel.. good.
no spark") get revised. Per the [[Anette]] entity page protocol,
both reads are held in tension; this entry is the 2026 voice that
disagrees with 2025.

The entry's final movement is the [[Self-Mythologizing]] register
in full collapse — back to third person, addressed in second
person to the reader, with a punitive imaginative scene: *"Now
make fun of him, tell others of his putrid life… Spit at him,
beat him."* Then the closing line, which holds: *"Neo truly is
pathetic isn't he."*
