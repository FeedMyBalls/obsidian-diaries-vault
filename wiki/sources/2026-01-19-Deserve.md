# Deserve

> Late January, 2:46am. The first 2026 entry. Compact, structured,
> thesis-driven — *"do we deserve anything?"* The opening of the
> year's reflective register before the longer March entries.
> Foundational for the [[Undeservingness]] concept.

## Summary

The opening question is doctrinal: *"Are people entitled to anything,
do we deserve anything?"* Neo's answer is a thesis statement: from
childhood, things never went his way, so he "decided that I just
don't deserve happiness." The decision is framed as adaptive —
acceptance of insignificance — but with a documented side effect:
"when things go well for me, I self-sabotage and get destructive."
[[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]] tracked back to its trunk.

The entry walks the [[Undeservingness]] doctrine through three
domains in order: **objects** (parents had to force him to pick
gifts as a child), **people** (every conversation savored, then
poisoned by guilt watching them enjoy others more), **love** (every
relationship undone by inability to accept being chosen).

The [[Schopenhauer]] reference is tighter and more pointed than
elsewhere in the corpus — *"in Schopenhauer's philosophy towards
Happiness, he says that it lies in the effort one puts towards
something, the difficulty"* — but Neo turns it inside out: the
effort itself becomes evidence of deformity. *"the very effort I
exerted is just evidence of my inhumanity when it comes naturally
to others."* If [[Schopenhauer]] is right, Neo is double-disqualified.

The closing paragraph names the [[Self-Sabotage-In-Love]] mechanism
in operational detail: *"Every second without them is the anxious
thought that they'll realize how much of a fraud I am, an
unlovable Monster, so they can suddenly say, 'I don't love you
anymore'."* The pre-emptive demolition is described as the
defense.

- "I just don't deserve happiness."
- "when things go well for me, I self-sabotage and get destructive."
- "I prefer listening to others — what use is there to convey how I
  feel and who I am."
- On effort being inhumanity: "the very effort I exerted is just
  evidence of my inhumanity when it comes naturally to others."
- "Every relationship I get into is misery, because I could never
  accept the fact that someone else would 'Love' me."
- "I subconsciously ruin the relationship, act like a jerk, because
  I'm afraid to show how much I love them."
