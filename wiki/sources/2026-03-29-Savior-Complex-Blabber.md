# Savior Complex Blabber.

> 6:50am. The canonical [[Savior-Complex]] document. Stream-of-
> consciousness register (lowercase, run-ons, "blabber" in the
> title), but tighter argumentative content than any prior savior-
> complex mention. Closes with the [[Self-Mythologizing]] third-
> person pivot — final paragraph addressed in third person to
> "Neo." Foundational for [[Savior-Complex]], [[Neo-Mythology]],
> and significant for [[Long-Term-Plan]] (medicine framed here as
> savior-complex made operational — flag at the project page).

## Summary

The entry begins by reporting an observation Neo's
[[Intentional-Isolation]] surfaced: when not socially active, he
notices his pre-isolation pattern was "to help others. fix others."
Strangers from Wizz, Discord, anywhere — including girls who
might have become romantic partners — became friends he listened
to and "fixed." The pattern's persistence surprises Neo *because
he likes it.*

The argument develops in layers:

- **Atonement frame.** Listed alongside [[Asexual-Atonement]]: he
  did not take advantage of vulnerable strangers ("didn't take
  advantage of their loneliness to make them my girlfriends");
  they confessed to him, and he resisted. The 2023 doctrine
  upheld through the 2026 isolation.
- **Career frame.** *"Ambition fuels me, not for myself but for
  others, that's the purpose of medicine."* This is the line
  that makes [[Long-Term-Plan]] legible — medicine isn't a job
  Neo wants; it's the [[Savior-Complex]] made operational. *(Flag
  for the [[Long-Term-Plan]] project page when built.)*
- **Inferiority/superiority oscillation.** Named explicitly as
  the engine: *"a mix of superiority vs inferiority, i am a
  complicated individual."* Superior-to-parents in self-
  sufficiency since childhood (paying his bills + theirs); inferior-
  to-everyone-else, must compensate via service. Both at once.
  *"i am a show off after all."*
- **The Neo-name mythology.** *"It might also be because of my
  name: Neo. It's such a fantastical name, futuristic? who knows,
  but it's something beyond this world, and its made me think
  this way. pragmatic yet fantastical."* The name as
  [[Neo-Mythology]] — self-fulfilling prophecy via etymology.
  Childish-imaginations-as-fuel: *"yes ill help people, maybe
  even save the world etc."*
- **Charisma documentation.** Sophomore-year leadership of a
  group, instant Discord-server popularity, banning by mods
  but "doesn't care. at least he had fun." The introvert-with-
  charisma combination [[Living-Contradiction]] documents.

The closing paragraph drops into third person — *"Cold and calm
not as expressive, but can feel peoples emotions from a mile
away. thinking hes not emotional at all, a robot, yet he is
extremely. instead, focuses on the emotions of others than
himself."* The pivot is the same one from
[[2026-03-16-She-Loves-Me-She-Loves-Me-Not]]; here it's the
canonical [[Self-Mythologizing]] move of writing one's own
character profile in third person at the end of a first-person
entry.

The final line — *"This is Neo."* — signs the document like a
self-portrait. The entry isn't introspection toward fixing
something; it's introspection toward documenting something. Neo
reads as if drafting his own Wikipedia stub.
