# What's my Goal Now

> Senior year, fall break. Routine-and-status entry, low affect. First
> entry to mention the [[Suicidal-Drawing-Incident]] — a Ronnie
> McNutt drawing seen by his physics teacher 27 days earlier — which
> got Neo sent to the [[Social-Worker]].

## Summary

A low-stakes entry, almost a logistics note. Watching Beastars S2,
RealDebrid subscription, switching from Honors Physics to Arts 3-4
on [[Gavin]]'s recommendation. AP Gov annotation work. Calculus retake.
Senior year admin.

Buried mid-entry: the social worker referral. A drawing of Ronnie
McNutt blowing his head off, made 27 days prior, was finally seen by
staff. Neo treats this almost as paperwork: "they think i wanna kill
myself, which yes i definitely do and will do before i turn 20."

The asexuality strategy from [[2023-07-09-Women-Live-In-A-Mans-World]]
is reaffirmed in passing — Neo notes researching ways to chemically
remove libido, abandons due to depressive side effects. Procreation
described as "weird," sex "starting to disgust me." The
[[Asexual-Atonement]] posture is now stable identity, no longer pivot.

The closing hint about "obligated friends now" with [[Gavin]] (because
Gavin helped him switch classes) shows the [[Transactional-Friendship]]
read Neo applies to most relationships — friends as accumulated
obligations rather than chosen ties.
