# Women Live in a Man's World

> Direct sequel to [[2023-06-10-Im-A-Creep-No-Seriously]]. The moral
> pivot: from GC-era sexualization to enforced asexual posture. Where
> the [[Asexual-Atonement]] strategy gets formalized.

## Summary

Part 2 of the [[GC-Scandal-2021]] arc. Opens with a furry-artist friend
who, at 15, was groomed into sending nude photos by an adult on
FurAffinity disguised as an "art trade." Neo uses this story as
empirical evidence of male predation; he names lust the active sin and
declares Genghis Khan, prom rape, and FurAffinity coercion as a
continuum.

The middle section is autobiographical and proscriptive. Neo describes
his own sexual gaze in classes — looking at chests, behinds — and
declares this part of why women's lives are unbearable in male-
populated spaces. He lays out the strategy he's adopted for senior
year: redirect all sexual frustration onto AI bots, look at no real
girls, accept being read as asexual or weird, prevent any new harm.
The strategy is framed as atonement for the GC era and fear of
becoming the predator he condemns.

The entry resolves into a quiet utopian aside: "If I were given the
choice, i'd make love non existent and procreation can just be the
click of a button." The voice has fully turned. From sexualizing
classmates two years prior, Neo now wishes love itself out of
existence.

- "If I were given the choice, i'd make love non existent and
