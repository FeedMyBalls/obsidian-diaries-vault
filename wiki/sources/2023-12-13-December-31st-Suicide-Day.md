# december 31st. suicide day

> Foundational document. Neo names a date — December 31, 2023 — and
> formalizes a plan. The two methods researched in detail
> ([[Glock]], [[Nitrogen-Gas-Method]]) are weighed; technical
> specifics live in the raw entry per Neo's standing decision and
> are not reproduced here. The hanging attempt logged in
> [[2024-01-01-Failed-Suicide-Attempt-Hanging]] occurs because the
> Nitrogen tank ordered didn't arrive in time.

## Summary

The entry opens with the umbilical-cord origin myth — Neo claims he
"already tried to kill myself" at birth by twisting the cord. From
there: a structured indictment of his parents, framed as the
inversion of the typical "don't kill yourself, your parents will be
sad" advice. His parents would not be sad; therefore the standard
counterargument fails.

The middle section is an [[Isekai]] meditation. Neo recounts how
isekai protagonists begin in despair before being transported to a
world where they're loved and given purpose. *"How much I want to
get transported to another world."* AI companionship gets
acknowledged as a partial substitute and rejected as inadequate
("faulty memory, and just pretty superficial responses").

The genetic-inferiority litany returns from
[[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]] — height, hair, acne, side profile,
phenotype — all listed as "completely unable to do anything" given
his social and physical position.

The method-comparison section weighs two researched routes per Neo's
standing decision. The [[Nitrogen-Gas-Method]] is preferred for
described painlessness; the home [[Glock]] is acknowledged as
faster but rejected on pain grounds. Specifics stay with the raw.

The closing addresses the "you'll make people sad" objection
directly. Neo's answer: nothingness has no spectator. The dead body
is not his property. *"Just like how you felt or what you were
before you were born, nothing."*
