# buried old stories

> The longest entry in the corpus. New Year's Day composite — Neo
> stitches together fragments from August 4, September 18, November 25,
> and December 25 2024 with multiple January 1 2025 reflections. The
> year-end synthesis. Contains the [[Anette]]-meet narrative (the
> late-2024 "war" with [[Canyon]] as third party), the full Wizz era
> retrospective, the [[Vice-Principal-Incident]] aftermath, and the
> December 25 attempt anniversary. Foundational for almost every
> 2025 entity and concept page.

## Summary

The structure is a chronological mosaic. Neo opens January 1 with a
present-tense paycheck-and-budget paragraph (~$700 after taxes,
expenses won't cover, the standard register), then drops fragments
from across the prior year, then returns to January 1 several more
times with retrospective passes.

**August 4 2024 fragment** — Neo's "inherent value remains the same
if not less" frame. iPhone SE, jailbroken. A scammed Discord-account
purchase ("guy in pakistan"). [[ChatGPT]] used as study aid replacing
textbook reading. Memory-loss / autopilot symptoms documented:
"forget if i did something just a few seconds ago." Speeding 20-30
over the limit "almost if not everyday."

**September 18 2024 fragment** — The most articulate impostor-self
passage in the corpus. Neo describes watching himself succeed from
outside himself: "I feel like a spectator of my own life." Things
that should have crushed him are routine. He doesn't trust the
success. *"It feels like I haven't [earned this]. It feels like I'm
playing a character... waiting for someone to call me out and
finally take the good things away and strip it all down, and return
me to who I truly am. Worthless."*

**November 25 2024 fragment** — Brief rage. *"fuck all of you. i'm
sick of this. ive never gotten the opportunity to even love."*
Names [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]]'s Isolation-vs-Intimacy stage by name (without
attribution).

**December 25 2024 fragment** — The anniversary register. *"The
Sleepless Nights of 21, Confusion from 22, Insanity of 23,
Desperation by 24 and the incoming Agony of 25."* Names that night
as "the anniversary of last year's attempt" (cf.
[[2024-01-01-Failed-Suicide-Attempt-Hanging]]). Mentions a friend
whose advice — "go with the flow" — Neo says kept him going through
2024. The first explicit credit Neo gives anyone for having helped
him survive a year.

**January 1 2025 fragments (multiple)** — These hold the bulk of
the meta-content.

- *The Wizz arc.* Started [[Punpun]] in April 2024, got absorbed by
  [[Wizz]], met "several people" but talked to one girl
  ("[[Pre-Calc-Wizz-Girl]]" *(name unknown)*) day and night. The
  pattern of "scrolling for more 'friends' to talk to" while still
  texting her. The realization: she was doing the same thing he
  was. She confessed. He used "his greatest weapon" — the block
  button. Spam-call followup. He unblocked. Asked her to delete
  Wizz "for her own good." She didn't. He distanced.
- *The "I Reek of Envy" passage.* The most articulate
  [[Living-Contradiction]] passage about social development.
  *"They envy their past selves, while I envy a past that never
  happened... War Without Reason lacks conviction, so I have
  nothing to both live or die for."* The [[War-Without-Reason]]
  framing is original to this entry.
- *The risk-and-cope inventory.* Speeding, gambling, taking
  excessive workload as cope. "Erotomania is my weapon."
  Self-knowledge fully online.
- *Return-to-loser period after Wizz.* TikTok blackpill content,
  the "save Europe" movement (cf.
  [[2024-07-04-Palestine-And-Israel]]), nightcore/hyperborean,
  4chan r9k revisited. The post-Wizz return.
- *The late-2024 war.* Hingex and Bumble Premium tried, refunded.
  Wizz again, deleted. Then: *"I met someone late this year"* —
  this is [[Anette]]. *"things were going my way, like I could be
  an adult, get up with cause."* Then: *"someone tried getting in
  my way"* — this is [[Canyon]], Anette's prior attachment. Neo
  blocked Anette ("the reliable weapon"). She came back. He
  decided not to walk away. *"I fought back, and I struck as hard
  as I can and say Fuck You. Turn everything in my favor, make it
  a war, he spreads the message, and I delivered the final blow.
  You lost. I finally for the first time in my life, won."* The
  victory. Then immediate post-victory deflation: *"with my social
  inexperience I kept blowing it... I realized I'm late. Too late
  in all of this."* The relationship's emotional arc is already
  present in this single passage, before they've even started
  dating (Jan 6 is five days away).
- *The 2025 entry posture.* "I enter 2025 in agony, to wake up
  every single day with a sharp pain in my chest." Libido
  non-existent for two months. The closing: "I wake up a failure."
