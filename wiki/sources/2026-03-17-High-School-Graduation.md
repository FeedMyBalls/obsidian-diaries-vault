# High School Graduation

> Two-year retrospective of the May 2024 [[Sunnyslope-High-School]]
> graduation ceremony, written one day after the long
> [[2026-03-16-She-Loves-Me-She-Loves-Me-Not]] entry. The first
> appearance of [[Hannah]] (graduation) — distinct from [[L]] and
> from [[Hat-In-Time-Crush]]. Sustained narrative register; almost
> no doctrinal commentary; closest the corpus comes to memoir.

## Summary

Neo opens with a stated voice-shift: *"Let's be more literal this
time, less fluff, more concrete."* The [[Self-Mythologizing]]
register the previous day's entry deployed is consciously dropped
for narrative reportage.

The graduation memory:

- Anxiety pre-event: *"I didn't want others seeing me with my
  parents."* The [[Body-Dysmorphia]] frame extended to parents-
  as-extension — their height, his height, the visible heredity.
  Strategy: wedge into crowd, be inaccessible to parents, prevent
  any photographs that would prove the lineage.
- The autistic kid (unnamed) approached him; Neo notes the kid
  would later be at [[PVCC]].
- [[Hannah]] (graduation) enters here. Neo had sent her an
  Instagram follow request the night prior — captioned with a
  Filthy Frank "shoot me" clip overlaid with text *"1 day after
  graduation"* — implying his death post-ceremony. *"Classic dark
  humor."* Username "infinite08.." (Neo doesn't share the full
  handle). By coincidence she landed adjacent in the line-up.
  Neo registers her tense glances back-and-forth as recognition
  of the post. He could "feel that she was looking at me then
  looked away then again several times, although mixed with a
  feeling of unease." They never spoke. The [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]]
  with its most aggressive opener (suicide-joke as icebreaker).
- Inside the stadium: [[Cunningham]], [[Cummings]], and others
  ("brightened my day"). [[Dr-Lovell]] passing-encounter,
  presumed passive-aggressive given prior drama Neo declines to
  recount. The flat ceremony — speeches Neo doesn't remember,
  staring blankly, getting his diploma, walking. Cap-throw he
  "probably didn't" do.
- Post-ceremony: searching for parents, brief escape attempt,
  small smile at the crowd's lifelong-memory weight that he
  knew did not register for him.

The closing turn returns to register-commentary: *"Who knew that
two years later, the same kid is a complete workaholic balancing
god knows what."* Neo addresses his 2024 self in third person —
*"to him during this time, he was completely unaware just how
different it would be soon"* — and signs off the chapter
narratively: *"Goodbye High School, Sunnyslope High School… It
serves as a fuel for my future endeavors."*

The final beat: *"Another Chapter unfolded. College."* The tense
shift from past to present-future is the entry's purpose — bridging
the 2024 self into the 2026 present.
