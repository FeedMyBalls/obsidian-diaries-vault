# Art Club and New Classes

> Senior year. The class switch from Honors Physics to Arts 3-4 lands.
> First school day in the new schedule, first Art Club session. Quiet
> closing line about being watched: "Someone kept staring at me.. I
> won't say who though.. but it's her."

## Summary

A logistics-and-people entry. Neo drops Honors Physics (95% A) for
Arts 3-4 on [[Gavin]]'s recommendation. The counselor pushes back —
AP-track student, strong GPA, why drop a hard class — but Neo points
out that he's heading to community college, not a four-year, so the
transcript hit doesn't matter.

The new period structure: Arts 3-4 (Ms. L), Personal Development
online, AP Computer Science, then Art Club. Neo describes his new
classmates with characteristic granularity — the rude gay kid who
didn't thank him for picking up dropped tape, the kind girls who
shared paint, [[Edd]] needing to call parents, the mumbled-speech kid
he asked to draw a YouTube thumbnail.

The closing paragraph is the entry's quiet center. Neo notes someone
"kept staring at me" in Art Club, declines to name her, and addresses
her directly in the final lines: *"We haven't really talked since last
school year.. since you left. so, hey. i guess."* This is [[D]]
returning after months away — the same girl whose [[RTC]]
disappearance is documented in [[2023-11-25-Alone-On-A-Friday-Night-And-D]].
