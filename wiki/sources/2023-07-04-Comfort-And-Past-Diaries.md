# Comfort. and Past Diaries

> Composite entry: a brief lyrical opening about [[G]], followed by
> recovered fragments from earlier 2022 and 2023 diaries — annotated
> in present-tense by July-2023 Neo with sardonic asides. Meta
> document about reading his own past writing.

## Summary

Opens with a tender, almost protective fantasy of being held by an
unnamed "her" — soft, warm, idealized. Below it: a collected stack of
past diary entries, mostly addressed to [[G]] (a sophomore-year
girlfriend). The April 2023 entries — *I love you*, *Let's be happy
together* — read as confessional letters to her after the breakup,
pleading and hopeful.

The interlinear annotations are written in Neo's late-July-2023 voice,
mocking his earlier self: *"reading this diary i wrote back in
april.. heh.. yeah too fucking bad neo.. too fucking bad.. she never
said a single word."* The structure shows Neo's relationship to his
own emotional record — half witness, half tormentor.

The bottom of the file collects 2022-era YouTube and Roblox project
ideas, marked "cool diaries tbh." Neo's register for past creative
ambition is markedly gentler than his register for past romantic
hope.

## Notable beats

- The opening fantasy: "Their dainty little hands run through your
  hair, your skin. You wonder to yourself how long can such ecstasy
  last."
- The dream entry about [[G]]: "She showed me a character and I had
  to guess… my last guess was Kirby, and she laughed."
- The 2023-07-03 self-annotation: "I'm starting to think I really
  fucked up. (you definitely did)"
- Embedded fragments from 2022 (game ideas, video ideas) tagged
  "cool diaries tbh" — kinder voice toward past creative self than
  past romantic self.
