## Summary

A meta-entry. Neo notes he hasn't written in a while because "nothing
unique has occured recently." The not-writing is itself information —
he writes when the pressure builds; the silence between entries is
its own register.

The mood pattern documented: oscillation between feeling "like I'm on
top of the world" and active suicidal ideation. Body dysmorphia is
"only gotten inadequate." February rated as "pretty good compared
to other months" by relative standards — March is anticipated as
worse on pattern.

[[Valentines-Day-2024]] gets one line: the days leading up to it were
"hell." The compensation pattern: while peers were "making out," Neo
played [[ULTRAKILL]] and called it "way better :)." The smile
emoticon doing a lot of work.

The closing material is the texture worth preserving. The Iron-Side
[[ULTRAKILL]] gaming PC giveaway: 400k+ entries, Neo has 97. Two
days remaining. He calls this "the only things that give my life
worth" because the lottery long-tail represents the only place in
his current model of the world where mathematically improbable luck
could change his trajectory. The post-attempt January entries and
this late-February mood meet here: he's not asking for change he can
make, only for grace from outside.
