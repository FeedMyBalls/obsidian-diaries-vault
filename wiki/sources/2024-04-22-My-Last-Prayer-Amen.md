# my last prayer. Amen

> The [[Vice-Principal-Incident]] entry. Senior year, mid-April. Neo
> accessed a website that staff classified as a hacking attempt; was
> sent to the vice principal; begged not to have his parents called;
> was overruled. Computer privileges revoked at home, which is the
> material precipitator for the next several months. Method research
> moves to fentanyl via TOR / [[Illicit-Acquisition]]. Per
> Neo's standing decision, technical detail stays in the raw entry.

## Summary

Three weeks of intended AP-exam preparation collapse around a single
incident. Neo searched and accessed a website at school. Staff
classified the action as hacking. Vice principal one-on-one. His
plea not to call parents was rejected. The fallout: parental
"barade" (Neo's word), classed as a failure, computer taken away.

The losing of the computer is consequential beyond punishment. Across
2023 and the early 2024 entries, the computer is Neo's
[[Aesthetic-of-Melancholy]] outlet — video editing, gaming, AI
chatbots, his only documented productive engine. Removed, his time
collapses into TikTok, Twitter, 4chan, and physical-method research.
He explicitly traces this as causal.

The [[Social-Worker]] gets a second appearance. Same one as the 2023
referral. Yawning during the session. "It's as if she just wanted to
go home." The ineffectiveness is registered without rage, almost
clinically — the [[Living-Contradiction]] meets institutional
indifference and observes it.

Method research updates to fentanyl. Neo describes intent to use a
[[Bitcoin-To-Monero]] route on TOR to acquire it. Specifics in raw.
Prom is dismissed. Mirror-avoidance documented: "i haven't looked at
my reflection for months." Selfies "heavily filtered." Neo notes
he's begun perceiving the filtered image as the real one — a clean
documentation of an active dissociative dynamic he names without
flinching.

The closing prayer is the entry's center. Addressed to a God Neo
explicitly does not believe in: a single direct request for painless
death-in-sleep, ending "If you succeed, then thank you. Amen."

## Notable beats

- "I begged him not to call my parents. He didn't care."
- On the social worker: "The way she'd just yawn, it's as if she
  just wanted to go home."
- "Everytime i see my self in a mirror i look away."
- "I ended up molding my perception to think I'm the filtered image."
- The closing prayer: "God. I pray unto you, please make this my
  last night, and kill me in my sleep, i want it to be painless,
  make it quick while i dream of the best and comforting things.
  If you succeed, then thank you. Amen."
