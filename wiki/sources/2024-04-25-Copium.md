## Summary

The opening register is the post-incident depressive baseline. No
computer. iPhone SE running addictive apps. TikTok reinstalled
after 18 months ("last time I used it was back in August of 2022").
Instagram Reels primary. AP exam prep abandoned: "I've already
accepted I'm either getting a 3 or straight up failing." [[Emir]]
referenced as the only Discord-vc social tether (CS2 share-screen).

The middle of the entry is the most extended *normal-teenager
documentation* in 2024. A Monday hanging out with [[Alec]] and
[[Edd]], cutting [[ELA]] class with a chill teacher, walking and
talking, comments about his shirt making his chest protrude, a girl
staring at him during a Calc test. A Ping Pong Club session — Team
"Autism" — that started fun and got bad. [[Alec]] talked about
Elliot Rodgers; Neo reads him as a possible self-perceived incel.
A girl in the club Neo found "not as competent like us, so another
non competent on the fight would've been more fair."

The closing returns to the depressive register. Method research
continues — fentanyl, TOR, plug-sourcing. Per Neo's standing decision,
technical detail in raw only. The closing line collapses both
registers into one sentence: *"I already want to kill myself, I'm
just looking for the right plug."*

The two-register structure of the entry is the data. Active social
life with friends one paragraph; method-acquisition logistics the
next. Both true. The [[Living-Contradiction]] running in real time.
