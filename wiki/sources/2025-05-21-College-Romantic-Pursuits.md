# college romantic pursuits.

> First explicit naming of [[Anette]] in the corpus. First explicit
> mention of [[Joe]]. The entry where the [[D]] reactivation pivots
> the post-breakup arc. Short relative to its content density.
> 716 words.

## Summary

The opening thesis: Neo has lost the ability to have crushes since
entering college. In high school he had two ([[L]] and [[D]]) — both
"massive" — but the relationship with [[Anette]] (Jan 6 – April 12)
"didnt' feel.. good. no spark, nothing." Even at peak. The
[[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]] requires distance; reciprocity
defused it.

The middle of the entry names everything. *"I went through my first
semester of college with full time work, and I went part time during
my second semester. Had a relationship Jan 6th, she confessed, we
made out a lot, kissed a lot, but eventually we broke up April 12th.
Whatever. Got into drama before that with Joe. idk. i dont care."*
This is the only sentence in the corpus where the relationship is
described in past-tense logistical register — dates, count of
make-outs, end-date — and dismissed in one word. *Whatever.*

[[Joe]] enters here. The "drama before that" is the 34-year-old club
president's interest in Anette during the relationship. Neo
brackets it ("idk. i dont care") rather than developing it. The
bracketing itself is the data — Joe as fully named threat is
discharged in seven words.

The pivot is [[D]]. The two high school crushes ([[L]] and [[D]])
recently graduated; Neo notes "they're a year younger than me, and
a grade younger too." During the breakup phase, while watching the
graduation livestream, he saw [[D]] had reactivated her Instagram
account. He followed her. She followed back.

The closing register lifts dramatically. *"I feel happy again. I
feel like I have purpose. The same way I did in high school. I've
never felt this.. excited for something to happen for years."* The
[[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]] re-engages instantly. Anette is
displaced by D within the same paragraph she's named in. The
"college has just been boring romantically despite the make outs
and the kisses" line says quietly what the architecture requires:
distance to function.

The closing prayer: *"Just one message with her. and myabe. just
maybe. UGH"* — rhymes structurally with [[2024-04-22-My-Last-Prayer-Amen]],
addressed to a different deity but with the same petitionary form.
