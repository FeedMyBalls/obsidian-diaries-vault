# meaningless

> Senior year. The entry where Neo reports the realization that his
> mother is also indifferent — collapsing the last identified reason
> for living. Mentions the [[Glock]] in the home and contains explicit
> ideation. Risk-seeking behavior (walking with bag at night to
> attract robbers) documented.

## Summary

Opens with mood-swing description — manic-then-flat oscillation Neo
has named before. Quickly turns to the precipitating event: a panic
attack that nearly made him pass out, during which his mother
"demeaned" him rather than helped. Neo notes he had long given up on
his father, but had been clinging to the belief his mother still
"supported me even when I'm mentally struggling." That belief
collapses here.

> [!quote]
> "They were my meaning of life, the reasons I was living, and slowly
> bit by bit as I grew older, that meaning started to dissipate, and
> at this very moment, it ceases to exist."

Risk behavior: walking alone at night with bag visible to invite
robbery. Suicide ideation explicit, with method named — a Glock with
9mm bullets in the home, "90% fatal" framing. Neo writes the post-
attempt request inline: "I hope whoever finds me bleeding in the
floor finishes what I started." Per Neo's standing decision, technical
method detail stays in the raw entry; this page summarizes only.

The closing skipped section ("DIARY //random ramblings; skip") shifts
back into senior-year admin — failed Calc test studied hard for, the
"pretending to be more happy towards my arts teacher so she doesn't
have to worry," [[Edd]]'s repeated insistence Neo "speak to skye"
(declined). The compartmentalization between crisis register and
school-admin register inside one entry is itself the texture.
