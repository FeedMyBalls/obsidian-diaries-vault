# I'm A Creep. No Seriously

> Foundational document. End of junior year. Neo writes the longform
> account of the November 8, 2021 [[GC-Scandal-2021]] — the office call,
> the printed messages, the public exposure — and traces every later
> self-narrative back to it. Also names [[K]] and the rape attribution
> Neo carries.

## Summary

The entry begins with puberty and the social transition out of mixed-
gender friendship. By 6th–7th grade, Neo's group chat had drifted into
photoshopping classmates and teachers in sexual scenarios, jokes about
rape, sustained sexualized contempt. Outside the GC he was "innocent
short cute fella'." Inside it he was — in his own framing — "an
absolute monster."

November 8, 2021 broke the wall between the two. Called to the office
mid-day after a praised video project. Asked to define respect.
Confronted with the entire group chat's messages printed on paper. The
news spread. Affected girls and teachers were informed by staff. Neo
was seen for the first time as the [[Living-Contradiction]] he
already was internally.

The recovery arc gets one paragraph. The damage gets the rest. Neo
introduces [[K]]: a girl from his classes whose post-scandal sadness,
he believes, drove her toward "the wrong boyfriend," who got drunk at
prom and raped her at a restroom. Neo traces the chain back to the GC
— and through the GC, to himself.

The entry ends mid-arc; Part 2 ([[2023-07-09-Women-Live-In-A-Mans-World]])
delivers the moral pivot.

## Notable beats

- "Outside of the GC, I was seen as an innocent kid at school… But the
  GC was contrary to my real life personality. I was an absolute monster."
- On the office meeting: "What's your definition of Respect... Silence."
- "I felt the world crumbling piece by piece."
- On [[K]]: "She was raped because of me. Imagine sitting in her
  position, having to endure such a traumatic event."
- "I have stopped my thoughts of sexualizing other people, and believe
  that women deserve more rights than us filthy men."
