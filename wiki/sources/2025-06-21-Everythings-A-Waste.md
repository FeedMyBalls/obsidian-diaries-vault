# everythings a waste

> Mid-summer, two months post-[[Anette]] breakup, one month after
> the [[D]] reactivation. The shortest 2025 entry. Brief regression
> entry — the [[D]]-pivot energy from
> [[2025-05-21-College-Romantic-Pursuits]] has not held.

## Summary

A short return to the floor. The energy lift from the [[D]]
follow-back has dissipated; the message has presumably not been
sent or has not landed. Neo declares the "tragedy" framing of his
own life: "no happy journey nor a happy ending." The framing is
familiar from 2023 entries, but the register here is more
resigned than acute — closer to the [[Aesthetic-of-Melancholy]]
mode than to crisis.

The closing line — "Everyone else will have their happy ending,
but alas I'm here to suffer for as long as i live" — is brief
and old. The 2023 register would have built it into 800 words;
2025-Neo writes it once and stops. The brevity itself is data:
Neo can't sustain even the despair-essay register he had in 2023.
The architecture for sustained writing has thinned.
