# alone on a friday night. and D

> Composite entry. The first half is consumer-electronics rambling
> (keyboards, mice, drawing tablets) and detailed [[Body-Dysmorphia]]
> documentation. The second half — the part Neo himself marks
> "actual diary" — is the foundational [[D]] document and the most
> developed account of the [[Romantic-Obsession-Architecture]] in
> 2023.

## Summary

**First half (rambling).** Friday night alone, family out, watching
[[Frieren]]. Self-camera triggers a body-dysmorphia spiral — acne,
unibrow, nose, "inflamated cheeks or face fat." Pivot to peripherals:
the CK62 keyboard from 2019, Glorious Model O mouse, Akko v3 yellow
cream switches considered and abandoned, the One by Wacom Intuous
he can't justify. Mews has been doing for 19 months. References
canthal tilt assessment ("if I do, i'd list it as one of the
reasons for killing myself, but not yet").

**Second half (the D arc).** Neo logs his crush history with the
shy Hat-in-Time-pfp classmate ([[Hat-In-Time-Crush]] — Neo redacts
her name in the source; per Neo, distinct from both [[L]] and the
2026 [[Hannah]], though all three are crushes from different
periods) — initiated one conversation, registered her "looking
away" as her shyness, kept returning, "felt like a creep," gave up.
Identifies this experience as why he no longer approaches.

[[D]] enters as contrast. Native (likely Navajo per surname). Sat
behind him in class. Overheard her telling the teacher about an
[[RTC]] stay. High-pitched voice others disliked, Neo "almost liked."
She approached him — first time a girl had — about [[Beastars]]
recognition. They had a brief Lackadaisy / Ibis Paint X exchange.
Then she vanished from school for weeks ("dragged out by her
counselor"); returned senior year.

The senior-year present: D shares the [[Viking-Center]] with him.
They glance at each other constantly, never speak. The Art Club
visit (cf. [[2023-10-17-Art-Club-And-New-Classes]]) included her.
Neo has self-imposed a no-approach rule: *"I shouldn't bother D this
time, just leave her alone and give her peace. She probably went
hell in that RTC thing so just let her have a peaceful year."* He
even stops attending Art Club to reduce her exposure to him.

The closing line is the entry's whole engine: *"If she still feels
weirded out in any way. Then.. I'll just kill myself."*

  I feel like a cute short loli girl lol."

  made me feel like a creep and a weirdo."

  last year. Asking what program she uses and she would answer Ibis

  conversations with me."

  weirdo. I'm not interested in her romantically, and I just want
  to make people feel safe."
- The hostage clause: "If she still feels weirded out in any way.
  Then.. I'll just kill myself."
