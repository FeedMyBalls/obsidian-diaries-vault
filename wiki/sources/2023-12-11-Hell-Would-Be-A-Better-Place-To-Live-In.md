# HAHAHAHAHAHAHHAAH hell would be a better place to live in

> The single most all-caps entry in the corpus. Late-night manic-rage
> register. Two days before [[2023-12-13-December-31st-Suicide-Day]],
> which formalizes the plan this entry can only scream around. The
> [[GTA-VI-Trailer]] is named as the only thing that briefly held
> him back.

## Summary

Almost-uniform all-caps for the first three quarters. The entry has
no event-narrative — it is interior weather. Neo declares no one
loves him (parents, "she," "another a long time ago"), declares the
counselor session useless ("BAT SHIT"), and lists what he's tried
that month ("I TRIED SO MANY TIMES").

> [!quote]
> "MY MOTTO EVERYDAY IM NOT EVEN KIDDING IS LITERALLY 'I WANNA KILL
> PEOPLE' OR 'I WANNA KILL MYSELF'"

The GTA VI trailer (released early December 2023) is named as a thing
that "made me want to keep living" — the smallest possible thread.
Neo lists his attempted self-rescues (Instagram comeback, ULTRAKILL
edits, growth, locking in), and declares each one failed.

The final paragraph drops out of all-caps and shifts register
abruptly to something almost philosophical: *"if there ever was hell.
knowing what torment would be inflicted upon you—such as the act of
eternal suffering, instead of the false expectation of hope in a dark
and grim world that sells you lies and deception would be less
torturous. at least you know what you're in for."* The voice change
within one entry is the entry's most striking texture.
