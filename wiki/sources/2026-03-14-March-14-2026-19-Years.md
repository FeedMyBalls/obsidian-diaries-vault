# March 14 2026. 19 years ..

> Birthday entry. The first sustained "alone but successful" entry —
> the structural inverse of [[2024-09-04-Life-So-Far]]. Same packed
> schedule, same financial-trading-academic load, same Dave-and-
> family triangulation, but Neo evaluates it positively. Foundational
> for [[Workaholism-as-Identity]] in its mature form.

## Summary

The opener: *"I haven't used Tiktok or Instagram and even twitter
since feb 1. I've deleted them all, socially isolated."* The
[[Intentional-Isolation]] strategy named with date stamp.

The middle of the entry is the most exhaustive schedule
documentation in the corpus — Sunday church compulsion, weekly
shuttle routes between [[ASU-West]] and [[ASU-Downtown]] (10:10am
Anatomy lab, 11:45am quizzes, [[Babysitting]] in afternoons,
8:00pm-4:30am [[Mayo-Clinic]] anesthesia tech shifts), with sleep
in fragments. Anatomy lab "always do terribly" despite study.
Microbiology Tuesday-Thursday, "30 minutes of sleep" before lab.

The [[Dave]] portrait is fuller here than anywhere else: 23,
coworker, [[Enneagram-Type-9]] per Neo's typing, "lazy,"
"unpleasing to stare at." The taxonomic detail is striking — Neo
catalogs Dave's beard, posture, weight, balding, intellectual
range — and explicitly rates himself superior on most axes
("smarter, much more pragmatic. He's Dave, he's 23. I'm 19. I am
much more physically attractive"). The [[System-Gaming]] pattern
appears: Neo "takes the lead" despite being subordinate, "gives
orders," counts items against the supervisor's standard "engraved
in my head" months ago. Perfectionist register; corrective register
toward Dave's looser stocking; teaching-Dave register (enneagram
subtypes, cognitive functions) to make night shifts bearable.

The [[Long-Term-Plan]] gets present-tense execution status:
"job pays for my college plus scholarships so I barely had to pay
tuition this semester. It's nice, my savings is nice especially
for my age." The [[Long-Term-Plan]] running on schedule.

The third register turn is the [[Intentional-Isolation]] philosophy
itself. Neo names that no one has reached out except [[Lia]],
whom he might have ghosted because he wanted space. *"I like
being alone. Its made me realize, I don't want romance or friends,
I'm content with myself."* But two paragraphs later: *"I want to
stop talking to Dave and just do my job, I want to move out and
not babysit, I just want my own time and my own space."* The
isolation framed as both preference and increasing appetite.

Closing is the entry's title: *"Alone but successful."* The
weight is on the *but* — the relation Neo finds between the two
states. Where 2024-09-04 had keyboard-collapse, 2026-03-14 has
a thesis sentence.
