# anger ..

> The most uncompressed [[Mother-Wound]] entry in the corpus. Two
> sections — afternoon ("nothing is changing") and a "// update hours
> later" section after a fight where Neo punched walls. The second
> section contains explicit violent ideation toward his mother. Filed
> here as the foundational document for [[Mother-Wound]].

## Summary

The afternoon section is the [[2024-02-27-Recent]] mood continuing —
the grinding-loop register, "like there's no light at the end of the
tunnel," exhaustion at the alarm. Short. Sets the floor.

The "hours later" section breaks the floor. Neo punched walls and
his knuckles are red. His mother had asked whether he should "seek
therapy." He read this as a bid she had no right to make — "what
prompts that bitch to ask me these questions" — and the entry
escalates into sustained sexualized contemptuous slurs aimed at her,
and ideation about killing her ("stab you with the sharpest knife I
could find at fast intervals, until I could hear your screams of
horror dissipate").

The entry's pivot is the sentence that contains the boundary Neo
respects even mid-rage: "Youre lucky you have a child, so even I
can't shout at you to call you a stupid fucking bitch." The baby
brother is the speed limiter. The fantasy is unbounded; the action
is bounded by the existence of someone Neo loves in the same house.

The closing is the [[Aesthetic-of-Melancholy]] returning: "i wish
this world was all a bad dream, and I can't wait to wake up." The
register collapse from rage to resignation in one paragraph.
