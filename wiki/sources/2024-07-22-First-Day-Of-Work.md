# First day of Work

> 2:01 lunch break, in-the-moment phone entry. First day of [[EVS]]
> orientation — Environmental Services / terminal cleaning at
> [[Mayo-Clinic]] Campus Facilities. The first material
> intervention against the post-[[Vice-Principal-Incident]]
> doomscrolling collapse. Foundational for the [[Workaholism-as-Identity]]
> arc Neo will later name in 2026.

## Summary

Short, fragmented, present-tense — clearly typed on phone during a
work break. Mostly a list. The orientation crew is described:
elderly women 40s-60s, a 29-year-old "as short as me," nurses, doctors.
Neo registers social discomfort: "This is pretty uncomfortable. I
just want to work. I don't want to talk. But here I am."

The conceptual frame Neo brings is significant. He rejects money as
the goal — "I don't care about money. I care about being assigned
something to help for the greater good." This is the first
documentation of the [[Savior-Complex]] frame applied to labor.
Money is acknowledged as instrumental; purpose is the actual
desired output. Neo will recapitulate this in 2026 explicitly
(cf. [[2026-03-29-Savior-Complex-Blabber]]) as a stable adult-self
position.

The closing line — "I'll just wait for any orders and pretend this
is good" — registers the gap between the frame and the present
reality. The frame is deployed; the present orientation does not
provide anything to do; pretense fills the gap. The entire 2024-2026
work arc later runs on essentially this dynamic.
