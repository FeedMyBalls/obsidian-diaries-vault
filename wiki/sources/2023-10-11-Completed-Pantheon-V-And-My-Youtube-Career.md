# Completed Pantheon V.. and my Youtube Career

> Three months grinding [[Hollow-Knight]]'s hardest achievement
> finished. Brief euphoria, then: "felt like nothing right after."
> Career-aspirations entry — Neo plans the future of his
> [[Youtube-Channel]] in detail.

## Summary

October 11, 2023: Neo beats Absolute Radiance on Pantheon V — the
hardest achievement in [[Hollow-Knight]] — after a three-month grind.
He accidentally skipped the cutscene, which "completely ruined the
moment." The euphoria lasted minutes.

Most of the entry is forward planning. Neo wants the upcoming Pantheon
V video to break his channel — to hit 5k or 10k views, get to 1k subs.
He's wanted 1k subs since 2016. He cycles through references: Technoblade
intros, Videogamedunkey writing, Pyro's documentary style (myhouse.wad
mentioned specifically). He weighs whether to keep the channel
Hollow-Knight-exclusive or pivot to documentary.

The closing paragraph reveals the underlying stakes: this video is a
fork. If it works, "it'll definitely completely change the direction
of my life." If it fails, plan B is to abandon the channel and
"resort" to drawing or animation. Half a decade of failed YouTube
attempts (2016, 2017, 2018, 2020, 2021) precede this attempt; 2023 is
"my peak youtube sorta excitement."

The entry ends with an addressed prayer: *"wish me luck, and i hope
you do it future me.. make them proud. make me proud.. make us proud."*

## Notable beats

- "I felt a short moment of euphoria once I realized I just beat
  Absolute Radiance… Didn't last that long though, felt like nothing
  right after."
- "i actually expected to get a thousand back in 2016. it's been more
  than half a decade now and i haven't gotten to that point yet."
- The fork: "if it does succeed and does finally change my channel
  and get some or even a lot of views, it'll definitely completely
  change the direction of my life."
- "make them proud. make me proud.. make us proud."
