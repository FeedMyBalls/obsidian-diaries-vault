# Unbearable Pain

> Four days after [[2023-11-18-Meaningless]]. The deepest 2023 entry.
> Sustained existential register; explicit acceptance of [[Blackpill]]
> ideology; method research moves toward the [[Nitrogen-Gas-Method]]
> as an alternative to the home Glock. Identifies the plan to "erase
> my past completely, removal of my last name."

## Summary

Begins as continuation of *[[2023-11-18-Meaningless]]* — the
meaning-collapse from the mother realization. Neo declares
optimism unworkable in a world this hostile. The grief moves from
parents to genetics to dating market to full structural fatalism.
He writes the line that organizes the entry: "Bone structure
defines how people will think of you."

> [!quote]
> "I've been forced to only take that position and nothing else.
> When people ask me why I look so miserable all the time, that's why."

The [[Blackpill]] acceptance is named explicitly — not as ideology
worth debating but as a "cure for any foolish ideas." [[Halo-Effect]]
gets cited correctly. [[Looksmaxxing]] vocabulary appears throughout
(canthal tilt, manlet, mewing). Neo declares the dating market
permanently closed to him: ~90% of women are taller, his face fails
again, his personality is the final nail.

A future-erasure plan emerges — get a lawyer, change his last name,
"complete separation from the pieces of shits that biologically
formed me." Distinct from suicide planning; this is exit-by-legal-
nuking. Both options sit on the same shelf in the entry.

The method research updates: [[Glock]] is still present-in-home and
viable, but Neo prefers the [[Nitrogen-Gas-Method]] — researched as
"painless," "euphoric." Per Neo's standing decision, technical detail
remains in the raw entry only.

The Ryan-from-AP-Gov anecdote sits at the entry's middle: a partner
project where Neo decides his redhead partner was babysitting him
out of pity. The hypothesis is unfalsifiable from inside the
[[Living-Contradiction]] — every kindness reads as performance,
because Neo treats his own kindness as performance.
