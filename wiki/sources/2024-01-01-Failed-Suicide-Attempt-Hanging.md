# failed suicide attempt. hanging

> ordered didn't arrive in time, so he switched methods. Survival

## Summary

Short, post-event entry. The Nitrogen tank Neo had been waiting for
across [[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]] and
[[2023-12-13-December-31st-Suicide-Day]] failed to arrive before the
chosen date. Neo substituted hanging. Setup, attempt, then [[SI]]
(survival instinct, his own term) intervened and forced him down.

The closing posture is not relief. Neo notes he plans to keep the
knot accessible "just in case i might need it again." His framing
of 2024: "probably gonna be the worst one yet. I still am curious
how it's going to play out."
