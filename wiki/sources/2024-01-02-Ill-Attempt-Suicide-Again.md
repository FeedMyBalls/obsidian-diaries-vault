# i'll attempt suicide again

> One day after the failed hanging in [[2024-01-01-Failed-Suicide-Attempt-Hanging]].
> Neo posts an open call to readers to help him acquire a firearm.
> The most direct method-recruitment entry in the corpus. Per Neo's
> standing decision, technical specifics and method comparisons are
> summarized only — not reproduced — in this wiki page; raw entry is
> intact.

## Summary

The entry frames itself as a public request: anyone reading the diary
who can supply Neo with a firearm should DM him. Neo invokes the
[[Ronnie-McNutt]] livestream as model — both for method and for
audience. He writes: "I'll 'an hero'... let the masses watch, at
least my death won't go in vain."

Method-wise, the entry compares routes Neo had previously researched
in [[2023-11-22-Unbearable-Pain]] and
[[2023-12-13-December-31st-Suicide-Day]] — gas, hanging, firearm —
and concludes that firearm is fastest and most reliable. The
specific weapon-and-caliber discussion lives in the raw text only.
Logistical obstacles (age, permitting, background check) are
acknowledged as the rationale for the public call.

The entry's underlying register is desperation framed as efficiency.
Neo presents method choice as an engineering problem — minimize
suffering window, maximize reliability — while the engineering frame
itself is the symptom.
