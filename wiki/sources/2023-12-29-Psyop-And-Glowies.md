## Summary

A polemic entry, no autobiographical reflection. The target is a
specific TikTok creator: a young woman in military uniform with
Hearts-of-Iron-style references (Baltic war), affecting "voices in
the head" and "wanted for war crimes" memes — markers of online
"weird girl" persona. Neo has researched her and identifies her
LinkedIn as "Psychological Operations Specialist for the United
States Military," which he reads as confession.

His thesis: military recruitment is targeting the
[[Erotomania]]-prone demographic he himself sits inside. Lonely men
will see her as "the glimmer of hope in their terrible and lonely
lives" and sign up to die for it. The government knows. The higher-
ups don't care. *"theyll use it to choke on the remaining energy of
lonely suicidal terminally online losers."*

The entry's tell is the position Neo is writing from. He is the
target market he describes. He recognizes himself among the
"vulnerable men" being exploited. He's writing this two days before
his own scheduled suicide date. The polemic is both political
analysis and self-defense — naming the trap so it can't catch him.
